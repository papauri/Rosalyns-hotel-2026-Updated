Reports and End of Day

How to read the financial reports, close the day with the End of Day report, and reconcile each cashier's till at Rosalyns Beach Hotel.

Who uses it
Managers, accounts staff, and whoever closes the day
Where to find it
Admin menu → Money → Reports (/admin/reports.php) and End of Day (/admin/end-of-day-report.php). POS Accounting (/admin/pos-accounting.php) is under Advanced. The Z-report and Drift report open from buttons on POS Accounting.
Permission needed
Reports and End of Day: Reports. POS Accounting, Z-report and Drift report: POS Accounting
Only shown when
Finance & Accounting is switched on in Settings → Modules. POS Accounting also needs POS & Stations.

Reports: the tabs

The page is called Reports & Analytics. It opens on this month. Tabs only appear for the parts of the business that are switched on.

How to change the dates and export

  1. Set Start Date and End Date and click Apply. If the dates are the wrong way round, they are swapped for you.
  2. Or click a quick button: Today, This Week (from Sunday), This Month, This Quarter, This Year, All Time (the last five years).
  3. Click Export CSV to download the data of the tab you are on for those dates. The file is named like hotel-report-revenue-2026-10-07.csv.

Each tab's Export CSV exports that tab's own data, including P&L Overview, Revenue, VAT Register (the Export for Filing button), Bookings, Occupancy, Guests, Conference, the till (F&B), Stock, Staff and Voids tabs. On the Aging & AR tab the file holds the age buckets (Age (days), Count, Amount, with a Total) followed by the detail rows (Reference, Client, Type, Method, Status, Date, Days outstanding, Amount).

You need Reports or Accounting permission to export.

The deferred revenue notice

On P&L Overview you may see a Deferred Revenue Notice and a line Deferred Revenue (unearned). It adds up money received for room bookings whose check-in is after today and whose status is Confirmed or Tentative, minus refunds on them. It tells you how much of the money collected belongs to stays that have not happened yet, so you do not count it as earned. Click the link in the notice to open the Bookings tab.

End of Day report

Open Money → End of Day. It shows one day. The label says Live (today) or Archived day. Change the day with the date box, the arrows, Refresh or Today. You cannot pick a future day.

How to email, WhatsApp, print or download the End of Day report

  1. Choose the day you want.
  2. Optional: type extra addresses in CC email (optional), separated by commas. It starts with the CC list set in Hotel Settings (End-of-day report CC).
  3. Click Email. The report goes to the hotel's report recipients, or to the hotel admin email if none is set. You will see "Report Sent" with "Sent to N recipient(s): ...".
  4. Click WhatsApp to send a summary to the hotel's WhatsApp number from WhatsApp Settings.
  5. Click Print to print the page, PDF to download a PDF, or CSV to download the day's figures.

If sending fails you will see "Could Not Send" and the reason, for example "No admin recipient configured. Add an email to site setting "eod_report_recipients" or "email_admin_email"." or "No WhatsApp recipient number configured...".

POS Accounting: closing cashier shifts

Use this to check each cashier's till against the system and close their shift. Open Advanced → POS Accounting. Pick the Business date and click Apply.

The top shows Paid POS sales, Cash collected, Mobile / card and Voids for the day. Below that, Cashier close matching lists every cashier who took sales that day.

  1. Tick the cashier (or use the select-all box). Cashiers already closed show Closed and cannot be ticked. A cashier with unfinished orders shows how many are open.
  2. Expand the row. Count the drawer and enter Declared cash, Declared mobile and Declared card. Enter 0 if there is none. Declared cash is the whole drawer, including the opening float. Each cashier row shows Opening float: ... (or none recorded, counted as 0). The expected amounts are hidden until you close, so the count is blind.
  3. Optional: open the list of sales to review them. Tick each as checked, or click Tick all. This does not close anything. Add an Accountant note to any sale, and a count note for the cashier.
  4. Optional: type a Bulk note for selected closes.
  5. Click Close selected.
  6. You will see "N POS shift(s) closed. Variance (counted - expected) - name: cash 0.00, mobile 0.00, card 0.00." A negative number means less was counted than expected.

Expected cash is the opening float plus cash sales minus cash refunds. Example: float MWK 20,000 plus net cash sales MWK 230,000 means the system expects MWK 250,000 cash and you count MWK 248,000. The variance is -2,000.00. Write the reason in the note.

Further down are the POS sales log and the POS audit logs for the day.

Z-report (shift close report)

Click Z-report on POS Accounting to see all the shift closes for the chosen date. Managers and admins only; anyone else gets "Access denied." It shows total revenue, orders, voids and net variance. A red banner warns of a total shortfall across all cashiers to investigate before sign-off. The table lists each cashier with their cash, mobile and card variance, an OVERRIDE tag where the close was approved despite a difference, and a View link.

The single report for one close shows the cashier, date and time, record number, then Tender Reconciliation with a Cash: line showing Float + Cash sales − Cash refunds = Expected cash (closes made before the float was included say the float was not part of their expected cash) (System (Expected), Counted (Declared), Variance, and BALANCED, SHORTFALL or OVERAGE for each of cash, mobile and card), notes, top items, the paid orders, refund and void payouts, and voided orders. Click Print / Save PDF to keep a copy. A cashier who is not a manager can open only their own closes. With no date given, the page shows the last 30 closes.

Drift report

Click Drift report on POS Accounting. It is read-only and changes nothing. It measures how far older till figures were out because of two faults that have since been fixed: tips counted inside revenue and VAT, and split payments. It looks at the last 90 days; change Start and End and click Apply, or click All time. A cash difference that is only the opening float is not shown as drift.

  • Tip/VAT overstatement register: each affected sale with the recorded total, the correct total, and how much revenue and VAT were overstated. Refunded sales are marked.
  • Historical shift-close accuracy: closed shifts whose expected figures would be different today, and whether a manager override was used at the time.

If the lists are empty you will see a green line saying no drift was found. Whether to correct any past figure is a decision for finance or the owner.

Rules the system enforces

  • A cashier can be closed only once for a business date.
  • All three declared amounts must be entered, and none can be negative.
  • A cashier with no paid orders is skipped when you close.
  • The End of Day report cannot be run for a future date.
  • Reports show only the sections for modules that are switched on.
