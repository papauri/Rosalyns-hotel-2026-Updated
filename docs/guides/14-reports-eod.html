Reports & End of Day

Every metric the hotel needs — nightly EOD review, Z-reports for cashiers, POS reconciliation, and the full analytics suite covering revenue, occupancy, guests, and stock.

Audience Manager & Admin Read time ~10 min Pages end-of-day-report · reports · pos-accounting · shift-close-report · accounting-dashboard

On this page

  • End-of-Day report

Reports follow your business preset. Tabs, KPI panels and the End-of-Day content — including the PDF, email and WhatsApp exports — include only the modules your business runs. A hotel sees Occupancy and ADR in the headline strip; a till business (shop, supermarket, bar, gym) sees Orders Today and Average Order Value instead. Room, conference, gym and event revenue lines each appear only when that module is on; a Full Hotel preset shows everything. See the Business Presets guide.

1. End-of-Day Report

/admin/end-of-day-report.php — the owner/manager's nightly snapshot. Review it every evening before closing. Shows a full day's operations, revenue, POS performance, compliance items, and a preview of tomorrow.

Selecting a date

The report defaults to today. Use the date picker to pull any past date. Day-over-day comparison deltas are shown automatically alongside most figures.

Sections at a glance

Section What it covers
Room Operations Expected vs actual check-ins and check-outs, stayovers, new bookings, cancellations, no-shows, occupancy % with day-over-day delta.
Revenue breakdown Room, conference, and F&B revenue split with VAT; refunds; pending amounts; transaction count; gross and net totals; ADR; RevPAR.
Payment method mix Cash, mobile money, card, bank transfer — counts and amounts. Non-cash total shown separately.
POS / Restaurant Orders by type (walk-in, room service, tab), gross margin, average order value, voids, and top 8 selling items for the day.
Reviews today New reviews submitted and average star rating.
Housekeeping snapshot Pending, in-progress, and completed tasks.
Outstanding folio Total still owed by in-house and confirmed guests.
Credit notes CNs issued today and CN redemptions applied today.
Dynamic pricing Bookings using a rate plan, discount given, package revenue, top rate plan and package for the day.
Tomorrow preview Expected arrivals, departures, and forecast revenue from confirmed bookings.
Day-over-day comparison Previous day vs today: gross, net, refunds, room revenue, POS, bookings, occupancy — each with a colour-coded trend arrow.

Key metrics explained

Metric Formula
ADR Room gross revenue ÷ rooms occupied
RevPAR Room gross revenue ÷ total rooms
Room sell-through Rooms occupied ÷ total rooms × 100
Empty room opportunity Unsold rooms × ADR (revenue left on the table)
F&B per occupied room POS gross ÷ rooms occupied
Payment capture rate Gross collected ÷ (gross + pending) × 100
Unpaid risk Pending payments + outstanding folio
Evening routine: open EOD, check occupancy vs target, review outstanding folio, check tomorrow's arrivals, flag any voids that need a manager review, then close.

2. Comprehensive Reports & Analytics

/admin/reports.php — 13-tab analytics suite. Set a date range at the top then switch tabs. Most tabs support CSV export.

Date range

Defaults to the current calendar month. Adjust Start date and End date and click Apply.

Tabs

Tab What you'll find
Overview Payment status breakdown, revenue by booking type, outstanding payments list with days overdue, payment capture rates.
Revenue Payment method breakdown, daily and monthly revenue trends, top 10 clients by spend, VAT collected per day.
VAT Daily VAT collected table — date, transaction count, VAT amount, total revenue.
Aging Receivables aging: 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, and 90+ day buckets for outstanding (pending/partial, non-refund) invoices. Age is measured from the invoice creation date — giving a true picture of how long a balance has remained unpaid regardless of any payment date activity.
Bookings Booking status breakdown, room-level stats (count, nights, revenue), cancellation rate, recent 15 bookings.
Occupancy Occupancy % by room type, ADR, RevPAR, no-show rate, upcoming booking forecast.
Guests Top 15 guest countries, repeat guests (more than 1 booking in period).
Conference Conference room stats, conference revenue, average event size.
F&B F&B / POS revenue split by order type, void summary.
Stock Stock-level data and usage for the period.
Staff Staff activity summary for the period.
Voids & Refunds Refund reason breakdown, refund status breakdown, daily refund trends.
Credit Notes CN list for the period, KPI summary (issued, redeemed, outstanding, voided, expired), type breakdown, redemption history.

Key occupancy metrics computed

Metric Formula
ADR Total room revenue ÷ total nights sold
RevPAR Total room revenue ÷ (total room inventory × days in period)
Occupancy rate Total nights booked ÷ total room nights available × 100
Cancellation rate Cancelled bookings ÷ total bookings × 100
Avg stay length Total nights ÷ booking count

3. Accounting Dashboard

/admin/accounting-dashboard.php — the accountant's command centre. Select a date range (defaults to current calendar month). Use Show All to remove date limits.

Financial summary panel

Metric Definition
Gross collected Sum of completed/paid payments (refunds excluded). Revenue received in the period.
Net excl. VAT Gross collected minus the VAT component — pre-tax revenue.
VAT collected VAT on all completed/paid sales, net of VAT returned on processed refunds. This is the net VAT figure for tax returns.
Pending Amounts on unpaid/partial invoices. Only guest receivables — pending refunds (liability) are tracked separately.
Refunds issued Total on refund-type rows. Identified by payment_type = 'refund' for consistency — does not double-count rows that also carry a refunded status.
Pending refunds Refunds authorised but not yet paid out. Represents a liability to guests.

Revenue by source

Three panels — Rooms, Conference, Restaurant & POS — each showing booking count, gross collected, VAT component, and outstanding balance on bookings that had payments in the period.

POS analytics

  • Revenue by order type — walk-in, room service, tab: orders, gross, COGS, voids.
  • Gross margin — gross revenue minus COGS from stock_orders.total_cost. If COGS is zero for any type, the margin will be inflated — this usually means ingredient costs have not been entered on those recipes.
  • Inventory shrinkage — the value of stock lost to wastage, stock-count variance, expiry and recalls for the period. This is a real hit to margin that COGS does not capture, so the panel also shows the net F&B contribution (margin minus shrinkage) with a breakdown by loss type.
  • Folio F&B (room service / minibar) — food & drink billed to room folios. Because it is collected under the room booking, it is included inside Room revenue above; this memo line shows it separately for F&B-department visibility and is not added on top (so nothing double-counts).
  • Daily revenue trend — last 14 days: room, conference, F&B, and refunds per day as a visual breakdown.
Conference outstanding balances now use the same VAT-inclusive (gross) basis as rooms, gym and events — a fix that previously understated what conference clients still owed when VAT is charged on top.

Compliance checks — what to do

Alert What it means Action
Missing receipts Completed payments with no receipt number. receipts.php → Generate Missing.
Invoices without numbers invoice_generated = 1 but no invoice number stored. invoices.php → Regenerate Invoice per row.
POS orders without ledger entry Paid stock orders not matched to a payments row. Run pos-accounting.php for the affected date and close the shift.

Quotation pipeline

Count and value of quotations in each status (Sent / Accepted / Expired / Declined) for the selected period. Use this to track conversion of proposals into confirmed revenue.

Credit notes summary

Issued count and value, total redeemed, and outstanding balance (remaining on active and partially-applied CNs). Outstanding CN balance is a future discount liability — monitor it monthly.

Outstanding summary

The Current Outstanding panel shows all-time unpaid balances on room and conference bookings (amount_due > 0). This is a current-state snapshot, not filtered by the selected date range — it shows what the hotel is owed right now regardless of when the booking was made.

4. POS Accounting & Shift Close

/admin/pos-accounting.php — the accountant's daily POS reconciliation screen. Use this after every trading session to verify cashier counts and formally close shifts.

Permission required: pos_accounting. Only users with this role can access this page.

Page overview

  • Select the business date at the top (defaults to today).
  • KPI strip shows: Paid POS sales, Cash expected, Mobile/Card totals, Void count and value.
  • The Cashier Close Matching table lists every cashier who placed orders that day.

Cashier table columns

Column Meaning
Cashier Staff full name.
Orders Number of paid orders for the day.
Expected System-calculated cash / mobile / card totals from stock_orders.
Declared Editable inputs — enter the actual counted cash, mobile receipts, and card slip total.
Variance Declared − Expected. Computed in real time. Green = OK, Amber = small difference, Red = shortfall.
Status Open / Has open tabs / Closed.
Notes Text box for the accountant to record an explanation for any variance.
Expand (▶) Opens a drawer showing the full order list for that cashier; each order has an accountant note field.

Closing a shift

  1. Enter the Declared amounts for each cashier (count the cash drawer and check mobile money receipts).
  2. Add a Note if there is a variance that needs explanation.
  3. Tick the checkbox next to each cashier you want to close (or use Select All).
  4. Optionally type a bulk note that applies to all closes in this batch.
  5. Click Close Selected.
  6. Each close is written to stock_shift_closes with variances recorded. An audit event is also logged to stock_order_audit.

Understanding variance colours

Colour Variance range What to do
Green Declared = Expected (or within rounding) Good — proceed to close.
Amber Small positive or negative difference Add a note explaining the difference before closing.
Red Significant shortfall (declared < expected) Investigate before closing. Ask cashier to recount.

Variance = Declared − Expected. A positive number means the cashier has more than the system expects (overage). A negative number means a shortfall.

Settled from tabs

The Settled from tabs count and amount on each cashier row shows orders that were created before the current business date but paid during it — i.e., orders that sat as open tabs overnight and were settled that day. This is informational; the amounts are already included in the Expected totals.

Open tabs warning: if a cashier has orders still in placed status, the Status column shows a warning. Resolve those open tabs in the POS before closing the shift.
Already closed: closed shift rows are greyed out and their checkboxes are disabled. You cannot re-close the same cashier for the same day.

Viewing the Z-Report

Click the Z-Report button in the page header to open the date-summary Z-report for the current business date (shift-close-report.php?date=…).

5. Z-Report — Shift Close Report

/admin/shift-close-report.php — a printable close-of-shift summary. Three modes:

Mode URL Who can view
Single Z-report ?id=N FOH staff: own closes only. Manager/Admin: any.
Date summary ?date=YYYY-MM-DD Manager & Admin only.
List (no params) Admin/Manager: last 30 closes. FOH: own last 30.

Single Z-report sections

  • Header bar — cashier name, shift date, closed-at time, record ID. Red Shortfall banner if total variance is negative. Amber Override banner if a manager forced the close.
  • Summary strip — Total revenue · Orders · Voids (count + value) · Settled from tabs · Tips collected (shown when tips > 0).
  • Variance table — Cash, Mobile money, Card: Expected vs Declared vs Variance, each coloured. Expected amounts include any tips collected via that method — cashiers must declare their full collected total including tips.
  • Notes — cashier or accountant notes.
  • Paid orders table — every order paid during the shift window.
  • Voided orders table — voided orders with reasons.
  • Top selling items — top 10 items by revenue for the shift.
Tips and cash reconciliation: when a guest leaves a cash tip, the cashier keeps it in the drawer. The system adds expected tips to the Expected Cash total on the variance table, so the declared cash count must include those tips. Mobile money and card tips are reflected in the respective Expected columns the same way.

Date summary

Shows every close record for the day side-by-side, plus aggregated totals — total revenue, orders, voids, and per-method variances — so a manager can see the whole day at a glance.

Print tip: use Ctrl + P (or browser print) on a Z-report page — the admin header and sidebar are hidden automatically for a clean printout.
