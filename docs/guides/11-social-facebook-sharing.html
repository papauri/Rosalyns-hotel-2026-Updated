Facebook and WhatsApp

How to connect the Rosalyns Beach Hotel Facebook Page so staff can post rooms, events, conference rooms, gym packages and menu items from the admin, and how to set up WhatsApp notifications.

Who uses it
An administrator sets it up. Staff with access to rooms, events, conference, gym or menu then post.
Where to find it
Admin menu → Website → Facebook Settings (/admin/facebook-settings.php) and WhatsApp Settings (/admin/whatsapp-settings.php)
Permission needed
Facebook Settings and WhatsApp Settings. To post: the permission for the page you post from (Rooms, Events, Conference, Gym or Menu).
Only shown when
The pages that carry the share buttons follow their own modules: Rooms and Conference Rooms need Bookings and Conference switched on; Events needs the Events page; Gym needs Gym. The two settings pages need Website & CMS switched on in Settings → Modules.

How to connect the Facebook Page

You do this once. You need a Facebook app (made at developers.facebook.com) and a Facebook login that has full control of the hotel Page.

  1. Open Website → Facebook Settings.
  2. In the blue box Add this address to your Facebook app, copy the address shown.
  3. In a new tab open developers.facebook.com → your app → Facebook Login → Settings. Paste the address into Valid OAuth Redirect URIs exactly as shown. Keep Client OAuth Login and Web OAuth Login switched on. Save in Facebook.
  4. Still in Facebook, open Settings → Basic and copy the App ID and App Secret.
  5. Back in Facebook Settings, paste them into App ID and App Secret. Leave Login configuration ID empty unless your app is a "Facebook Login for Business" app.
  6. Click Save and connect with Facebook. Facebook opens.
  7. Log in and approve the three permissions it asks for: pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and pages_manage_posts. Choose your hotel Page when Facebook asks (or "Opt in to all current and future Pages").
  8. You return to Facebook Settings. If you have more than one Page you will see Choose the Page to post to. Click your hotel Page.
  9. The Connection card now says Connected as and your Page name.
  10. Tick Enable Facebook Page Posting, set Default Hashtags (each starts with # and they are separated by spaces, for example #hotel #malawi), and tick the areas where you want share buttons.
  11. Click Save Facebook Settings. You will see "Facebook settings saved successfully."

The App Secret is saved scrambled and never shown again. To keep it, leave the box blank. If posting is switched off, every Post to Facebook button disappears from the admin. If posting is on but the Page is not connected, the buttons stay on screen but are greyed out, with a hint: "Facebook is not connected yet. Open Facebook Settings and click Connect with Facebook." Nothing can be posted until you connect.

How to check the connection

  1. Open Website → Facebook Settings.
  2. On the Connection card click Test connection.
  3. Read the result. A green line means all is well. A warning tells you what is missing.

Test connection only reads from Facebook. It never posts anything.

Other buttons on the card:

  • Reconnect: signs in again with Facebook and replaces the saved token. Use it when the token has expired or a permission is missing.
  • Disconnect: removes the saved token after you confirm. Share buttons stay greyed out until you connect again. Your App ID and App Secret are kept.

If you really want to see a post go out, open Advanced: paste a token by hand and click Publish test post.

Publish test post puts a real post on your Page. Delete it on Facebook afterwards if you do not want it to stay.

How to post one item to Facebook

The button appears on these pages when posting is enabled, a token is saved, and the matching share tick is on:

  • Rooms: Settings → Rooms, the Facebook button on each room card (tooltip Preview & post this room to your Facebook Page).
  • Events: Conference & Events → Events, the Facebook button on each event.
  • Conference: Conference & Events → Conference, the Facebook button on each room card.
  • Gym packages: Gym → Packages (Share on Facebook).
  • Menu items: the Feature button on each item in the menu page.
  1. Click the Facebook button. A window opens: Compose post on the left, a Post preview on the right.
  2. Read the caption. It is written for you from the item's details and your hashtags. Edit it as you like. A character count sits under it.
  3. Tick or untick Include room image in post (the wording changes for events, conferences and menu items).
  4. Click Post to Facebook Page.
  5. You will see "Posted to Facebook!" with a link to the post. If the caption is empty you will see "Please enter a caption."

The share buttons on Rooms, Conference, Events, Gym and the menu page keep working when you move between admin pages by clicking the menu, without reloading the browser. If a share window ever does not open, press F5 to reload the page and try again.

How to post several items together

  1. On the Rooms page click Compose & Preview Post in the bar Share All Rooms on Facebook. Conference has the same bar for conference rooms. Gym has Share All Packages. The menu page has Share Full Menu.
  2. Tick the items to include. Use the select-all and clear buttons if you want.
  3. Edit the caption and the image option, check the preview, then click the post button.

How to set up WhatsApp

The WhatsApp Settings page sends booking messages through Meta's WhatsApp Business service. The same hotel number is used for the End of Day WhatsApp summary.

  1. Open Settings → WhatsApp Settings.
  2. In API Configuration fill in Hotel WhatsApp Number (with country code, like +265888123456), WhatsApp Phone Number ID, WhatsApp Business Account ID (both numbers from Meta) and Permanent Access Token. The token is saved encrypted and is never shown again: the box is empty after saving and its hint reads "Saved — leave blank to keep the current token". Leave it blank to keep the saved token; type a new one only to replace it.
  3. Under Notification Triggers tick the events to message about: New Booking Created, Booking Confirmed, Booking Cancelled, Guest Check-in, Guest Check-out.
  4. Under Notification Recipients choose Send to Guest (uses the phone number on the booking) and Send to Hotel/Admin. Enter the staff numbers in Admin WhatsApp Numbers, separated by commas.
  5. Under Message Template Names type the names of your approved Meta templates for Booking Confirmed Template and Booking Cancelled Template. They must match Meta exactly. Letters, digits and underscores only. Blank gives booking_confirmed and booking_cancelled.
  6. Tick Enable WhatsApp Notifications and click Save Settings. You will see "WhatsApp settings saved successfully!"
  7. To check it works, enter a number under Test WhatsApp Integration and click Send Test Message. Confirm the prompt. The result shows as Test Result at the top.

A test sends a live message and may be charged by Meta or WhatsApp.

Rules the system enforces

  • Posting needs the right permission for the thing you post: Rooms, Events, Conference, Gym or Menu.
  • You can post only active rooms and events.
  • Each area can be turned off with its share tick on Facebook Settings. The gym packages tick is Show share button on Gym packages.
  • WhatsApp cannot be enabled without the access token and phone number ID.
  • Restaurant receipts (till, table settle and receipt page): when WhatsApp is enabled here they are sent automatically. When it is not, WhatsApp opens on the staff device with the receipt typed in (an Open WhatsApp link) and staff press Send.
