Modules and business presets

This screen switches whole areas of the system on or off for Rosalyns Beach Hotel. A preset sets many switches at once for a type of business. Nothing is ever deleted: switching an area off only hides it, and switching it on again brings it back with all its data.

Who uses it
Owner or administrator only. This is a set-up screen, not a daily one.
Where to find it
Admin menu → Settings → Modules (/admin/module-settings.php). The page title is Module Settings.

What is on the screen

From top to bottom:

  • A short note: "Disabled modules are hidden from the sidebar and dashboard — no data is deleted. Finance & Accounting is always on and cannot be disabled."
  • Quick Setup — Business Presets: one button per business type. The button that matches the current settings shows a green Active badge. If your settings do not match any preset exactly, no button has the badge.
  • A yellow note: "Changes take effect immediately. The navigation sidebar updates on your next page load."
  • Individual Modules: one card per module, each with an on/off switch and a status line (Enabled or Disabled).
  • Guest Website Pages: two cards, Events Page and Restaurant Page.

How to apply a preset

  1. Open Settings → Modules.
  2. Click the preset button you want. The button greys out for a moment while the system checks the effect.
  3. A window opens titled Apply "<preset name>" preset?. Read it:
    • The first line says what the preset "Turns ON" and what it "Turns OFF". If nothing changes it says "This preset matches the current module state — nothing changes."
    • Menus & pages removed (orange) lists the menu areas that will disappear.
    • Menus & pages added (green) lists the menu areas that will appear.
    • If staff will lose access, you see "N users will lose access to the following:", then each person by name and role with "Will lose: ..." and the names of the permissions. If nobody is affected you see "No active users currently rely on the modules being turned off."
  4. Click Apply Preset to go ahead, or Cancel (or click outside the window) to change nothing.
  5. You will see "Preset applied: <preset name> — refreshing admin view...". The page reloads by itself after a second so the menu matches.

The preview only lists active staff whose role is not admin. People with the admin role are never listed because their permissions are not removed.

Applying a preset changes every switch at once, including the Restaurant and Events guest pages. Always read the list in the window before clicking Apply Preset. The preset is all-or-nothing: if saving fails part way, nothing is changed.

How to switch one module on or off

  1. Open Settings → Modules.
  2. Click the switch on the module card.
  3. Switching Bookings & Reservations off asks first: a window titled Disable Module? says "Disabling "Bookings & Reservations" will hide its pages and navigation links. Disabling hides all booking pages and the Bookings nav section. You can re-enable it at any time." Click Yes, Disable to continue or Keep Enabled to stop. Every other module switches off straight away with no question.
  4. You will see "Disabled: <module> — refreshing admin view..." (or "Enabled: ..."). The page reloads after a second.

Unlike a preset, a single switch does not show a list of affected staff. Use a preset if you want that preview.

How to switch a station on or off

  1. On the POS & Stations card, make sure the main switch is on.
  2. Click Configure Stations. The four station rows appear.
  3. Click the switch next to the station. The label changes between On and Off.
  4. The page reloads after a second.

A station screen needs two things to be on: POS & Stations and its own station switch. Turning a station off removes its screen and its menu entry.

How to switch the Restaurant or Events page on or off

  1. Scroll to Guest Website Pages.
  2. Click the switch on Events Page or Restaurant Page.
  3. The page reloads after a second.

These two switches control the guest-facing page and the staff pages that go with it (see the next section). They are separate from the module switches. For example, a gym may use the POS for a snack till and still not want a restaurant page.

What disappears when something is off

Three things happen when a module is off:

  1. Its menu items disappear from the admin menu. If every item in a menu group is gone, the whole group disappears.
  2. Its pages are blocked, even by typing the address. Staff who try to open one are sent back to their home page. The one exception: users with the admin role can still open a page by its direct address, but they will not see it in the menu.
  3. Its permissions stop applying. The staff permission is still stored (switch the module on again and it works again), but the person cannot use the page. In Staff & Access, permissions for an off module are no longer offered for new grants, although anything a person already holds is still shown.

Three menu items depend on a combination, not a single switch:

  • Invoices and Quotations need Finance plus at least one of Bookings, Conference or Gym. They disappear for a till-only business.
  • Credit Notes needs Finance plus either Bookings or Conference.
  • POS Accounting needs both Finance and POS.

These pages are never switched off by a module: the Dashboard, Hotel Settings, Email Templates, Staff & Access, Backups, Modules, Visitor Analytics, Automated Emails, WhatsApp Settings, Facebook Settings, API Keys, System Logs, Cache and the Help links (Staff Guides, Admin Reference, Email Template Tags). Hotel Settings is deliberately kept available, because it is the only place email sending is set up.

The Hotel Settings page also adapts: with Bookings & Reservations off, the page is called Business Settings and the booking-only cards are hidden (see Hotel Settings).

Rules the system enforces

  • Finance & Accounting can never be switched off. If something tries, the system answers "Finance & Accounting cannot be disabled — it is required by all business types."
  • No data is deleted when a module is switched off. Bookings, stock, members and money records stay as they were.
  • A station only works when both POS and the station switch are on.
  • Starting categories added by a preset are only added when they do not already exist.
  • Every module and preset change is written to the system log, with the name of the person who made it.
