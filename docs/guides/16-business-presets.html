Business Presets & Module Settings

Shape the whole system around your actual business — hotel, bar, gym, shop or supermarket — with one click.

URL /admin/module-settings.php Role admin only (module_settings) Read time 8 min

What a preset is

A business preset is a one-click profile that switches the right feature modules on and off for your type of business. Instead of toggling ten modules by hand, you pick what you are — and the admin menus, dashboard, finance pages, guest website and even the report exports all reshape themselves to match.

Preset What it turns on
Full Hotel Everything — rooms, housekeeping, POS + all station displays, stock, conference, gym, website. The complete system.
Hotel (No Restaurant) Rooms, housekeeping, conference and gym — no POS or stock.
Bar / Restaurant POS with Kitchen and Bar displays, stock, tables and recipes. No rooms, no website CMS.
Conference Venue Bookings, conference rooms and the website. No POS or stock.
Gym / Fitness Gym suite (packages, members, check-in, reports), a POS till for the front desk, events and the website. No stock, no rooms.
Retail / Shop POS till + stock + finance only. Products instead of menus; no food-service pages.
Supermarket Like Retail, plus the website. Starter categories (Groceries, Household…) are seeded so you can start scanning products immediately.
Finance is always on. Every business reconciles takings, issues receipts and reads reports — the Finance module can never be disabled.

Applying a preset

  1. Open Configuration → Module Settings.
  2. Click the preset that matches your business. The currently active preset carries a green Active badge.
  3. An impact dialog opens first — nothing changes until you confirm. It shows exactly what turns ON, what turns OFF, which menus & pages appear or disappear, and which staff accounts would lose access to areas being switched off.
  4. Confirm. Modules apply in one step, guest-facing pages follow, starter product categories are seeded for shop-style presets, and the admin view refreshes itself so the new menus show immediately.
You can also toggle any single module on or off on the same page — presets are just shortcuts. Station sub-modules (Kitchen, Bar, Coffee, Room Service displays) live inside the POS card and can be switched individually.

What changes when a module is off

  • Admin menus — items and whole menu groups for that module disappear; typing the page URL directly is blocked too.
  • Dashboard — cards, widgets and quick links for that module vanish; a POS-first business gets an orders/takings overview instead of check-ins.
  • Finance pages — payments, receipts, invoices and other lists open showing records from your active modules; wording adapts (e.g. "F&B" becomes "POS / Till Sales" when there is no restaurant).
  • Guest website — navigation links, footer links, policies and the pages themselves follow the preset. A gym preset shows no Rooms or Restaurant pages; the homepage always stays.
  • Reports & End of Day — tabs, KPI panels and the PDF / email / WhatsApp exports all include only what your business runs.
  • User roles — creating a staff account only offers roles relevant to your preset (a supermarket is not offered "Chef").
No data is ever deleted. Switching presets hides features, not history. Finance lists show a "Showing records for your active modules only" notice with a Show all history link whenever older records from disabled modules exist — one click brings everything back into view.

Products vs menus (shop-style presets)

The POS sells from one catalog, but it is scoped by business type. A restaurant manages Food & Drinks menus with recipes and kitchen routing; a shop, gym or supermarket manages Products — simple items with a price and a barcode. Selling a product deducts one unit of stock automatically (product = stock item), and the same barcode is used to receive stock in and sell it out. Switching presets swaps which catalog the till shows; neither is deleted.

Adding employees

  1. Open Configuration → User Management and click Add User.
  2. Pick a role — the list only offers roles that make sense for your preset. Existing users keep whatever role they already have.
  3. Tick Send welcome email — the new employee receives their username, a temporary password and the login link. A Resend welcome button on each user row issues a fresh temporary password later if needed.
  4. Station staff land on their own screen after login — a bartender opens straight onto the Bar Display, a cashier onto the POS till.
Self-service password change: every employee can click the key icon in the admin header, enter their current and new password, and confirm with a 6-digit code emailed to them (valid 10 minutes). No admin needed.
