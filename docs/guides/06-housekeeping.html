Guide 06 · Housekeeping

Housekeeping

Track every individual room's clean / dirty / inspected status. Every change is logged with old → new state and timestamp.

URL /admin/housekeeping.php Role housekeeping · manager · admin Read time 5 min

Status colours

Status Meaning
Clean Ready for the next guest.
Dirty Guest has checked out / room needs service.
In progress Housekeeper currently cleaning.
Inspected Manager-verified clean.
Out of service Maintenance issue, blocked from booking.

A typical morning

  1. Sign in. The board shows every room grouped by floor.
  2. Tap a dirty card → choose Start cleaning → status moves to In progress, timer starts.
  3. When done, tap Mark clean.
  4. The supervisor walks through and taps Inspected on each green card to mark it ready for the next arrival.

Out of service

  1. Tap a card → Mark out of service.
  2. Type a reason (e.g. "AC not cooling, parts ordered").
  3. The room is removed from the bookable inventory automatically.
  4. Once fixed, tap Return to clean.

Audit history

  • Every status change is saved to housekeeping_audit_log.
  • Tap a room name to see who changed what, when, and the reason.

Tips

If a guest reports a dirty room, mark it dirty immediately — the room stays bookable but supervisors get an alert.

Always sign out at end of shift — the next housekeeper sees their own action history.
