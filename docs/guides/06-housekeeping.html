Housekeeping and Room Maintenance

This guide covers the two screens that keep rooms ready for guests at Rosalyns Beach Hotel. Housekeeping is for cleaning tasks, including the clean after a guest checks out. Room Maintenance is for repairs and other work that takes a room out of use.

Who uses it
Housekeeping supervisors, receptionists, managers, maintenance staff
Where to find it
Admin menu → Front Desk → Housekeeping (/admin/housekeeping.php) and admin menu → Front Desk → Room Maintenance (/admin/room-maintenance.php)
Permission needed
Housekeeping for the Housekeeping screen. Room Maintenance for the Room Maintenance screen. Each screen needs its own permission.
Only shown when
Housekeeping: the Housekeeping module is switched on in Settings → Modules. Room Maintenance: the Bookings & Reservations module is switched on in Settings → Modules.

What is on the screen

Housekeeping screen

The page title is Housekeeping Management. From top to bottom:

  • Auto-Create Checkout Cleanup and Add Assignment buttons at the top right.
  • Number cards: Today's Tasks, Pending Tasks, In Progress, Completed Today, High Priority. A Blocked card appears only when at least one task is blocked. Click a card to open a window (for example "Pending Tasks - Quick Insights") listing up to 12 matching tasks with the room, who it is assigned to, the due date and the notes.
  • Occupied Rooms list. Shown only when at least one guest is checked in. Each room card shows the room, the guest name and the check-out date. A card marked Checkout Today has a guest leaving today.
  • Staff Workload table (columns Staff Member, Active Tasks, High Priority Pending, Completed Today). It lists only staff who have an open task or finished one today.
  • All Assignments table with a search box ("Search room, staff…") and a status filter (All Statuses, Pending, In Progress, Completed, Verified, Blocked). Columns: Room, Type, Priority, Status, Scheduled, Assigned To, Notes, Actions. The Scheduled column shows the due date, and under it the scheduled time, the time the task was started and the time it was completed when they exist. Notes are cut off after 30 characters.

The buttons in the Actions column are small icons. Point at one to see its name. They are, left to right: Mark as started (only on Pending tasks), Mark done (Pending and In progress tasks), Edit (every task except Verified ones), View History, Verify (only on Completed tasks) and a red bin to delete.

Tasks are listed with Pending first, then In progress, Completed, Verified and Blocked. Inside each group, High priority comes before Medium and Low, then the earliest due date.

Room Maintenance screen

The page title is Room Maintenance Management. It has the same layout:

  • Auto-Create Tasks and Add Maintenance buttons at the top right.
  • Number cards: Today's Tasks, Pending Tasks, In Progress, Completed Today, Verified Today, High/Urgent Priority, Emergency. Click a card to see up to 12 matching requests.
  • Rooms Needing Maintenance list. Shown when a room is marked Maintenance or Out of Order, or has a guest checked in. Each card shows the room and its status.
  • Staff Workload table (same four columns as above).
  • All Maintenance Tasks table, 10 rows per page with page numbers underneath. It has a search box ("Search room, title, staff...") and a status filter (All Statuses, Pending, In Progress, Completed, Verified, Cancelled). Columns: Room, Title, Type, Priority, Status, Scheduled, Assigned To, Actions. The action icons work exactly as on the Housekeeping screen.
  • Maintenance Log at the bottom (shown once there is something to show). It lists the last 50 room status changes with the columns Room, From, To, Reason, By, At.

Priorities for cleaning tasks are High, Medium and Low. Maintenance requests also have Urgent.

What happens to a room after check-out

You do not have to create the clean by hand. This is the normal flow:

  1. Reception checks the guest out (see the Reception and Bookings guide). The room status changes to Cleaning.
  2. At the same moment a cleaning task is created for the room. It is Pending, due today, with the note "Room turnover cleaning".
  3. Open Housekeeping. The task is in the All Assignments table. Assign it to a cleaner by clicking Edit and choosing a name in Assigned To.
  4. The cleaner starts the job. Click Mark as started. The status becomes In Progress.
  5. When the room is clean, click Mark done. The status becomes Completed. As soon as the room has no other open cleaning task, the room status changes from Cleaning to Available and the room can be sold or checked in.
  6. A supervisor looks at the room and clicks Verify. The task becomes Verified and is locked. Verifying does not change the room status. It only records who checked the room and when.

A room is ready for the next guest as soon as its cleaning task is Completed. You do not have to wait for Verify.

How to work through a cleaning task (start, finish, verify)

  1. Go to Front Desk → Housekeeping.
  2. Find the room in All Assignments. Use the search box (room number or staff name) or the status filter if the list is long.
  3. To start: click Mark as started (the play icon). A box asks "Mark this assignment as started?". Click Start. You will see "Assignment marked as started." The start time is recorded.
  4. To finish: click Mark done (the tick icon). A box asks "Mark this assignment as completed?". Click Mark Done. You will see "Assignment marked as completed." The finish time is recorded. If the task was never started, the start time is set to the same moment.
  5. To verify (supervisor): click Verify (the double-tick icon, shown only on Completed tasks). A box asks "Mark this assignment as verified? This cannot be undone.". Click Verify. You will see "Assignment verified successfully."

Verified cannot be undone. A verified task cannot be edited. The only thing you can still do to it is delete it.

You can also skip a step by editing the task and choosing a different Status (see How to edit, correct or delete a cleaning task).

How to add a cleaning task

  1. Click Add Assignment. A window titled Add Assignment opens.
  2. Choose the Room (required). When you pick a room, the line under the box shows its current state, for example "Room: cleaning | Housekeeping: pending".
  3. Choose the Assignment Type (required): Regular Cleaning, Checkout Cleanup (High Priority), Deep Clean, Maintenance or Turn Down Service.
  4. Choose the Priority (required): Medium, High or Low.
  5. Choose the Due Date (required). It cannot be before today. The note under the box says "Due date cannot be in the past".
  6. Optional: Scheduled Time (time of day, for example 09:00 or 14:30, in 5-minute steps). Started At (leave blank and the system stamps the time when you click Mark as started).
  7. Status: leave it on Pending for a new task. The list is Pending, In Progress, Completed, Verified, Blocked.
  8. Assigned To: choose a staff member, or leave Unassigned.
  9. Estimated Duration (minutes): starts at 30. Minimum 5, in steps of 5. Actual Duration (minutes) is for when the job is done (it is saved when you edit a task).
  10. Tick Recurring Task if the job repeats. Two more boxes appear. Recurring Pattern: Daily, Weekly or Monthly. Recurring End Date: leave empty for no end date.
  11. Type any Notes.
  12. Click Save. The button shows "Saving…". The result appears at the bottom left of the window. On success you will see "Assignment created successfully." and the list refreshes. Click Close to shut the window.

The Recurring Task settings are saved with the task. The screen does not create the next task for you, so add the next one yourself when you need it.

How to create the checkout cleanings in one click

Use this at the start of a shift if some checked-out rooms have no cleaning task yet.

  1. Click Auto-Create Checkout Cleanup.
  2. A box asks "Auto-create checkout cleanup assignments for all rooms that need it?". Click Auto-Create.
  3. You will see "Auto-created 3 checkout cleanup assignments." (the number is however many were made, which can be 0).

The system looks at each room whose latest booking is checked out (or still checked in past its check-out date) with a check-out date of today or earlier. If that room has never had a checkout cleanup task, it creates one: type Checkout Cleanup, priority High, due today, Unassigned, note "Checkout cleanup required". A room is never given a second checkout cleanup, even if you run this again.

How to assign cleaning to occupied rooms

Use this for a daily stay-over clean. It is in the Occupied Rooms section.

  1. Click each room card you want. A selected card shows Selected. Click again to unselect. Select All selects every card (click it again to unselect all).
  2. Choose a staff member in the first box (or leave Unassigned).
  3. Choose Medium Priority, High Priority or Low Priority.
  4. Click Assign Selected (n). The button is greyed out until you select at least one room.
  5. You will see "Bulk assigned 4 rooms for housekeeping."

Each task is Regular Cleaning, Pending and due today. A room that already has a Pending or In progress task is skipped, so the number in the message can be lower than the number you selected.

For one room only, click Quick Assign on its card. The Assign Room window opens with that room and today's date filled in. Fill in the rest as in How to add a cleaning task and click Save.

How to edit, correct or delete a cleaning task

Edit

  1. Click Edit (pencil icon) on the task. The Edit Assignment window opens with the saved details.
  2. Change what you need. This is where you assign a cleaner, change the priority, set Status to Blocked, or fill in Actual Duration (minutes).
  3. Click Save. You will see "Assignment updated successfully."

If you set the Status to Completed or Verified here, the completed time is set to now. Choosing Verified records you as the person who verified it. If you move a Completed task back to Pending or In Progress, the completed time is cleared and the room goes back to Cleaning.

Move the task to a different room

Edit the task and change the Room. Both rooms are updated.

Delete

  1. Click the red bin on the task.
  2. A box asks "Delete this assignment? This cannot be undone.". Click Delete.
  3. You will see "Assignment deleted successfully."

If the deleted task was the room's only open cleaning, a room that was in Cleaning goes back to Available. Delete is the only action available on a Verified task. Deleting it is recorded in the task history.

How to see the history of a task

  1. Click View History (the clock-arrow icon) on the task.
  2. The Audit History window opens, titled with the room and the task number. It lists each change (for example Status Changed), who made it and when.
  3. If nothing has been recorded you will see "No audit history available." Click Close when you are done.

How to report and add a maintenance request

  1. Go to Front Desk → Room Maintenance and click Add Maintenance. The Add Maintenance window opens.
  2. Choose the Room (required). The line under the box shows the room's current status.
  3. Type a Title (required), for example Air conditioner not cooling. Add a Description if you need to.
  4. Choose the Maintenance Type (required): Repair, Replacement, Inspection, Upgrade or Emergency. Choosing Emergency sets the priority to Urgent for you.
  5. Choose the Priority (required): Medium, High, Urgent or Low.
  6. Choose the Due Date (required). It cannot be before today.
  7. Status: leave on Pending for a new request. The list is Pending, In Progress, Completed, Verified, Cancelled.
  8. Start Date and End Date: the window the work covers. The window opens with now and tomorrow filled in. The end must be after the start.
  9. Optional: Started At (leave blank to stamp automatically), Assigned To, Linked Booking (pick the booking of the guest who reported it, or leave None; the list shows the 100 most recent pending, confirmed and checked-in bookings as reference - guest name (room)).
  10. Estimated Duration (minutes) starts at 60 (minimum 5). Actual Duration (minutes) is for when the work is done.
  11. Block room during maintenance is ticked by default. While it is ticked and the request is not Completed, Verified or Cancelled, the room cannot be booked for any night between the Start Date and the End Date. Untick it if guests can still use the room.
  12. Tick Recurring Task to record a repeat pattern, then choose Daily, Weekly or Monthly and an optional Recurring End Date. The screen does not create the repeat requests for you.
  13. Type any Notes and click Save. You will see "Maintenance schedule created successfully."

When a request is open (Pending, In progress or Completed), the room's status is set to Maintenance, unless the room is Occupied or Out of Order. The change is recorded in the Maintenance Log.

Requests the system creates for you

  • Every time the Room Maintenance screen opens, the system creates a task titled "Auto-generated maintenance task" for each room that is in Maintenance or Out of Order, or has a guest checked in, and has no open request. The type and priority depend on the room: Out of Order gets Repair and High, Maintenance gets Repair and Urgent, and a room with a guest gets Inspection and Medium.
  • The Auto-Create Tasks button does the same on demand. Click it, then click Auto-Create in the box "Auto-create maintenance tasks for all rooms that need them?". You will see "Auto-created 2 maintenance tasks."

How to work through a maintenance request (start, finish, verify)

  1. Find the request in All Maintenance Tasks.
  2. To start: click Mark as started. The box says "Mark this maintenance as started?". Click Start. You will see "Schedule marked as started."
  3. To finish: click Mark done. The box says "Mark this maintenance as completed?". Click Mark Done. You will see "Schedule marked as completed."
  4. To sign off (manager or supervisor): click Verify (only on Completed requests). The box says "Mark this maintenance as verified? This cannot be undone.". Click Verify. You will see "Maintenance verified successfully."

Once a request is Completed or Verified it no longer stops the room being booked.

How to assign maintenance to several rooms

  1. In Rooms Needing Maintenance, click each room card you want, or click Select All.
  2. Choose a staff member (or Unassigned), a type (Inspection, Repair, Replacement, Upgrade, Emergency) and a priority (Medium Priority, High Priority, Urgent Priority, Low Priority).
  3. Click Assign Selected (n). You will see "Bulk assigned 3 rooms for maintenance."

Each request is titled "Bulk assigned maintenance task", due today, Pending. Rooms that already have an open request are skipped. Edit the request afterwards to give it a proper title.

How to edit, cancel or delete a maintenance request

  1. To edit: click Edit. The Edit Maintenance window opens. Change what you need and click Save. You will see "Maintenance schedule updated successfully."
  2. To cancel: edit the request, set Status to Cancelled and click Save. The request stays in the list for the record.
  3. To delete: click the red bin. The box says "Delete this schedule? This cannot be undone.". Click Delete. You will see "Maintenance schedule deleted successfully."
  4. To see who changed what: click View History to open Audit History.

On a maintenance request, Save also checks the Due Date is not in the past. If you need to edit an old request, move the due date to today first.

If the room has no open request left (all are Verified, Cancelled or deleted) and its status is Maintenance, the room goes back to Available.

Checking a guest in to a room that is not clean

Reception cannot check a guest in while the room is not ready. A room is not ready when its status is anything other than Available, or when its cleaning is Pending or In progress.

  • Without the permission Check-in to Room Not Clean you will see "Check-in blocked: Room 12 (cleaning pending) is not marked clean yet. Ask housekeeping to finish the room, or ask someone with the "Check-in to Room Not Clean" permission." Ask housekeeping to click Mark done on the task, then check the guest in again.
  • With that permission, a box titled Room not marked clean asks you to confirm. Click Check in anyway to go ahead. The check-in is recorded in the booking timeline as "Checked in to a room not marked clean", with your name.

Rules the system enforces

  • You need the Housekeeping permission to open Housekeeping and the Room Maintenance permission to open Room Maintenance. Without it you are sent back to the dashboard.
  • Cleaning tasks can only be created for active rooms, and only assigned to active staff.
  • A cleaning task or maintenance request cannot be due in the past when you add it. A maintenance request also cannot be due in the past when you edit it.
  • A maintenance request's end date must be after its start date.
  • Verified tasks and requests are locked. They cannot be edited. They can only be deleted.
  • Verify only works on tasks that are Completed and not yet verified.
  • A room is never given two open checkout cleanings, and the same room is never given a second checkout cleanup by Auto-Create Checkout Cleanup.
  • While a room has an open cleaning task, an Available room is moved to Cleaning. When the room has no open cleaning task (everything is Completed, Verified or deleted), a room in Cleaning is moved to Available. This check runs each time you open the Housekeeping screen and after every change you make on it.
  • If a room's status is set to Maintenance or Out of Order, its Pending and In progress cleaning tasks become Blocked.
  • Open maintenance requests move the room to Maintenance, except Occupied and Out of Order rooms, which are left as they are. Pending, In progress and Completed requests all count as open for this. Verifying, cancelling or deleting them releases the room.
  • A maintenance request with Block room during maintenance ticked stops bookings for the room between its Start Date and End Date until the request is Completed, Verified or Cancelled.
  • Checking a guest in to a room that is not clean is blocked unless you have the Check-in to Room Not Clean permission and confirm.
  • Every change to a cleaning task or maintenance request is saved in its history.

"Database error: ..."The system could not save.Try again. If it keeps happening, tell your manager and give them the full message.
