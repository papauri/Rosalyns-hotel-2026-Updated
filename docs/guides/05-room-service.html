Room Service

The Room Service Dashboard is where staff place in-room dining orders for checked-in guests, watch them move through the Kitchen, Bar and Coffee Bar, and mark them delivered. Every item is charged to the guest's room bill (folio) the moment the order is placed.

Who uses it
Room service staff, restaurant staff, receptionists, managers.
Where to find it
Admin menu → Restaurant & Bar → Room Service (/admin/room-service-dashboard.php).
Permission needed
Room Service Dashboard to see the page. Manage Room Service to place orders and mark them delivered.
Only shown when
The Point of Sale and Room Service Station modules are switched on in Settings → Modules.

What is on the screen

  • Edit Room Service Menu — a button that opens the Menu page at the Room Service items.
  • Five figures for today: Today's RS orders, Revenue today, Items served, Avg delivery (time from sent to served) and In progress.
  • Place a Room Service Order — on the left the Room Service menu with a tab for each menu type (the tab name shows the item count) and a tile for each item. On the right the Checked-in booking list (— Select a guest / room —), the Order lines, the Total, Delivery notes (optional) and the button Send to kitchen & charge to folio.
  • Active queue — every room-service order not yet delivered, with columns Ref, Room / Guest, Items (with R: how many are ready and S: how many are served), Stations (stations still working), Total, Status, Placed, Fired (when it reached the stations, with any delay), Wait and a Delivered button. Click the reference to see the order's full history. It says "No room service orders in progress." when empty.
  • Today's folio charges — the food and drink charges posted to bookings today, with Time, Booking, Room, Guest, Item, Qty, Total and Stock. It says "No food/drink charges posted to bookings today." when empty.

Without Manage Room Service you can look at the page but cannot place or deliver orders. When a station finishes all its items for an order the page shows a "ready for collection" alert and plays a sound, for example "Kitchen: order ORD-... (Rm 12) is ready for collection."

How to place a room service order

  1. In Checked-in booking choose the guest. The list shows only guests who are checked in, as room number, guest name and booking reference.
  2. Tap items on the menu. Only items that are available and switched on for room service are shown. Each tile shows which station will prepare it.
  3. In the Order list change quantities if needed (1 to 99 per line).
  4. Optionally type Delivery notes (optional).
  5. Click Send to kitchen & charge to folio. The button stays off until the order has at least one item.
  6. You will see "Room service order ORD-... placed for Rm 12 — 3 item(s), MWK 24,500.00. Charged to folio."

You can also take a room order at the till (Room button). See the POS guide. Both ways produce the same kind of order.

How to mark an order delivered

  1. Find the order in Active queue.
  2. Click Delivered. The page asks "Mark order ORD-... as delivered?" Confirm.
  3. You will see "Order #123 marked as delivered." The order leaves the queue.

Marking delivered marks every line as served and closes the order. Any item whose stock had not yet been taken off is taken off now. The room charge is not changed: it was posted when the order was placed.

How the order reaches the guest's bill

  • When the order is placed, one charge per item is added to the guest's booking as a food or drink charge, at the menu price (tax included). The booking balance is recalculated at once.
  • The guest pays it with the rest of the bill: through Payments or at check-out. It is never paid at the till.
  • Stock for the recipe ingredients is taken off when the charge is posted. The Kitchen, Bar and Coffee Bar do not take it off a second time.
  • The order goes straight to the station screens. Each station works the lines as normal (see the Kitchen guide).
  • If any charge cannot be posted, the whole order is refused and nothing is sent.
  • After the guest has checked out the bill is locked, and new orders cannot be placed because only checked-in guests are listed.

How to cancel or correct an order

  • Before any station has started it: the Kitchen, Bar or Coffee Bar can use Cancel before prep, or someone with the till can cancel it from the Tabs tray. The folio charges are cancelled (voided) and stock is put back.
  • After work has started: a manager voids the order (POS guide). The folio charges are voided and the booking balance is recalculated.
  • One item cannot be made: the station uses 86 this item on the line. That line's charge is removed from the folio.

How to change what room service sells

  1. Click Edit Room Service Menu (or open Restaurant & Bar → Menu).
  2. On the item's row tick or untick Room Service under Routing, and click Save. Items need to be Available too.

Full menu steps are in the POS guide.

Rules the system enforces

  • Only checked-in bookings can have room service orders: "Only checked-in bookings can have room-service orders."
  • An order needs at least one item and a total above zero.
  • A line quantity cannot be more than 100.
  • Prices come from the saved menu, not from the screen.
  • A room that already has an active room service order cannot get another through the till until the first is finished.
  • Only people with Manage Room Service can place orders or mark them delivered.
  • An order that is already delivered or cancelled cannot be marked delivered again.

Problems and fixes

What you seeWhyWhat to do
"Pick a checked-in booking."No guest chosen.Choose one in Checked-in booking.
"Add at least one item."The order is empty.Tap at least one menu item.
"Only checked-in bookings can have room-service orders."The guest has checked out or is not yet in.Check the booking status.
"Menu item not found or not available."The item was switched off after the page loaded.Reload the page and choose again.
"Failed to add charge for (item): ..."The bill could not take the charge.Read the reason, fix it and try again. Nothing was placed.
"Quantity cap: 100 per line."Quantity too high.Lower it.
"You do not have permission to perform this action."No Manage Room Service permission.Ask a manager.
"Order is already finalized and cannot be marked delivered."It was already delivered or cancelled.Nothing to do.
"Security token invalid. Refresh and try again."The page is out of date.Reload and repeat.
