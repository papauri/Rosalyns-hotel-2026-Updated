Guide 04 · Coffee station

CDS — Coffee Display

The coffee counter's live order screen. Built for fast, repeat orders.

URL /admin/cds.php Role coffee_staff · manager · admin Read time 4 min

What lands here

  • Espresso, Americano, Latte, Cappuccino, etc.
  • Tea, hot chocolate.
  • Pastries, breakfast plates routed to the coffee station.

Step-by-step

  1. Order arrives → chime + ticket.
  2. Tap Start as you begin building the drink.
  3. When each drink is complete, tap Ready on that line. This marks it done and deducts the ingredients from stock at this moment.
  4. If milk, beans, syrup or another recipe ingredient is short, CDS shows a friendly stock dialog and blocks Ready/Bump until stock is received, the recipe is fixed, or the item is 86'd.
  5. Tap Collect to mark the drink taken by the guest or runner.
  6. Tap Bump when all items on the ticket are done.
  7. Call the guest by table or room number printed on the ticket.
Stock deducts at Ready, not when the order fires. Cancels before any item is marked Ready cost no stock.
Use Sound Settings from the top/drawer menu to choose coffee alert tones and volume. Ticket cards show FOH/orderer, guest/service type and item counts, and action buttons spin while each change is being saved.

RUSH priority

  • A ⚡ RUSH badge means the cashier has marked the order urgent. RUSH tickets jump to the top of the board.
  • An URGENT message in the bottom bar fires the moment Rush is set or cleared.
  • You can also toggle Rush from the Rush button in the ticket footer.

Ticket timer & age colours

  • Elapsed time shows as: just fired → 45 sec → 3m 12s → 12 min → 1h 04m.
  • Sub-label gives context: On time · Watch closely · Late · Overdue — check with FOH (over 1 hour).
  • All times are in Malawi local time (UTC+2) — accurate to the second.

Useful behaviour

  • Items that share an ingredient (e.g. milk) all decrement the same stock row when you tap Ready.
  • Cards turn amber after 5 minutes — pull the espresso shot first when you see this.
  • Recall recent served tickets from the Served menu (up to 80 entries per view) if a guest comes back saying their drink was wrong. Recalling restores any deducted stock so you can re-make correctly.
  • Use ticket notes when you need to send a clarification back to POS without leaving the station screen.

Order Lifecycle Log (ticket footer)

Each CDS ticket has a Log link in the ticket footer. Tap it to open the full order timeline for that ticket in a new browser tab, showing every event from placement through stock deduction and payment. Use it to audit a complaint or confirm a comp order.

Mobile-friendly & fullscreen kiosk

Use a tablet on a counter stand. Long-press any control to read the tooltip. Burger menu in the top-right has all the controls.
  • Buttons use short click-lock protection to avoid accidental double actions.
  • Fullscreen kiosk mode — a banner appears at the bottom on load saying "Tap anywhere to go fullscreen". One tap hides browser tabs and address bar. If you exit accidentally, the banner reappears and any tap re-enters it. Ideal for counter stands and wall-mounted tablets.
  • The board refreshes every 2 seconds — RUSH alerts and note changes from POS arrive almost instantly. POS receives ready and station-note popups within about 1 second, with item summaries and a 2-minute closeable banner.
