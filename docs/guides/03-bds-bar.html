Guide 03 · Bar

BDS — Bar Display

Same engine as the kitchen — tuned for bar drinks. Works as a standalone touchscreen.

URL /admin/bds.php Role bar_staff · manager · admin Read time 4 min

What's different from the kitchen?

  • Only drink items appear here (cocktails, beer, wine, soft drinks, spirits).
  • Tickets are usually short (1–3 lines) and fast — the bar pace is high.
  • Each drink can be ticked off individually as it's poured.

Step-by-step

  1. New ticket lands → chime + card.
  2. Tap Start when you begin pouring.
  3. When each drink is poured, tap Ready on that line. This signals the drink is at the pass and deducts the bottle/ingredient from stock at this moment.
  4. If the required bottle or ingredient is short, BDS shows a friendly stock dialog and blocks Ready/Bump until stock is received, the recipe is fixed, or the item is 86'd.
  5. Tap Collect to mark the drink taken by the runner for delivery to the guest.
  6. Tap Bump when the full round is complete — any items not yet marked Ready are deducted at Bump.
Stock deducts at Ready, not at order fire. If an order is cancelled at the till before you tap Ready, no stock is removed.
Use Sound Settings from the top/drawer menu to choose bar alert tones and volume. Ticket cards show FOH/orderer, guest/service type and item counts, and action buttons spin while each change is being saved.

Filters

All · New · Cooking · Ready — same logic as the kitchen.

RUSH priority

  • A ⚡ RUSH badge on a ticket means the cashier or manager has marked the order high-priority. Rush tickets sort to the top of the board.
  • An URGENT message in the bottom bar appears the moment Rush is set or cleared — you can't miss it.
  • Tap Rush in the ticket footer to toggle it from the bar screen.

Ticket timer & age colours

  • Each ticket shows elapsed time since fired: just fired → 45 sec → 3m 12s → 12 min → 1h 04m.
  • Sub-label gives context: On time · Watch closely · Late · Overdue — check with FOH (over 1 hour on the board).
  • All times are accurate in Malawi local time (UTC+2).

Bar-specific tips

Stock deducts when you tap Ready on each drink — bottle counts in Stock Ingredients drop at that moment, not when the order fires.
  • Use the built-in ticket message flow to send quick clarifications back to POS when needed.
  • If you cancel a drink (broken glass, refused), tell the cashier to use Cancel Before Prep (if not yet started) or Void (manager, if already poured). Stock for poured items is restored automatically on void. Never just remove the ticket.
  • Voids are admin/manager-only. If your manager isn't there, leave the ticket on the board and notify the till.
  • A short countdown badge on each ticket shows how long it's been waiting.

Mobile / tablet & fullscreen kiosk

  • Burger menu in the top-right contains: filter, sound, sign-out.
  • Action buttons use brief click-lock behavior to prevent double taps.
  • Long-press any button for help text.
  • Fullscreen kiosk mode — a banner at the bottom says "Tap anywhere to go fullscreen" on load. One tap hides the browser tabs, address bar and all chrome. If you exit accidentally, the banner reappears and the next tap re-enters fullscreen. Keeps the board full-screen on any device.
  • The board polls every 2 seconds — RUSH alerts and FOH note changes arrive in under 2 seconds. POS receives ready and station-note popups within about 1 second, with item summaries and a 2-minute closeable banner.

Order Lifecycle Log (ticket footer)

Each BDS ticket card has a Log link in the footer. Tap it to open /admin/order-lifecycle.php in a new tab — shows every event on the order: placement, KDS/BDS events, stock deductions, payment, and any void with reason. Use this when a guest disputes a charge or a drink needs investigating.

Cancel and Void actions inside the lifecycle page require admin or manager role.

End of shift

Tap Daily Report to see the bar's served tickets, total drink count, top sellers and average prep time. Use this to settle stock variance against Stock Ingredients.
