Bar Display (BDS)

The Bar Display is the screen the bar uses to see drink orders sent from the till, make them, and mark them ready and served. It is the same screen as the Kitchen Display, showing only the lines that are set to go to the Bar.

Who uses it
Bartenders (default role: Bar Staff). Managers.
Where to find it
Admin menu → Restaurant & Bar → Bar Display (BDS) (/admin/bds.php). It opens in a new browser tab.
Permission needed
Bar Display (BDS)
Only shown when
The Point of Sale and Bar Display (BDS) modules are switched on in Settings → Modules.

What is on the screen

  • Top bar: BDS and the hotel name; counts for Open, Ready and Served shift; Hours with an Open or Closed label; filter buttons All, New, Cooking, Ready; the clock; FOH chat; Sound; Settings (sound settings); Served; Help; BDS Guide; Daily Report (only with the Station Reports (KDS/BDS/CDS) permission); Dashboard (only with the Dashboard permission); Logout. On small screens these are in a Menu panel.
  • Board: one card per ticket, showing the table or order type, a timer, the order reference, who took the order, any order note, the lines (quantity, name, line note and the next-step tag) and the buttons at the bottom. Lines for other stations (for example food) are shown with that station's name, and a note such as 1 item at other station tells you the Kitchen is still working.
  • Bottom panels: FOH Notes, All Day (total of drinks still waiting or being made) and Online Flow.
  • If the bar is outside its hours a banner reads BDS is currently closed with the hours. When there is nothing to make the board says No open tickets.

Which drinks come here is decided on the Menu page by each item's Station: choose Bar (BDS) for bar drinks. Items set to Coffee Bar (CDS) go to the coffee bar screen instead (see the POS guide).

Drinks are handed over at the till. When a guest pays straight away at the till, or when a tab is settled, the system marks the Bar drinks as served by itself, takes the stock off, and the ticket leaves your board. You do not need to bump drinks for the cashier to be able to take payment.

How to make a ticket

  1. Watch for a new ticket. Its timer starts when the cashier sends it.
  2. Click Start all to mark every pending drink as being made, or tap each line. Start all only shows while the ticket still has pending lines for the bar.
  3. When a drink is ready, tap its line (tag Ready). If an ingredient is short you see "Stock check stopped this action: Gin short by 750.000 ml. Receive stock, adjust the recipe, or 86 the item before marking it ready." and the line does not move.
  4. When the runner takes it, tap again (Collect), then once more when it is delivered (Serve).

The timer changes at 5 minutes (warning) and 10 minutes (late). A ticket waiting more than 12 minutes without being ready is flagged urgent. The small icons on your lines are Recipe card and 86 this item. The eye icon (View whole order) shows all stations' lines and the stream icon (Order log) shows the order's history.

How to bump a whole ticket

  1. Click Bump at the bottom of the ticket.
  2. All remaining bar lines are marked served and the ticket leaves the board. Stock for any line not yet deducted comes off now.

If stock is short the bump is refused and nothing changes. Bump is greyed out when the ticket has no bar lines.

How to mark a ticket as a rush

  1. Click Rush. You see "Marked as RUSH — FOH notified". The ticket moves to the top of the board.
  2. Click Un-rush to clear it. You see "Rush cleared".

How to cancel a ticket before work starts

The Cancel before prep button (a cross in a circle) only shows while no line on the order has been started at any station.

  1. Click it. Cancel Order Before Prep opens.
  2. Click a reason: Customer cancelled, Wrong order sent, Out of ingredient, Quality issue, Test / training order or Other. You can add more in Additional details or custom reason….
  3. Click Confirm Cancel (or Keep). A reason shorter than 8 characters gives "Choose a reason before confirming".
  4. You see "Order cancelled — FOH notified".

An order that already has any payment recorded cannot be cancelled here; a manager must void it at the till.

How to 86 an item on a ticket

  1. Click 86 this item on the line. The window 86 Item says "86 (item name)? This removes it from the order and sends an urgent alert to FOH."
  2. Click 86 this item to confirm (or Keep). You see "Item 86'd — FOH notified".

The line is cancelled and the order total comes down. If it had not been started the stock is put back; if it had, it is recorded as wastage. If the order was already paid a pending refund is raised for the cashier. The cashier who opened the order is told to inform the guest and offer an alternative.

This does not switch the drink off for future orders. Ask the till to 86 the menu item (see the POS guide).

How to talk to the front of house

  1. Click the speech-bubble icon on a ticket to open its notes. A number shows how many there are, and the icon turns red when a note is unread.
  2. Reply with a quick reply (On it!, 5 minutes, 10 minutes, Ready soon, Out of stock, Need clarification) or type in Custom reply… and click the send arrow. You see "Reply sent to FOH".
  3. To start a note, type in Send a note to FOH about this ticket… (up to 240 characters) and send it. You see "Sent to FOH".
  4. Click the small cross on a note to dismiss it. FOH chat keeps the last 6 hours of messages.

An urgent note covers the middle of the screen. Click Got it to dismiss it or Open chat to reply.

How to look at served tickets

  1. Click Served. The Served Menu opens.
  2. Choose Current Shift, Yesterday or After Hours.
  3. You see Orders served, Items served, Revenue, Avg prep and the tickets.

Bar hours, pause and the daily report

  • Hours: set at Admin menu → Advanced → Station Hours (permission Ingredients & Recipes). If never saved, the bar uses 11:00 to 03:00 (closing the next morning). Hours decide the business day for the board, Served shift, reports and shift closes, and the "station closed" warning at the till. A ticket sent while the bar is closed is still accepted, once the cashier clicks Fire Anyway. Full steps are in the Kitchen guide.
  • Online Flow: Pause / Go Live and a wait time from 5 to 180 minutes (Save). It is saved and shown on this panel only. The till does not check it.
  • Daily report: click Daily Report (or Admin menu → Advanced → Station Reports). Bar staff see the Bar only. Use CSV, Print or Email. Details are in the Kitchen guide.

Rules the system enforces

  • You can only change lines that belong to the bar. Other stations' lines are shown but cannot be tapped.
  • Stock comes off when a line is marked Ready, Served or bumped, or when the till hands the drink over. If an ingredient is short, the action is refused.
  • A ticket cannot be cancelled once any line has started or any payment is recorded.
  • An item cannot be 86'd on an order that is already refunded, voided or cancelled, or once the line is served.
  • A cancel needs a reason of at least 8 characters.
