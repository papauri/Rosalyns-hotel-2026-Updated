Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for when you are stuck. The questions at the top are the ones staff ask most. Below them is every "How to…" and every error message from all the guides, so you can type what you see on the screen and find the fix.

If you are lost

How do I find something quickly?

In the admin, press Ctrl + K (Cmd + K on a Mac), or / when you are not typing in a box, or click Search in the top bar. Type a booking reference, guest name, phone number, receipt number, room, menu item, setting or a question such as "how to refund". Records, pages and the matching parts of these guides all appear. Use the arrows and Enter to open one.

In the guides, use the search box at the top of every page (press / to jump to it).

Which guide should I read first?

Read the guide for your job on the guides home page: Reception & bookings for the front desk, POS till for restaurant and bar staff, the Kitchen, Bar or Coffee screen guide for the stations, Housekeeping for room cleaning, and Payments or Reports for money. Everyone should skim the Admin reference once.

How do I get help for the page I am on?

Click Help in the admin top bar. It opens help for that page: where it is in the menu, which permission it needs and who has it, and the guide sections and common problems for that page.

Where is a page, and who is allowed to open it?

The System map lists every page in the admin menu with the permission it needs, the roles that have it by default and the module that must be switched on. It is read straight from the system, so it is always up to date. You can also type "where is …" in any search box.

What can my role do?

Each role starts with a set of permissions, listed under Roles on the System map. An administrator can add or remove single permissions for one person in Staff & access. Search "what can a receptionist do" (or any role) for the list.

A page I need is missing from my menu. Why?

You only see the pages your permissions allow, and pages for parts of the system that are switched off are hidden for everyone. Ask an administrator to tick the permission in Staff & access, or to check Settings → Modules. The System map shows the permission and module behind every menu page.

It says "Security token invalid" or "Invalid form token". What do I do?

The page was open too long. Reload the page and do the action again. Nothing was saved by the failed attempt.

It says something "already exists" and will not save.

The system refuses duplicates, such as a second supplier, room or menu item with the same name (capital letters and extra spaces do not count as different). Find the existing record and edit it instead. See Duplicates and required fields.

I clicked Save twice. Is it saved twice?

No. Save buttons lock while saving, and a booking or payment sent twice is recorded once; the second click says "already created" or "already recorded".

The dashboard shows a red "System needs attention" box.

A backup is old, disk space is low, a background job has stopped or the database is down. Open Backups or System Logs from the box and tell the administrator.

Signing in and passwords

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in page click Forgot Password?, enter the email address on your account, then open the email and click the link within 1 hour to choose a new password (at least 8 characters). See Signing in.

My account is locked.

After 5 wrong passwords an account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait for the minutes shown, or use Forgot Password?. If it says the account has been deactivated, an administrator must tick Active Account again in Staff & access.

Why was I signed out?

You are signed out after 8 hours without activity. Sign in again; you go back to the page you asked for.

How do I change my password?

Click the key button in the top bar, enter your current password and the new one twice, then click Email Me a Verification Code. Type the 6-digit code from the email within 10 minutes and confirm. The new password works from your next sign-in.

A new staff member's invitation link says it is invalid or expired.

Invitation links last 72 hours and stop working once used or when a newer invitation is sent. Send a new one with the envelope button in Staff & access.

Front desk

The guest arrived early and I cannot check them in.

Check-in is refused before the booked arrival date. Use Adjust Stay Dates on the booking first, then check in. See How to change the stay dates.

Check-in is greyed out or says a payment is needed.

At least a part payment must be recorded before check-in. Record the deposit or payment, then check in. A physical room must also be assigned. See How to check a guest in.

Check-in says the room is not clean yet.

Housekeeping has not released the room. Ask housekeeping to finish and verify the cleaning, or ask someone with the Check-in to Room Not Clean permission. See Checking a guest in to a room that is not clean.

The guest is leaving early or late. How do I check them out?

A plain checkout only works on the booked departure day. On any other day use Check Out & Settle on the booking page (or the checkout button on the list). The settlement window charges the extra nights or refunds the unused ones before checking the guest out. See How to check a guest out.

Checkout is blocked because the guest still owes money.

Record the payment first. Only someone with the Check-out With Balance permission can check out a guest who still owes; it is recorded on the booking's timeline.

How do I upgrade a guest to a better room?

Open the booking's More menu and choose Upgrade / change room type. Charge the difference, or give it free (a complimentary upgrade needs a manager or the Edit Booking Financials permission). See How to upgrade or downgrade a room type.

I cannot delete a booking.

Bookings with money on them, and guests who are in-house, cannot be deleted. Refund or void the payments first, or cancel the booking instead. Deleted bookings can be restored from the Deleted tab. See How to delete and restore a booking.

Restaurant, bar and money

How do I set up a happy hour or a promotion at the till?

Admin menu → Restaurant & Bar → Deals & Promos → Add Deal. Choose the type (Happy Hour, Percentage Off, Fixed Amount Off, Buy X Get Y Free, Spend & Save or Combo Deal), the items it covers, and the days, hours and dates. The till applies it automatically; staff do nothing. See How to set up deals and promotions.

Which emails go out automatically, and how do I stop or test them?

Admin menu → Settings → Automated Emails lists every automatic job (payment reminders, quotation and tentative-hold reminders, pre-arrival and review emails, gym renewals, the nightly backup) with a switch each, a master switch, Run now and Send test to me. See Automated Emails.

The internet dropped while I was using the till.

Only orders paid straight away with Pay are saved on the device and sent automatically when the connection returns; they can never be charged twice. Everything else (Fire, tabs, refunds, closing the shift) needs the connection. If you see a network error, do not assume it went through; check Tabs once you are back online. See What happens when the internet drops.

How do I charge food or drinks to a guest's room?

On the till choose Room Service (or the Room order type), pick the checked-in room, add the items and click Fire. Each item goes on the guest's bill straight away. Do not use Pay for a room order. See How to charge an order to a guest's room.

How do I give a guest money back?

Open the payment and click Refund. Choose the amount, the reason and the status (use Pending until the money has actually gone out, then settle it). Mobile money refunds must start as Pending or Processing. See How to refund a payment.

Where do I see today's takings and what still needs doing?

Money → End of Day shows the day's net takings, cash to bank, what is still owed and a close score with the items that need attention. See End of Day report.
