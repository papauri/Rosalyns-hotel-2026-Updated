Rosalyns Beach Hotel All guides Admin reference

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for when you are stuck. The questions at the top are the ones staff ask most. Below them is every "How to…" and every error message from all the guides, so you can type what you see on the screen and find the fix.

If you are lost

How do I find something quickly?

In the admin, press Ctrl + K (Cmd + K on a Mac), or / when you are not typing in a box, or click Search in the top bar. Type a booking reference, guest name, phone number, receipt number, room, menu item, setting or a question such as "how to refund". Records, pages and the matching parts of these guides all appear. Use the arrows and Enter to open one.

In the guides, use the search box at the top of every page (press / to jump to it).

Which guide should I read first?

Read the guide for your job on the guides home page: Reception & bookings for the front desk, POS till for restaurant and bar staff, the Kitchen, Bar or Coffee screen guide for the stations, Housekeeping for room cleaning, and Payments or Reports for money. Everyone should skim the Admin reference once.

How do I get help for the page I am on?

Click Help in the admin top bar. It opens help for that page: where it is in the menu, which permission it needs and who has it, and the guide sections and common problems for that page.

How do I turn the on-screen hints (the round ? button) on or off?

Click the round ? button at the bottom right of the admin screen. When hints are on, small gold ? marks appear on buttons and cards: hover over one (or press and hold on a phone) to read what it does. Click the button again to switch them off. You can also press ? on the keyboard when you are not typing in a box. On the POS Till, open More and choose Help mode (Help Tooltips in the menu on a phone). On the Kitchen, Bar and Coffee screens it is the ? in the top bar.

What does the question mark (?) next to a column heading or a figure card mean?

The small question mark (?) tells you what the number counts, where it comes from and which period it covers. Tap it (or click it on a computer) to read it. On the Stock pages every column heading and figure card has one. If a table is too wide for a phone and shows as cards, a What the columns mean list sits above the cards. It is a short explanation only: it does not change any figure. See the note at the top of Stock, Recipes and Orders.

Where is a page, and who is allowed to open it?

The System map lists every page in the admin menu with the permission it needs, the roles that have it by default and the module that must be switched on. It is read straight from the system, so it is always up to date. You can also type "where is …" in any search box.

What can my role do?

Each role starts with a set of permissions, listed under Roles on the System map. An administrator can add or remove single permissions for one person in Staff & access. Search "what can a receptionist do" (or any role) for the list.

On a phone or tablet: how do I open the menu, find Edit or Delete in the More menu, or hide the install bar?

Menu: on phones, tablets held upright and iPads held sideways, tap the Menu button (three lines) in the top bar. The menu slides in; tap outside it to close. Edit, Delete and other extras: a row shows at most two buttons, and the rest are behind More (three dots). Pop-ups: Save and Cancel always stay in view; only the middle scrolls. Install bar: the Install Admin App bar at the bottom can be hidden with the cross, and it stays hidden for a while. See Using the admin on a phone or tablet.

A page I need is missing from my menu. Why?

You only see the pages your permissions allow, and pages for parts of the system that are switched off are hidden for everyone. Ask an administrator to tick the permission in Staff & access, or to check Settings → Modules. The System map shows the permission and module behind every menu page.

It says "Security token invalid" or "Invalid form token". What do I do?

The page was open too long. Reload the page and do the action again. Nothing was saved by the failed attempt.

It says something "already exists" and will not save.

The system refuses duplicates, such as a second supplier, room or menu item with the same name (capital letters and extra spaces do not count as different). Find the existing record and edit it instead. See Duplicates and required fields.

I clicked Save twice. Is it saved twice?

No. Save buttons lock while saving, and a booking or payment sent twice is recorded once; the second click says "already created" or "already recorded".

The dashboard shows a red "System needs attention" box.

Your hotel data has not been backed up for a long time, the last backup attempt failed, disk space is low, a background job has stopped or the database is down. Open Backups or System Logs from the box and tell the administrator. For a backup warning, an administrator should open Backups and press Run Backup Now.

What needs my attention in System Logs?

Open Settings → System Logs (permission: System Logs). The Needs attention panel at the top covers the last 7 days. If it says All clear., there is nothing to do. Each box has a number, a short meaning, examples, Last seen and a link to the right page.

  • Errors the system logged: something failed while the system was working. If the same message keeps coming back, send it to support. Click Show the errors.
  • Repeated failed sign-ins from one address (red): see Why do I see failed sign-ins?
  • Emails that did not send: usually a wrong or made-up guest address. Check the address on the booking, or the email settings if it happens for everyone.
  • Stock went below zero: items were sold or used before their delivery or count was recorded. Receive the stock or do a Stock Count.
  • Offline work that did not sync: a sale made while a device was offline was refused when it reconnected, so it was not saved. Open the Offline Log and enter it again by hand.
  • API key refused: someone called the hotel's API with a wrong or missing key. If a partner site should connect, give them a correct key in API Keys. Random probes can be ignored.

The event list below the panel starts on Warnings and errors; choose Everything in Show to see all of it. Tick Group repeats to fold the same message into one line with a count. A form refused because of a typing mistake (for example a number in the wrong format) is a warning, not an error, and is not counted in the panel. See System Logs.

Why do I see failed sign-ins?

Someone typed a wrong username or password. A few are normal, for example a forgotten password. In Needs attention the box Failed sign-ins shows how many were wrong password, unknown username or blocked. When 3 or more tries come from one address inside an hour, the box turns red and reads Repeated failed sign-ins from one address. That can mean someone is guessing passwords. If the address is not one of your staff, change that user's password.

Staff often fail because of a typing slip in the email or username, or because they typed the wrong password. The sign-in box takes a username or email. An email only works when no one else has the same email on their account. If someone cannot sign in, they can try their username, or the administrator can look it up in Staff & access (it shows under the email). The page says "Invalid username or password. If this keeps happening, wait 15 minutes and try again." for every kind of mistake, so it never tells anyone which part was wrong. See My account is locked and Can I sign in with my email?

Signing in and passwords

Can I sign in with my email?

Yes. On the sign-in page the first box is called Username or email. Type either your username or the email address on your account, then your password. The email works only when one account has that email. If two accounts ever shared one email, sign in with your username instead. Staff & access no longer lets two people use the same email.

If it fails, the page says "Invalid username or password. If this keeps happening, wait 15 minutes and try again." The same words appear for a wrong username, a wrong email and a wrong password. Five wrong passwords still lock the account for 15 minutes. The POS Till, kitchen and bar screens sign in as before. See Signing in.

I forgot my username.

You do not need it. Type the email address on your account in the Username or email box and sign in with your password. If that does not work, ask the administrator: your username shows as Username: ... under your email in Staff & access. If you forgot your password, click Forgot Password?. See I forgot my password.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in page click Forgot Password?, enter the email address on your account, then open the email and click the link within 1 hour to choose a new password (at least 8 characters). See Signing in.

My account is locked.

After 5 wrong passwords an account is locked for 15 minutes. The page does not say it is locked: it shows the usual "Invalid username or password. If this keeps happening, wait 15 minutes and try again." even with the right password. Wait 15 minutes, or use Forgot Password?. If it still fails after that, an administrator checks that Active Account is ticked in Staff & access, because a switched-off account shows the same message.

Why was I signed out?

Staff stay signed in for a whole shift: you are signed out only after 8 hours without activity. Sign in again; you go back to the page you asked for. After a system update everyone signs in once more, one time only. If you are signed out sooner than 8 hours, tell the administrator.

How do I change my password?

Click the key button in the top bar, enter your current password and the new one twice, then click Email Me a Verification Code. Type the 6-digit code from the email within 10 minutes and confirm. The new password works from your next sign-in.

A new staff member's invitation link says it is invalid or expired.

Invitation links last 72 hours and stop working once used or when a newer invitation is sent. Send a new one with the envelope button in Staff & access.

Front desk

The guest arrived early and I cannot check them in.

Check-in is refused before the booked arrival date. Use Adjust Stay Dates on the booking first, then check in. See How to change the stay dates.

Check-in says the room is not marked clean.

The room is blocked when its status is anything other than Available (for example Cleaning), or when it has a cleaning task due today or earlier that is not done. Ask housekeeping to mark the task done, then check in again. Someone with Check-in to Room Not Clean can click Check in anyway. See Checking a guest in to a room that is not clean.

How do I move a guest who is already in the hotel to another room?

On the Bookings list click Change room on the booking. You can pick a room of the same type or of another type. The old room goes to Cleaning. The booking then shows only the new room. See How to assign or change a room.

Check-in is greyed out or says a payment is needed.

At least a part payment must be recorded before check-in. Record the deposit or payment, then check in. A physical room must also be assigned. See How to check a guest in.

Check-in says the room is not clean yet.

Housekeeping has not released the room. The room can still be assigned (it shows as Turnover), but the check-in waits. Ask housekeeping to finish the cleaning, or ask someone with the Check-in to Room Not Clean permission. See Checking a guest in to a room that is not clean.

The guest is leaving early or late. How do I check them out?

A plain checkout only works on the booked departure day. On any other day use Check Out & Settle on the booking page (or the checkout button on the list). The settlement window charges the extra nights or refunds the unused ones before checking the guest out. A late checkout extends the stay at the guest's booked nightly rate, exactly like Extend stay; there is no separate late-checkout charge. See How to check a guest out.

Checkout is blocked because the guest still owes money.

Record the payment first. Only someone with the Check-out With Balance permission can check out a guest who still owes; it is recorded on the booking's timeline.

How do I upgrade a guest to a better room?

Open the booking's More menu and choose Upgrade / change room type. Charge the difference, or give it free (a complimentary upgrade needs a manager or the Edit Booking Financials permission). See How to upgrade or downgrade a room type.

The Assign Room list shows rooms I cannot pick, or a room says Turnover.

The window now lists every room of the booked type, with the reason a room cannot be used (booked, blocked, maintenance). Turnover means housekeeping is still cleaning the room or the last guest has not left: you can assign it, but check-in waits until it is marked clean. A confirmed guest who arrived late can be given a room too. See How to assign or change a room.

How do I sell two adjoining rooms as one family room (joined rooms)?

Admin menu → Front Desk → Individual Rooms → Room Combinations. Name the pair, choose the room type it is booked as and the two rooms, then choose whether the rooms are sold on their own as well or only as the joined room. A room can be in only one active pair. Use Deactivate to stop selling a pair that bookings have used, and Delete only for one nobody has booked. New combinations are set to Only as the joined room by default, which makes the pair count as one room everywhere. A joined booking is assigned a whole pair in the Assign Room window. See Individual rooms.

I cannot delete a booking.

Bookings with money on them, and guests who are in-house, cannot be deleted. Refund or void the payments first, or cancel the booking instead. Deleted bookings can be restored from the Deleted tab. See How to delete and restore a booking.

Why can't I cancel this booking?

Checked-in, checked-out, cancelled and no-show bookings cannot be cancelled. Check the status first. A cancel also closes the booking's room tab or room-service orders. It is refused only if one of them is part-paid (or a delivered room-service order has been paid). You will see "Order ORD-... is part-paid. Settle or refund it in Orders & Tabs first, then try again." Settle or refund that order in Orders & Tabs, then cancel again. Food the kitchen has already started does not block the cancel: it stays on the guest's bill and the refund is reduced by it. Food not yet started is cancelled and its stock goes back. Nothing is changed by a refused cancel. See How to cancel a booking or mark a no-show.

Why can't I choose Single for two guests?

The price tier must be able to seat the guests, so the booking is never priced cheaper than it should be. Single seats 1 guest, Double seats 2, and Triple seats as many as the room holds. With two guests in a room, Single (1 guest) is greyed out. Pick Double (2 guests) or Triple. For several rooms of the same type the tiers are added up: 4 guests in 2 rooms that hold 3 can be Double + Double or Triple + Single, not Double + Single. A dearer tier is always allowed.

If you save a tier that is too small, you see "Room 1 has 2 guests but is priced as Single (1 guest). Choose Double (2 guests), or ask someone who can override prices." and nothing is saved. An administrator or manager can save the cheaper tier on purpose; the booking history then records "Deliberate under-tier". On Edit Booking the check runs only when you change occupancy or the number of guests. See Price tiers and what they are called and How to create a booking.

Why does a room for 4 say Triple?

Single, Double and Triple are price tiers, not a head count. Triple means 3 or more guests, so a room that holds 4 uses the Triple price. On New Booking the choices read Single (1 guest), Double (2 guests) and Triple (3+ guests). For a room that holds more than 3 the last one reads Triple / family (3–4 guests), with the note "Occupancy sets the price tier. Rooms that hold 4 use the 3+ price." Nothing is wrong and no price changed. A booking for several rooms also shows Group booking: N guests in M rooms at the top. See Price tiers and what they are called.

Can each room in a group have its own guest name?

Yes. You can keep a name per room. On New Booking, each room line has Guest name for this room (optional). To add or change it later, open the room's booking and click Edit Booking (needs Edit Bookings), then fill in Guest name for this room and save. The lead booker stays the guest on the booking and is still the one who pays. Changing only this name does not email the guest. You then see Room guest: [name] on Booking Details, the Bookings list, the Calendar, Individual Rooms, Housekeeping, Room Maintenance, the Room board and the Dashboard arrivals and departures. Searching the Bookings list by that name finds the booking. On a group quotation, invoice or stay statement the room line reads Room – Name. See How to create a booking and How to edit a booking.

How is a booking total worked out?

Click the small i next to the total (Bookings list, Dashboard, Booking Details, Tentative page, Individual Rooms or Payment Details). A window called How this total is worked out opens. It lists Room rate x nights, then Child charge, Packages / add-ons, Tourism levy and VAT where they apply (VAT is shown as included in the room rate above or as added), and ends with Total (incl. VAT & levy). On Booking Details and Payment Details it goes on to Folio extras, Paid and Balance due (or Credit owed to guest). Press Esc or click outside to close it. See How a booking total is worked out and the same in the Payments guide.

Why is the booking total higher than the room price? Does the total include VAT?

Yes, the total includes VAT and levy. The room rate you set already includes VAT and the tourism levy (the ones that are switched on), and nothing is added on top. The Total on a booking is the full amount the guest pays. You see the same full amount on the Bookings list, the booking summary window, the Dashboard Pending Bookings list (Total (incl. VAT & levy)), Booking Details (for rooms booked together, Group total (incl. VAT & levy)), and in the Export CSV file. On Payment Details the room bill reads Total (incl. VAT & levy), so Paid plus Due adds up to it. Conference totals also show the amount with VAT (incl. VAT). When you edit a conference amount and VAT is added on top, the field says before VAT: type the price without VAT. Nothing was added to what guests pay. The old figure was only the room price before VAT and levy. In Reports, a column named Revenue (excl. VAT, incl. levy) is the income before VAT. See The Booking Details page.

Restaurant, bar and money

How do I set up a happy hour or a promotion at the till?

Admin menu → Restaurant & Bar → Deals & Promos → Add Deal. Choose the type (Happy Hour, Percentage Off, Fixed Amount Off, Buy X Get Y Free, Spend & Save or Combo Deal), the items it covers, and the days, hours and dates. The till applies it automatically; staff do nothing. See How to set up deals and promotions.

How do I reply to the kitchen, or message a station, from the till?

Click Messages in the till's top bar. It is a chat with tabs for All, Kitchen, Bar and Coffee Bar. Click Got it or a quick reply to answer a waiting note, or type in the box at the bottom and click Send. Notes you have acknowledged stay in today's history. See How to chat with the stations and send them a note.

How do I delete a POS order (a test or duplicate order)?

You need the Delete Orders permission (administrators have it; an administrator can give it to anyone in Staff & Access). Open the till's Today's orders and click Delete on the order, or use More → Delete on the row in Orders & Tabs. Type a reason of at least 8 characters. Payments are removed, room-service charges come off the guest bill, stock is put back only for items the kitchen had not started, prepared food is recorded as wastage, and the cash drawer is not changed. It is refused if a credit note is linked. See How to delete an order.

Where do I see all of today's orders on the till?

Tap Today's orders on the till's top bar. It needs the POS All Cashiers' Tabs permission. It lists every order of today from all cashiers, with View, Settle, Add items, Refund, Cancel, Void and Delete as your permissions allow, and a More menu on each row. See How to use Today's orders.

The till shows an "Order cancelled", "Order voided" or "Item 86'd" notice. What do I do?

Use the buttons on the notice: Mark as read (or Mark all as read), Open order, Replace item or Remove from tab for an 86'd item, and Settle refund to hand money back for an 86'd item on a paid order. A settled refund is counted on your shift. See cancellation, void and 86 notices.

I cannot rename or switch off a dine-in table.

A table with an open order cannot be renamed or switched off. Settle or cancel the order first. Table names can be edited in the list under Restaurant & Bar → Tables and must be different from each other. See How to set up the dine-in tables.

Which emails go out automatically, and how do I stop or test them?

Admin menu → Settings → Automated Emails lists every automatic job (payment reminders, quotation and tentative-hold reminders, pre-arrival and review emails, gym renewals, the nightly backup) with a switch each, a master switch, Run now and Send test to me. See Automated Emails.

The internet dropped while I was using the till.

Only orders paid straight away with Pay are saved on the device and sent automatically when the connection returns; they can never be charged twice. Everything else (Fire, tabs, refunds, closing the shift) needs the connection. If you see a network error, do not assume it went through; check Tabs once you are back online. See What happens when the internet drops.

Can I sign out if the till still has unsent sales?

Not straight away. Tap Sign out; the till tries to send the sales first. If it cannot, it says "You have N unsent sale(s) on this tablet. Stay signed in until they send." Stay signed in until they send. To leave anyway, tap Sign out again within 10 seconds; the sales stay saved on the tablet for the person who took them. Before you leave the till, check it shows no unsent sales. See Signing out with unsent sales.

How do I charge food or drinks to a guest's room?

On the till choose Room Service (or the Room order type), pick the checked-in room, add the items and click Fire. Each item goes on the guest's bill straight away. Do not use Pay for a room order. See How to charge an order to a guest's room.

The till says "Low / none on record" for an item. Can I still sell it?

Yes. Selling is never blocked at zero stock. Tap the item as normal. The ingredient's stock goes below zero, and a manager fixes it at the next delivery or stock count. See How a sale takes stock off the shelf.

How do I search for a menu item?

Open Restaurant & Bar → Menu and type in Search by item name, category or barcode. The list narrows as you type and a count shows how many items match. Use All categories and the status list (Available only, Unavailable only, Room service items, Not linked to stock) to narrow it more. If nothing matches on the tab you are on, the page says "No food items match ..." and offers a button to show matches on the other tab. Clear search and filters shows everything again. See How to add or change a menu item.

How do I set 250 g of beef or a 25 ml shot on a menu item?

Open the menu item and go to What it uses from stock. Search for the stock item as you type and tap it in the list, then press Add. For stock counted in kg or litres the unit starts on g or ml: type a whole number in the empty box, for example 250 or 25, or tap a quick button. The small grey line shows the maths (= 250 g = 0.250 kg). Save the item. See What it uses from stock.

How do I sell one sausage from a pack?

Open Restaurant & Bar → Menu, click Edit on the item (or Add item) and go to What it uses from stock. Find the sausages with the Category list or the Search box (matches show as you type), and tap them in the list under the box. Choose pieces as the unit and type the whole number 1 (or tap the 1 button). If the pieces choice is greyed out, the number of pieces in one packet is not set yet. Set it in Stock → Ingredients under Pieces in one packet (for example 6). The menu only reads that number and cannot change it, so a menu change never changes stock for everyone. The small grey print shows the working, for example = 1 ÷ 6 packet = 0.166667 packet. Click Add, then Save changes (or Add item for a new item). Each sale now takes 1 sausage off stock, not the whole packet, and 6 sales use 1 packet (to within 0.000002 of a packet). If the unit list has no pieces choice, the stock item is not counted in packets, packs, boxes or dozens: change its Unit in Stock → Ingredients, or use a fraction of its own unit. The number of pieces in a packet is set there only, in Pieces in one packet. See What it uses from stock.

I added a menu item but it isn't on the till

Open Restaurant & Bar → Menu, find the item (use Search by item name, category or barcode) and click Edit. Check that Available (untick to hide it everywhere) and On the till (POS) are ticked. For room service tick In room service. A row marked Website only is not on the till at all. Click Save changes, then reload the till page. If the item was switched off at the till (86), mark it unavailable and then available again on the Menu page to put it back. Items saved on the Menu page reach the till, room service and the Kitchen, Bar and Coffee Bar screens. See How to add or change a menu item.

What does "Opening stock not recorded (N items)" mean, and how do I fix it?

N ingredients have never had a quantity recorded, so they are not counted as out of stock or low. To fix it: open Stock → Stock Count, tick Opening stock count (first set-up) under Start a count, choose What to count, click Start count, type the quantity and the unit cost for every item you are counting (an item left blank is left out and can be counted later), then click Submit for approval (or Submit & correct stock if the second-person setting is Off). When the setting is On, a different manager then clicks Approve & correct stock. See How to do the Opening stock count.

Can I do the opening stock count in parts?

Yes. Under What to count, choose One category or Only the items I choose (then tick the items in the Items to count list) instead of Everything. For example, count the items that already have a cost first, then start another opening count for the rest. Items that are not in a count keep the stock they have now. Each item you count still needs a unit cost, and someone other than you must approve it. See How to do the Opening stock count.

Why does it say some items are already in another opening stock count?

An item can be in only one opening count that is still open (being counted or waiting for approval). The page says "Not started: 2 of these items are already in another opening stock count that is still open (...)" and names the items and the other count. Finish or delete that other count first. Or choose Only the items I choose and leave those items out. See How to do the Opening stock count.

I started a stock count by mistake. How do I delete it?

Open Stock → Stock Count. For a count still being counted, click Delete this count under "Started this count by mistake?", or Delete on its row in the Counts list, then confirm. The person who started it, an administrator, or someone who approves stock counts can do this. A rejected count is deleted with Delete count, by an administrator or by an approver who did not count it. Stock is never changed by a delete, and the deletion is kept in the activity log. A count waiting for approval or approved cannot be deleted. See How to do a stock count.

Why won't my purchase order send?

Usually because the order would take a stock budget over its limit. The order page then shows one of these, before you press Mark as sent:

  • A warn budget: tick "I know this order goes over budget and I want to send it anyway" and send again. Without the tick you see "This order takes the budget over for [category]. Tick the box to send it anyway."
  • A needs approval budget and you cannot approve: the button is locked. You see "This order takes the budget over and needs approval. A budget manager has to send it." Ask a manager with Stock Budget (set & approve).
  • A needs approval budget and you can approve: type a reason of at least 5 characters in the box. Without it you see "This order takes the budget over for [category]. Give a reason (at least 5 characters) to approve it."

A purchase order also needs at least one line before it can be marked as sent. See How to set and watch the stock budget.

How do I set the stock budget?

Open Stock → Budget. You need Stock Budget (set & approve); managers have it. Click Add a line, pick Whole stock (all categories) or a category, and type a Fixed amount (or a % of expected sales). Choose Warn if over or Needs approval, then click Save budgets. You will see "Budgets saved." To start from last month, use Copy last month or Fill from last 3 months, then check the lines before you save. See the stock budget section.

Why does my % budget say "No sales basis yet"?

A budget set as a % of sales needs food and drink sales to work out the amount. If there are no sales to measure against yet, the line shows a dash for its budget and "No sales basis yet". That is not a warning: the line is not counted as over, and it does not hold up any purchase order. Anything you receive or order for it still shows under Received and Already ordered. It starts working once there are sales. Until then you can set a fixed amount instead. See How to set and watch the stock budget.

Why does the forecast say "Too early to tell"?

In the first 7 days of the month there are not enough days to judge how fast stock is being spent, so the page says "Too early to tell" and does not give a month-end forecast or a pace verdict. From day 8 the page can show a month-end forecast. For a % of sales line in those first 7 days the page uses last month's full sales, so the budget already shows. See How to set and watch the stock budget.

Why will it not let me receive or approve an item with no cost, or receive more than I ordered?

An item with no cost cannot be received by barcode, and a count that adds stock for an item with no cost cannot be approved. Type the cost first. If more arrived than a purchase order says, you can receive it, but you must type an Over-delivery note of at least 5 characters. See How to raise and receive a purchase order.

I can't approve a stock count - it says another count was approved

The page says "This count is out of date: another count was approved after these items were counted ...". Another count that covers some of the same items was approved after you counted them. Approving this one too would add the same stock twice, so it is refused and nothing is posted. Click Reject count (type a reason of at least 8 characters), then Count again. This takes a fresh snapshot of stock. Count the lines again and submit. See How to do a stock count.

The stock count says "406 Not Acceptable" when I save

That happened with very big counts (hundreds of lines). Big counts now save normally. Reload the page, enter or check your readings and click Save readings again. If you still see it, tell your administrator. See How to do a stock count.

How do I order by the crate?

First tell the system the pack. Open Stock → Ingredients, click Edit on the item, fill in Bought in packs? (for example crate) and Units in one pack (for example 24), then Save. On a purchase order, the Add line box then shows Order in: pick the crate or the single unit. Type how many crates, and the price per crate or per bottle. The box shows the total, for example 2 crates = 48 bottles. Stock is always counted in bottles. See How to raise and receive a purchase order, and How to add and change ingredients.

How does the system stop stock theft?

It cannot stop a person, but it makes stock hard to lose without a trace. Every sale takes stock off by recipe. Wastage must be written down with a reason and a name. Stock counts are blind for the person counting, every shortage needs a reason, and a big shortage cannot be approved by the person who counted. Manual adjustments and batch write-offs are for managers only and need a reason. Missing stock on the Stock Count page lists ingredients losing stock that no sale or wastage explains. Every change is saved with who made it. Run a surprise count each week. See How the system protects your stock.

Stock is missing — what do I do?

Open Stock → Stock Reports → Adjustments, set the dates and read every entry for that ingredient. Then check the Usage, Wastage and Stock-In tabs, the recipe for that item, any manual adjustments, and voids and refunds on Orders & Tabs. Then run a fresh spot count with a different person. If you cannot explain the loss, approve the count with the honest reason (for example Theft suspected) so it is written off and recorded. See When stock is short.

What is Missing stock?

Missing stock (last 30 days) is a list on the Stock Count page. It shows up to 20 ingredients that lost stock in the last 30 days through count shortages or manual subtractions, which no sale or wastage entry explains, with the cost lost on the right. If nothing is found it says "No unaccounted shortages detected. All stock movement traces to sales or recorded events." Under it, Stock added by hand (last 30 days) lists stock added by hand, because an addition can hide an earlier loss. It shows where to look, not who is to blame. See How the system protects your stock.

Why are some stock count lines orange or red, and what is "N lines over the alert level"?

A shortage worth at least the minimum value is shaded orange when it reaches the alert at variance share of the expected stock, and red when it reaches the needs approval above share (red needs a second approver). The yellow box above the lines counts them, for example "2 lines over the alert level." Surpluses and small shortages are not shaded. Change the limits in Hotel Settings. See How the system protects your stock.

What is "No stock count approved for N days — start one."? Can I turn it off?

It is the stock count reminder. It shows at the top of Stock Overview and Stock Count when no count has been approved for the number of days you chose. Click start one to begin a count. To change it or switch it off, open Hotel Settings → Hotel details & policies → Restaurant & stock and change Remind to count stock after (days, 0 = off), then click Save Hotel Details. No email is sent. See Setting it up.

What is "Needs review" on the Stock Overview, and what does Dismiss do?

Managers and administrators see Needs review: big count shortages, stock changed by hand, and batches written off, recalled or expired in the last 7 days. Click Look to open the record. Dismiss hides one row and Dismiss all hides them all, but only for you. Other managers still see them and nothing is deleted. See How the system protects your stock.

Why can't I approve my own stock count?

Because a big shortage must be checked by someone other than the person who counted. If a shortage is over both the cost limit and the percentage limit you will see "This count has shortages over the approval threshold (MWK 5,000.00 and 10%). It must be approved by a different manager or admin than the person who counted it." Ask another manager or admin to approve it. Approving or rejecting a count also needs the Admin or Manager role: without it you see "Only admin/manager can approve counts." An opening stock count needs a different approver too, unless an administrator has switched the second-person rule off in Hotel Settings. See Setting it up.

Can I submit and approve a stock count in one step? Why do I need approval from a second person?

Only when the setting Stock count: a second person must approve in Hotel Settings is Off. Then the count button reads Submit & correct stock, and one press submits and approves the count, so stock is corrected at once. With the setting On (the default), the button reads Submit for approval, and a different person must press Approve & correct stock before stock changes. That way the person who counted cannot hide a loss on their own. If a check fails, the count stays Waiting for approval with the reason. See How to do a stock count and Setting it up.

Can I submit a count before every item has been counted?

Yes. Submit saves what you have typed, then sends only the items that have a reading. Type a reading on each item you have counted (0 if the shelf is empty), then click Submit for approval (or Submit & correct stock if the second-person setting is Off). Items left blank are left out of this count: their stock is not changed, and you can count them in a later count, including a later opening count, once they are in stock. You can leave out items on purpose, for example items that are not in stock yet: just leave their boxes blank, and submit only counts the items you typed a reading for. The confirm box says how many items have no reading, and the message afterwards names the items left out. Those names are also kept in the activity log. If no item has a reading, submit is refused with "Nothing to submit: no item has a reading yet." See How to do a stock count.

A receptionist cannot confirm, cancel, or mark a booking paid. Why?

Each booking action needs its own permission in Staff & Access: Check-in Guests, Check-out Guests, Cancel Bookings (cancel and no-show), Add Payments (mark paid), and Edit Bookings (change dates, Confirm Booking, Convert to Confirmed, charges on the guest bill). Changing a room's status needs Housekeeping or Room Maintenance. An administrator ticks them in Permissions. See Example: set up a receptionist.

The Facebook share buttons are greyed out. How do I connect Facebook?

Open Website → Facebook Settings. Add the redirect address shown there to your Facebook app (Facebook Login → Settings → Valid OAuth Redirect URIs), type the App ID and App Secret, click Save and connect with Facebook and approve the three permissions. If it was connected before and stopped, click Reconnect. See How to connect the Facebook Page.

How do I give a guest money back?

Open the payment and click Refund. Choose the amount, the reason and the status (use Pending until the money has actually gone out, then settle it). Mobile money refunds must start as Pending or Processing. See How to refund a payment.

A guest says the invoice link I sent on WhatsApp does not open.

The link is valid for 30 days. It also stops working at once if the booking is cancelled or deleted. The guest sees "This link has expired" and is asked to contact the hotel. Send the invoice again with Send via WhatsApp if the booking is still active. See How to send an invoice or quotation.

Why does a till sale have one receipt number and a separate order number?

Each till sale has one receipt number. The order number is shown separately on the receipt, so the two are not confused. A walk-in sale with no email sends nothing by itself, so use the receipt options on the till. See How to print a kitchen ticket or send a receipt.

Why didn't the guest get a receipt?

A receipt with a PDF is emailed by itself, once, whenever a payment is saved as Paid or Completed (room, conference, gym, event, and till or room-service sales that have a customer email). Read the green message you saw when you saved the payment. It says what happened: "Receipt emailed to the guest." or why not:

  • "Receipt not emailed: guest has no email address - use Email receipt on the payment." Add the address, then open the payment and click Send Receipt.
  • "Receipt not emailed: the guest email address is not valid - correct it..." Fix the spelling, then use Send Receipt.
  • "Receipt not emailed: email sending is not set up yet (SMTP in Email settings)..." Ask an administrator to set up email in Hotel Settings.
  • "Receipt not emailed: payment receipt emails are switched off in Booking Settings..." Tick Active on the Receipt Email tab of Email Templates.
  • "Receipt NOT emailed (the email could not be sent - details are in System Logs)..." Try Send Receipt again; if it keeps failing, tell your manager.

A Pending payment gets no receipt until it is marked Paid or Completed. A receipt is sent only once per payment, so use Send receipt on the booking, or Send Receipt on the payment, to send it again. On the till, an order with no customer email sends nothing by itself. See What the guest is sent, and when.

Why can't I take a payment on this booking?

Because nothing is owed. When the balance is zero there is no payment form. You will see "Nothing is owed on this booking." on the Payments & balance window and on the Add Payment page, and Mark as Paid says "Nothing is owed on this booking. No payment was recorded." If you force it you will see "Nothing is owed on this booking. Use a credit note or refund instead." The same goes for conference, gym and event accounts.

If the guest really paid more than the balance, type the amount they gave you. You will see "That is MWK ... more than owed. The extra will be issued to the guest as a credit note." Tick The guest paid more than owed and save. Without the tick you get "That is more than the balance due. Tick 'The guest paid more than owed' to record it — the extra becomes a credit note." The same tick is on Amount Collected when you create a booking. See What happens when a guest pays too much.

How do I resend a receipt?

Click Send receipt. You find it on the booking page (top of the page and the Invoices box), in the More menu on the Bookings list, in a conference More menu, in a gym or event inquiry window, and on the Invoices page for a fully paid account. If there is one payment you are asked to confirm. If there are several, pick one from the list; the latest is already selected. The receipt goes to the email address on the booking. You can also open the payment and click Send Receipt. See How to send or reprint a receipt.

Why is Send invoice missing?

An invoice asks for money, so it is shown only while a balance is owed. The button reads Send invoice (balance due). If the booking is fully paid it is hidden and Send receipt is shown instead. If you try anyway you will see "This booking is fully paid. Send the receipt instead." On a booking with nothing paid yet, the menu shows Send quotation first; once a payment comes in the quotation goes away. A part-paid booking shows Send invoice (balance due) and then Send receipt. Invoices are also not sent for tentative bookings: send a quotation. See How to send an invoice or quotation.

Quote, invoice or receipt: which one is sent when?

A quotation is the price before the stay: it goes with a front-desk booking that has nothing paid. An invoice asks for money: it is sent by hand (Send invoice (balance due), only while something is owed), or at check-out when the guest still owes. A receipt says money came in: it is sent by itself for every payment. A payment never sends an invoice. At check-out the guest gets a Final Invoice if a balance is owed, or a Stay statement (paid in full) if nothing is owed. When money goes back, the guest gets a refund notice or a credit note. See What the guest is sent, and when.

Why can't I delete this payment?

A payment that has been received cannot be deleted, whatever its status. You will see "Payments that have been received can't be deleted. Use a refund or credit note instead." Money that was received is kept as the record. To undo it, use Refund (or issue a credit note). Only a payment that was never received (pending, cancelled or failed, with no receipt number) can be deleted. A payment made by credit note is undone by voiding the credit note. A cancelled payment cannot be reopened either: record a new payment instead. See How to edit a payment and How to refund a payment.

Where did my credit note money go?

If the guest paid by credit note, the money went back to that same credit note when the booking was cancelled. It is never paid out as cash. The cancel summary says so: "... paid by credit note was returned to credit note CN-... (re-opened, no cash paid out)." Check the note under Money → Credit Notes. If the note was voided or has expired, it cannot be reopened. That share then shows on the booking as credit owed back: refund it, or issue a new credit note. See Money paid by credit note.

Why does a figure say Unavailable, and why do the money-owed totals match?

The money-owed total is one figure. It is the same on the Dashboard (the Owed to us card), Accounting, End of Day, Reports and Invoices. It includes room, conference, gym and event accounts and open unpaid tabs. Unavailable means that figure could not be loaded, so it is not shown as a wrong number. Reload the page. See How to see who still owes money.

A shop sale was voided. Did the goods go back on the shelf?

In shop mode (the restaurant module is off) the screen asks "Put the goods back in stock?". OK puts them back on the shelf. Cancel records them as damaged waste. In a restaurant, stock for food already started is recorded as wastage. See How to cancel or void an order.

Where do I see today's takings and what still needs doing?

Money → End of Day shows the day's Revenue earned (what the hotel earned that day: nights stayed, charges on guest bills, till sales, conferences and events on their event date), Cash collected on its own line, cash to bank, what is still owed and a close score with the items that need attention. See End of Day report.

Rooms, website, settings and other questions

How do I see which rooms are free on a date?

Open Front Desk → Calendar. A room with no booking bar and no colour block on that day is free (available). Use All statuses → Available to narrow it down. See How to use the Calendar.

How do I stop a room being sold (block a room, close dates, stop sales)?

For set nights: Front Desk → Blocked Dates → Block Single Date or Block Date Range, choose Individual Room Level (Specific room) (or Room Type Level for every room of a type), save. Until the room is fixed: Individual Rooms → Status → Maintenance or Out of Order. Existing bookings are not moved. See How to block dates.

Why can't I block a room? (the buttons are greyed out, or "You need the Block Rooms permission...")

Taking a room off sale needs the Block Rooms permission. It covers blocking and unblocking dates on Blocked Dates, and choosing Maintenance or Out of Order on Individual Rooms. Without it the buttons are greyed out and say "Needs the Block Rooms permission", and if you get past that the system answers "You need the Block Rooms permission to do this. Ask a manager to do it or to give you the permission." Managers, Receptionists and Housekeeping have it by default. To give it to someone else, open Staff & Access and tick Block Rooms for them. Maintenance requests still put a room into Maintenance by themselves. See How to block dates.

Where do I see a guest's whole bill and everything done to a booking?

On the Bookings list click More → Full details. The booking page has Folio / Charges (the bill), Payment breakdown, Invoices, Internal notes and the Activity timeline (who did what and when). See The Booking Details page.

Why did a pending booking turn to Expired by itself (it disappeared from Pending)?

A pending booking with no payment lapses after the hours set in Hotel Settings → Hotel details → Unpaid pending bookings lapse after (48 hours unless changed). Its room is released. Tentative holds expire when their time runs out, and unpaid confirmed bookings are only released if you set hours above 0. See Everything the system does by itself.

I switched off a photo and cannot find it to switch it back on.

In the Media Portal use the status filter: Active (the default), Inactive (N) or Active and inactive. Open the item and make it active again. In Gallery you can also drag a photo by its handle, or use Move up and Move down, to change the order. See Media Portal.

What does the system do by itself, and what does it never do by itself?

By itself: expire tentative holds and unpaid pending bookings, send reminder emails (if switched on), create the next recurring maintenance task, take the nightly backup after 01:00, and take stock off when something is sold. Never by itself: send the End of Day report or the daily station reports, close a cashier's shift, or restore a backup. See Everything the system does by itself.

Where can I see who changed something, and when?

For one booking, its Activity timeline. For one order, Order log on the kitchen, bar or coffee screen. For everything (sign-ins, setting changes, refunds, errors) an administrator opens Settings → System Logs. See System Logs.

How do I make children free, or charge a fixed amount per child?

Open Settings → Rooms and click Edit Room. In the Children part, choose % of the room rate or Fixed amount per child per night. Type 0 to make children free. To make only the first few children free in each room, fill in Free children (first N in each room). One room can have its own rule in Front Desk → Individual Rooms; it starts as Same as room type. Rooms stay as they were until you edit them, and bookings already made keep the rule they were made with. See Child charges.

A booking is refused because of too many children. What do I do?

The room has a limit in Most children in one room. You will see a message like "[room name] takes at most 2 children per room." Reduce the children, add adults, add another room or pick a room type that takes more. An administrator or manager can raise the limit in Settings → Rooms (or for one room in Individual Rooms); leave the box blank for no limit. See Child charges.

What does "2 children, first 2 free" mean on a quotation or invoice?

It tells the guest how the children were charged. 2 children, first 2 free means both stay free. 1 child x MWK 10,000 x 7 nights means one child paid MWK 10,000 for each of 7 nights. The quantity on the line is the number of children who pay. Emails can show the same words with the {{child_charge_rule}} tag. See What the guest is sent, and when.

Who is online right now? How do I see who is logged in?

An administrator or manager opens Settings → Staff & Access (or System Logs) and looks at Who's online at the top. It shows Active now or Idle N min, the screen each person has open and when they signed in. It updates every minute. A person drops off after 30 minutes with no activity, or as soon as they log out. Administrators also see the IP address. The panel does not keep anyone signed in. See Who's online.

What does "Also booking right now" on New Booking mean? Two of us are booking at once.

Another member of staff has New Booking open at the same time. The box shows who and since when. When you are both booking the same room type for overlapping nights, their line turns amber and shows the room type and dates instead. Agree who takes the room: if you both pick the last one, the second save is refused with "Someone else was booking the same rooms at that moment. Nothing was saved - please try again." The box never blocks saving. See How to create a booking.

How do I clear the server error log?

An administrator opens Settings → Cache and finds Error logs. Click Clear beside a file, or Clear all error logs. The file is emptied, not deleted, and what was in it cannot be brought back. The page never shows what is inside. Every clear is recorded with who did it. See Cache.

What do the number cards, tabs and chips at the top of a page do?

They filter the list below them. Click a card or tab to see only those rows, and the page takes you straight to the filtered list. Click the same card again, All or Clear to see everything. See Lists, number cards, tabs and times.

The times look wrong (wrong time or time zone).

Times on a booking's page, on the till and on an order receipt are shown in your own device's time zone. Fix the clock and time zone on the phone or computer. The hotel's own time zone is fixed at installation. See Lists, number cards, tabs and times.

I lost an API key. Can I see it again?

No. A key is shown once, when it is created or regenerated, with the words "copy this key now". Click Regenerate on the key in Settings → API Keys and send the new key to the client. The old key stops working. See API Keys.

Can a receptionist take a payment and print a receipt?

Yes. The normal Receptionist role has Add Payments and Receipts. POS Accounting is for the accountant only, and is granted to one person at a time. See Example: set up a receptionist.

What does each permission let a person do?

Every permission in the Permissions window, in plain words, with the roles that have it by default: Every permission, in plain words. To change one person's access click Permissions on their row in Settings → Staff & Access.

How do I pin a page to the top of the menu (favourites)?

Click the star at the right of any menu item. It moves into the Favorites group at the top. Click the star again to remove it. See The sidebar menu.

Where do messages from the website contact form go?

Into Website → Contact Inquiries as New / Unread, and an email goes to the hotel's reservations address. Click Reply via Email to answer. The guest is told to expect a reply within 24 hours. See The Contact form.

A guest left a review but it is not on the website.

New reviews wait for approval. Open Website → Reviews → Pending and click Approve & publish. See How a guest leaves a review.

How do I change the menu on the website?

The website Restaurant page and the printable menu read straight from Restaurant & Bar → Menu. Add or change the item there and mark it available; an item marked unavailable (86) is hidden from the website. See The Restaurant page and the printable menu.

What does the guest see on the website, and where does their form land?

Booking, booking look-up, rooms, restaurant, events, conference, gym, contact and reviews: The guest website lists each page, what the guest does and which admin page handles it.

I cancelled a gym member. What happens to their unpaid bill?

Cancelling a member voids the unpaid part of their gym bills and releases their future bookings and classes. Money already paid stays as credit owed back: refund it or issue a credit note. Suspending or expiring a member leaves their bills alone. See How to edit a member, renew, suspend or cancel.

A guest's booking was cancelled or deleted after the kitchen had started their room-service food. Who pays (food kept, not eaten)?

Food nobody had started is cancelled and its stock goes back. Food already started or served stays on the guest's bill (the food was made), and the booking's refund is reduced by that amount. Only a part-paid order stops the cancel, until you settle or refund it in Orders & Tabs. See How to cancel a booking and Room service.

Does the system send our sales or VAT to MRA?

No. The system has no connection to MRA. It does not report, submit or fiscalise sales, receipts or VAT. It keeps the VAT Register and the quarterly VAT totals (Money → Reports → VAT Register) that you or your accountant use to file the VAT return with MRA separately. VAT collected is held on behalf of MRA and is not the hotel's income. If MRA requires fiscal receipts from your till, that is done outside this system. See Accounting dashboard and VAT.

The website shows old content, text or prices after I changed them.

The browser or the website kept an old copy. First reload the page (hold Shift and click reload). If it is still old, an administrator opens Settings → Cache, ticks All caches and clicks Clear Selected Caches. Under Total size, Last cleared shows when this was last done. Clearing the cache never deletes hotel data. See Cache.

The kitchen, bar or coffee screen does not beep when a new ticket comes in.

Check that Sound on the screen is switched on, turn the device volume up, and make sure the browser tab is not muted. The board still updates by itself without sound. See Kitchen Display. On the till, a ready alert also plays a sound: see How you know an order is ready.

How does a waiter know an order is ready?

The till shows a small notice under the top bar, such as "Kitchen: all 3 items ready", plays a sound and (on a tablet or phone) vibrates. It goes only to the person who placed the order. Tap it to open the order. See How you know an order is ready.

How do I take a backup before an update?

An administrator opens Settings → Backups and clicks Run Backup Now. It takes 5 to 30 seconds. You will see "Backup saved (N KB). Your hotel data is protected up to this moment." Then click Download to keep a copy off the server. See Backups.
