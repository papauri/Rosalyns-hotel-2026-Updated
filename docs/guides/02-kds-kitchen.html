Kitchen Display (KDS)

The Kitchen Display is the screen the kitchen uses to see food orders sent from the till, work through them, and mark them ready and served. It also covers the Station Reports page and the Station Hours page, which apply to the Kitchen, Bar and Coffee Bar screens.

Who uses it
Chefs and kitchen staff (screen). Managers (reports and hours).
Where to find it
Admin menu → Restaurant & Bar → Kitchen (KDS) (/admin/kds.php). It opens in a new browser tab. Reports: Admin menu → Advanced → Station Reports (/admin/kds-report.php). Hours: Admin menu → Advanced → Station Hours (/admin/station-settings.php).
Permission needed
Kitchen Display (KDS) for the screen. Station Reports (KDS/BDS/CDS) for reports. Ingredients & Recipes for Station Hours.
Only shown when
The Point of Sale and Kitchen Display (KDS) modules are switched on in Settings → Modules. Station Reports and Station Hours also need the Restaurant Page module.

What is on the screen

Top bar

  • KDS and the hotel name at the left.
  • Open — tickets still on the board. Ready — tickets that are ready. Served shift — tickets served in this business day. Hours — the station's opening hours. An Open or Closed label shows if the station is inside its hours.
  • Filter buttons: All, New, Cooking, Ready.
  • The clock, FOH chat, Sound (mute or unmute the chime), Settings (sound settings), Served, Help (shows hints on each button), a guide link (KDS Guide), Daily Report (only with the Station Reports (KDS/BDS/CDS) permission), Dashboard (only with the Dashboard permission, so kitchen-only staff stay on this screen) and Logout.
  • On small screens the same controls are in a Menu panel, which also has Enter Fullscreen.

Board — one card per ticket. The card shows:

  • The place: Table 5, the order type (for example WALK IN) or the room.
  • A timer showing how long the ticket has waited since it was sent, the order reference, and who took the order.
  • The order note, if there is one.
  • The lines: quantity, item name, any note for that line, and a small tag showing the next step. Lines that belong to another station (for example drinks on a food ticket) are shown with that station's name. Under the lines a note such as 2 items at other stations tells you the other stations are still working.
  • Buttons along the bottom (see below).

Bottom panels

  • FOH Notes — notes from the front of house that have not been dismissed. It says "No active notes." when there are none.
  • All Day — a running total of items still waiting or being cooked across all tickets. It says "No items working." when empty.
  • Online Flow — the pause and wait-time setting (see Online Flow).

The board updates by itself. When new tickets or notes arrive a chime plays (if Sound is on). If the station is outside its hours a banner reads KDS is currently closed with the hours.

The board only lists tickets sent during the current business day that still have at least one line for the kitchen that is not served and not cancelled. Which items come here is decided on the Menu page by the item's Station (see the POS guide).

How a ticket moves through the kitchen

Every line goes through these steps. The tag on a line shows the step that tapping will do next.

Line isTag showsTap does
Pending (just arrived)StartMarks it as being cooked.
CookingReadyMarks it ready for pickup. The ingredients for the recipe come off stock now.
ReadyCollectMarks that the runner is collecting it. The cashier is sent a "ready for collection" note.
CollectingServeMarks it as delivered to the guest.
Served or cancellednone (struck through / CANCELED)Nothing; the line is finished.

When every kitchen line on an order is ready, collecting or served, the cashier's till shows "Kitchen: order ORD-... (Table 5) is ready for collection." This is sent once per order and station.

A ticket's status on the filter buttons is: New (nothing started), Cooking (at least one line started), Ready (everything for the station is ready).

How to work a ticket

  1. Watch the board for a new ticket. Its timer starts as soon as the cashier sends it.
  2. To cook the whole ticket at once, click Start all. This appears only while the ticket still has pending lines for your station. Or tap each line to start it.
  3. When a dish is plated, tap its line (tag Ready). If the ingredients are not in stock you see "Stock check stopped this action: Chicken short by 2.500 kg. Receive stock, adjust the recipe, or 86 the item before marking it ready." and the line does not move. Tell your manager to receive stock, or 86 the item.
  4. When the runner takes it, tap the line again (tag Collect), and when it reaches the guest tap it once more (tag Serve).
  5. You can tap lines in any order. A line can also be served directly from Pending or Cooking; the stock still comes off at that moment.

The timer changes after 5 minutes (a warning) and again after 10 minutes (late). For a ticket that is already ready and waiting, the same 5 and 10 minute marks apply. A ticket that has waited more than 12 minutes without being ready is marked urgent on the board.

Small icons on each of your lines: Recipe card (shows the recipe, ingredients and portion for that line) and 86 this item (see below; not shown once the line is served or cancelled). The eye icon on the ticket, View whole order, shows every line across all stations. The stream icon, Order log, shows the full history of the order.

How to bump a whole ticket

  1. Click Bump at the bottom of the ticket. It turns into the main button once all your lines are ready.
  2. Every remaining line for your station is marked served and the ticket clears from your board. The ingredients for any line that had not yet been deducted come off stock now.

If stock is short the bump is refused with the same "Stock check stopped this action ..." message and nothing changes. Bump is greyed out if the ticket has no lines for your station.

How to mark a ticket as a rush

  1. Click Rush on the ticket. You see "Marked as RUSH — FOH notified". The ticket is flagged as a rush and moves to the top of the board.
  2. To clear it click Un-rush. You see "Rush cleared".

How to cancel a ticket before cooking starts

The cross-in-a-circle button (Cancel before prep) only appears while no line on the order has been started at any station.

  1. Click the button. The window Cancel Order Before Prep says "Choose a reason — FOH will be notified immediately."
  2. Click one reason: Customer cancelled, Wrong order sent, Out of ingredient, Quality issue, Test / training order or Other. You can add extra words in the box Additional details or custom reason….
  3. Click Confirm Cancel (or Keep to leave the ticket alone). If the reason is shorter than 8 characters you see "Choose a reason before confirming".
  4. You see "Order cancelled — FOH notified". Stock is restored and, for a room-service order, the room charge is removed.

If the order has already been paid, or part of it paid, you see "Order ... has payment recorded against it - it cannot be cancelled. A manager must void it (this writes the reversal)." If cooking has started you see "Cannot cancel: preparation has already started".

How to 86 an item on a ticket

Use this when an ingredient has run out or a dish cannot be made for this order.

  1. Click 86 this item (the small icon on the line). The window 86 Item reads "86 (item name)? This removes it from the order and sends an urgent alert to FOH."
  2. Click 86 this item to confirm, or Keep. You see "Item 86'd — FOH notified".

What the system does:

  • The line is cancelled and the order total comes down by that line (including its share of any discount and tax).
  • If the line had not been started, its stock is put back. If it had been started or was ready, the loss is recorded as wastage.
  • If the order was already paid, a pending refund is raised for the cashier to complete.
  • For a room-service order the matching room charge is removed.
  • If every line on an unpaid tab is 86'd, the tab is cancelled.
  • The cashier who opened the order gets an urgent message telling them to inform the guest and offer an alternative.

This does not switch the dish off for future orders. Ask the till to 86 the menu item (see the POS guide) so it cannot be ordered again.

How to talk to the front of house

  1. On a ticket click the speech-bubble icon. A number shows how many notes are on that ticket. The icon turns red when there is a note you have not opened.
  2. To reply to a note, click one of the quick replies (On it!, 5 minutes, 10 minutes, Ready soon, Out of stock, Need clarification) or type your own in Custom reply… and click the send arrow. You see "Reply sent to FOH".
  3. To start a note yourself, type in Send a note to FOH about this ticket… (up to 240 characters) and press the send arrow. You see "Sent to FOH".
  4. To dismiss a note click the small cross on it.
  5. All messages with the till for the last 6 hours are in FOH chat (top bar, or the chat bubble at the right edge of the screen).

An urgent note from the till covers the middle of the board with an alert. Click Got it to dismiss it, or Open chat to read and reply. If there are several, Close all — notes stay in the chat closes them all; the notes stay in the chat.

How to look at served tickets

  1. Click Served in the top bar. The window Served Menu opens.
  2. Choose Current Shift, Yesterday or After Hours.
  3. The window shows Orders served, Items served, Revenue and Avg prep, then the tickets with guests, who took the order, times, lines and the station log.

How to use Online Flow (pause and wait time)

The Online Flow panel at the bottom of the screen is headed Online Flow (saved note). It shows either Marked live with "Wait time saved: 20 min", or Marked paused with the wait time saved.

  1. To pause, click Pause. You see "Pause saved. This is a note on this screen only; it does not stop orders." The button changes to Go Live.
  2. To resume, click Go Live. You see "Marked live and wait time saved (note only; orders are not affected)."
  3. To change the wait time, type minutes (from 5 to 180, in steps of 5) in the box and click Save.

This is a saved note only. It does not stop orders, and it is not shown at the till: staff can still send tickets while the panel says Marked paused.

How to set the station hours

Station hours set the "business day" for each screen. They decide which tickets a board shows, what counts in Served shift, the windows in reports and shift closes, and the "station closed" warning at the till. Tickets are never refused when a station is closed.

  1. Open Admin menu → Advanced → Station Hours. The page is titled Station Hours.
  2. For each station (Kitchen, Bar, Coffee Bar) set Opens and Closes as times. Each card shows Open now or Closed now, the Current and Yesterday windows, and Close day: Next calendar day when a station closes after midnight.
  3. Click Save Station Hours. You will see "Station hours updated."

If hours have never been saved the system uses these: Kitchen 06:00 to 23:00, Bar 11:00 to 03:00 (the next morning), Coffee Bar 06:00 to 22:00. A time that is not valid gives "Use 24-hour HH:MM format for every station."

How to read and send the daily station report

  1. Click Daily Report on the screen, or open Admin menu → Advanced → Station Reports.
  2. Choose the Station (All Stations, Kitchen, Bar or Coffee Bar) and the Date, then click Apply. Staff with the Chef, Bar Staff or Coffee Bar Staff role are fixed to their own station.
  3. The report shows Items Served, Total Qty, Revenue, Avg Prep Time (with the shortest and longest), Voided / Cancelled, a By station table, Top items by revenue, and an All tickets table with Ref, Type, Table / Guest, Item, Qty, Total, Stn, Status, Fired, Started, Ready, Served and Prep.
  4. Click CSV to download it, Print to print it, or Email.
  5. For Email type the Recipient email in the window Email station report and click Send. You will see "Report emailed to name@example.com."

If nothing was served that day it says "No tickets for the selected day & station."

Rules the system enforces

  • You only act on lines that belong to your station. A line for another station is shown for information and cannot be tapped.
  • Stock for a line comes off when it is marked Ready, Served or bumped, never earlier. If ingredients are short, the action is refused.
  • A ticket cannot be cancelled once any line has started, or once any payment has been recorded.
  • An order that is already refunded, voided or cancelled cannot have an item 86'd. A served line cannot be 86'd.
  • Cancel needs a reason of at least 8 characters.
  • The cashier cannot settle a tab while kitchen food is unserved, unless a manager forces it (see the POS guide). Bar and coffee drinks do not block settlement.
  • Notes to the front of house are limited to 240 characters on a ticket and 255 characters overall.
