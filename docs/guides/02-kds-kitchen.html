KDS — Kitchen Display

A live ticket board for the kitchen. No printers, no paper — chefs work directly off the screen.

URL /admin/kds.php Role chef · manager · admin Read time 4 min

Layout

  • Tickets fly in from the right when fired by the till.
  • Cards turn amber after 5 min, red after 10 min — keeps the kitchen on time.
  • A soft chime plays when a new ticket lands. Tap Sound to mute, or open Sound Settings to choose alert tones and volume for the station screen.
  • Each ticket shows FOH/orderer, guest/service type, line count, item count, fired time and current status so the kitchen can identify who sent it without leaving the board.

Step-by-step

  1. A new ticket arrives — it appears on the board and chimes.
  2. Tap Start on the ticket header (or on individual lines). The card border turns blue.
  3. As you cook each item, tap Ready () on that line. This stamps the ready time and deducts the ingredients from stock at this moment.
  4. If recipe stock is short, the board stops the action and shows a friendly stock dialog. Receive stock, adjust the recipe, or 86 the item before trying Ready/Bump again.
  5. Tap Collect () to mark the item ready for collection by the runner. The item moves to collection status and appears orange on the board.
  6. Once the runner delivers the item, tap Serve () or Bump the whole ticket. The ticket disappears from the active board.
Stock deducts at Ready, not at fire. If an order is cancelled before you tap Ready on any item, no stock is lost. This ensures physical prep and stock usage stay in sync.

Item action buttons (per line)

Button When to use Stock effect
Start When you physically pick up the item to cook. None.
Ready Plate/glass is up and ready to leave the pass. Deducts ingredients from stock.
Collect Runner has taken the item for delivery. None (already deducted at Ready).
Serve Individual item confirmed delivered to guest. None.
Bump All items on ticket complete — clears the board. Deducts any items that were bumped without going through Ready first.

Ticket timer & age colours

  • Each ticket shows elapsed time since it was fired. The format is compact: just fired → 45 sec → 3m 12s → 12 min → 1h 04m.
  • The sub-label below the timer gives context: On time — fired 14:32 · Watch closely — fired 14:20 · Late — fired 14:05 · Overdue — fired 13:50 · check with FOH. "Overdue" means the ticket has been on the board over one hour without being bumped — verify with Front of House.
  • Card border colour escalates: normal → amber at 5 min → red at 10 min → pulsing red "urgent" glow at 12 min.
  • All times are shown in Malawi local time (UTC+2) — the timers are accurate to the second.

RUSH priority

  • A RUSH ticket has a ⚡ RUSH badge in the ticket header and a red left border.
  • RUSH tickets sort to the top of the board, above all other orders.
  • When a ticket is marked Rush, an URGENT inbox message arrives in the bottom bar so every station knows immediately.
  • The cashier on POS (or any admin) can toggle Rush from the My Orders tracker — so expect to see priority change mid-service.
  • Rush can be set or cleared from the Rush button in the ticket footer on the station screen.
  • When Rush is cleared, stations get a second URGENT message confirming the priority has been removed.
Any station (KDS, BDS, CDS) or POS cashier can toggle Rush. The last person to tap wins. Coordinate with FOH before clearing a Rush set by the cashier.

FOH notes & station messages

  • The bottom bar has three panels: FOH Messages, All-Day count, and Station Control.
  • Messages from POS (order notes updated by cashier) appear as URGENT items with a red border and pulse animation.
  • You can reply to any message from the bottom bar — your reply goes back to the POS cashier's inbox.
  • You can also send a new note from inside a ticket card using the note composer at the bottom of the card.
  • POS receives station replies, ready alerts, and My Orders status updates within about 1 second. Ready popups include the first few ordered items and stay visible for 2 minutes unless the cashier closes them.
  • Messages expire after 6 hours or can be dismissed by tapping the ✕ on each row.

Filters

Filter Shows

New Just arrived, not started.
Cooking At least one item started or in collection.

Order Lifecycle Log (ticket footer)

Every KDS ticket card has a Log button in the footer alongside Rush and Bump. Tapping it opens /admin/order-lifecycle.php in a new tab — a full audit trail showing:

  • The exact second the order was placed, fired, each item marked Ready, collected, and served.
  • Every stock deduction with ingredient name, quantity, and unit cost.
  • KDS events with actor name and IP (who tapped what and when).
  • A linked payment row if payment has been taken, and any void with reason and actor.
  • Cancel or Void action buttons for admin and manager roles (when applicable).
The Log button is visible to all station roles but the Cancel and Void action buttons inside the lifecycle page are restricted — Cancel requires admin/manager, Void requires admin only.

Recall a ticket (mistake)

  1. Tap Served in the top bar (opens the Served Menu).
  2. Find the ticket, tap Recall (available within 10 minutes of bump).
  3. The ticket reappears on the board. Any stock that was deducted for Ready/Collected items is automatically restored so you can re-prep and re-deduct correctly.
Recall is only available within 10 minutes of bumping the ticket. After that, ask a manager to void the order from Stock Orders.

Mobile / tablet

  • A burger menu in the top-right opens a drawer with all controls (filters, sound toggle, daily report, sign-out).
  • Long-press any button to read its tooltip.
  • The board reflows to a single column at narrow widths.
  • Fullscreen kiosk mode — a banner at the bottom of the screen says "Tap anywhere to go fullscreen" on every load. Tap anywhere and the browser hides its tabs, address bar and all UI chrome. If you accidentally exit fullscreen (e.g. back-swipe on Android), the banner reappears and the next tap re-enters it automatically. This ensures the board uses the full screen on every device.

Daily report

Top bar → Daily Report → opens an end-of-day summary. Includes: tickets served, average prep time, item counts, optional CSV export and email delivery.

Tips

Always tap Ready per item as you complete them — this is how stock gets deducted. Bump when everything is done and collected.
  • If the kitchen network drops, the board still works; orders queue locally.
  • Press ? to peek at every button label.
  • The board polls every 2 seconds — RUSH alerts and note changes from POS arrive within 2 seconds of being sent.
  • New tickets animate in smoothly (fade-up). If you see a flash of old cards on hard-reload, that means the screen was idle overnight and the business-window filter has already cleared them — normal behaviour.
  • Card sizes are uniform — short tickets stretch to match their neighbours in the same row so the grid always looks clean and nothing feels like a squeeze.
  • Action buttons show a spinner while the server records the change, preventing double taps on Start, Ready, Collect, Serve, Rush, 86, and Bump.
