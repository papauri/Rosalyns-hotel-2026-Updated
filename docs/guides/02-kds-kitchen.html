KDS — Kitchen Display

A live ticket board for the kitchen. No printers, no paper — chefs work directly off the screen.

URL /admin/kds.php Role chef · manager · admin Read time 4 min

  • Tickets fade in when fired by the till.
  • The board uses the same Japandi palette as the admin dashboard — warm paper background, off-white cards, muted clay and sage accents. Status is carried by a coloured edge and a small label, not by flooding the whole card, so a busy pass stays readable.
  • Cards warm to amber after 5 min and red after 10 min — keeps the kitchen on time.
  • A soft chime plays when a new ticket lands. Tap Sound to mute, or open Sound Settings to choose alert tones and volume for the station screen.
  • Each ticket shows FOH/orderer, guest/service type, line count, item count, fired time and current status so the kitchen can identify who sent it without leaving the board.
  • Everything scales continuously with the screen — the same board is legible on a 10″ counter tablet and on a 70″ wall display, with no setting to change. Type, cards and the dock all grow together; on a large screen you simply get more columns of larger tickets.

Step-by-step

  1. A new ticket arrives — it appears on the board and chimes.
  2. Tap the line itself when you pick the item up. There are no per-line button rows any more: the whole line is the control, and each tap moves it one step forward. The label on the right of the line always tells you what the next tap will do.
  3. Tap again when the plate is up — that is Ready, and it is the moment the ingredients come off stock.
  4. If recipe stock is short, the board stops the action and shows a friendly stock dialog. Receive stock, adjust the recipe, or 86 the item before trying again.
  5. Tap once more for Collect when the runner takes it, and once more for Serve when it reaches the guest. A finished line is struck through and stops responding to taps.
  6. When every line is done, tap Bump in the ticket footer. The ticket leaves the active board.
Stock deducts at Ready, not at fire. If an order is cancelled before you tap a line up to Ready, no stock is lost. This keeps physical prep and stock usage in sync.

Tapping a line: the four steps

Every ticket line walks the same path. You never choose an action from a row of buttons — you just tap the line, and the label on its right edge tells you where the next tap takes it.

Line shows Tap it when Stock effect
Start You physically pick the item up to cook it. None.
Ready Plate/glass is up and ready to leave the pass. Deducts ingredients from stock.
Collect The runner has taken the item for delivery. None (already deducted at Ready).
Serve That single line is confirmed delivered to the guest. None.
struck through Nothing — the line is finished and no longer tappable. None.
Lines belonging to another station are shown for context with a Kitchen / Bar / Coffee Bar badge, greyed out and not tappable. You can see what the bar is doing without being able to touch their work.

The ticket footer

Control What it does
Start all Starts every pending line at once, for when you fire the whole ticket together. Only shown while some line is still pending.
Bump Marks the ticket delivered and clears it from the board. Deducts stock for anything not already taken at Ready. Recallable for 10 minutes.
Rush / Un-rush Toggles priority. Sorts the ticket to the top and alerts every station.
Notes Opens the FOH message thread for this ticket, with a reply box. The badge counts messages; an indicator marks unread ones.
Whole order Shows every line on the order across all three stations — useful for timing your prep with the bar.
Log Opens the full order lifecycle over the board (see below).
Cancel Only appears while all lines are still pending. Cancels the order outright and restores stock.

Each line also carries two small icons on its right: Recipe for portion and prep guidance, and 86 to pull the item and alert FOH. These act on the single line and do not advance it.

Ticket timer & age colours

  • Each ticket shows elapsed time since it was fired. The format is compact: just fired → 45 sec → 3m 12s → 12 min → 1h 04m.
  • The sub-label below the timer gives context: On time — fired 14:32 · Watch closely — fired 14:20 · Late — fired 14:05 · Overdue — fired 13:50 · check with FOH. "Overdue" means the ticket has been on the board over one hour without being bumped — verify with Front of House.
  • Card border colour escalates: normal → amber at 5 min → red at 10 min → pulsing red "urgent" glow at 12 min.
  • All times are shown in Malawi local time (UTC+2) — the timers are accurate to the second.

RUSH priority

  • A RUSH ticket has a ⚡ RUSH badge in the ticket header and a red left border.
  • RUSH tickets sort to the top of the board, above all other orders.
  • When a ticket is marked Rush, an URGENT inbox message arrives in the bottom bar so every station knows immediately.
  • The cashier on POS (or any admin) can toggle Rush from the My Orders tracker — so expect to see priority change mid-service.
  • Rush can be set or cleared from the Rush button in the ticket footer on the station screen.
  • When Rush is cleared, stations get a second URGENT message confirming the priority has been removed.
Any station (KDS, BDS, CDS) or POS cashier can toggle Rush. The last person to tap wins. Coordinate with FOH before clearing a Rush set by the cashier.

FOH notes & station messages

  • The station dock along the bottom has three panels: FOH Notes, All Day (running item counts) and Online Flow. The dock is a fixed band that scrolls internally — it never pushes the board off-screen or slides under the taskbar, however many notes arrive.
  • Messages from POS (order notes updated by cashier) appear as URGENT items with a red border and pulse animation.
  • You can reply to any message from the dock — your reply goes back to the POS cashier's inbox.
  • You can also open a ticket's own thread with the button in its footer and reply in place, which keeps the conversation attached to that order.
  • If a note has been dismissed or the order has moved on, a reply is refused with a clear message rather than silently disappearing — so a "sent" note on the pass really did reach the till.
  • POS receives station replies, ready alerts, and My Orders status updates within about 1 second. Ready popups include the first few ordered items and stay visible for 2 minutes unless the cashier closes them.
  • Messages expire after 6 hours or can be dismissed by tapping the ✕ on each row.

Order Lifecycle Log (ticket footer)

Every KDS ticket card has a Log () button in the footer alongside Rush and Bump. It opens the full audit trail (/admin/order-lifecycle.php) in a panel over the board — the station screen stays exactly where it was behind it. Close it with the ✕ or the Esc key and you are back on the pass.

The log shows:

  • The exact second the order was placed, fired, each item marked Ready, collected, and served.
  • Every stock deduction with ingredient name, quantity, and unit cost.
  • KDS events with actor name and IP (who tapped what and when).
  • A linked payment row if payment has been taken, and any void with reason and actor.
  • Cancel or Void action buttons for admin and manager roles (when applicable).

Each timing row now names both ends of the interval it measures — Placed → fired, Fired → ready, Ready → served, Placed → paid, Total cycle — so there is no guessing which two stamps a duration is the gap between. If a ticket has not been fired or readied yet, the log says so instead of showing an empty timing.

The Log button is visible to all station roles but the Cancel and Void action buttons inside the lifecycle page are restricted — Cancel requires admin/manager, Void requires admin only.

Recall a ticket (mistake)

  1. Tap Served in the top bar (opens the Served Menu).
  2. Find the ticket, tap Recall (available within 10 minutes of bump).
  3. The ticket reappears on the board. Any stock that was deducted for Ready/Collected items is automatically restored so you can re-prep and re-deduct correctly.
Recall is only available within 10 minutes of bumping the ticket. After that, ask a manager to void the order from Stock Orders.

Mobile / tablet

  • A burger menu in the top-right opens a drawer with all controls (filters, sound toggle, daily report, sign-out).
  • Long-press any button to read its tooltip.
  • The board reflows to a single column at narrow widths.
  • Fullscreen kiosk mode — a banner at the bottom of the screen says "Tap anywhere to go fullscreen" on every load. Tap anywhere and the browser hides its tabs, address bar and all UI chrome. If you accidentally exit fullscreen (e.g. back-swipe on Android), the banner reappears and the next tap re-enters it automatically. This ensures the board uses the full screen on every device.

Daily report

Top bar → Daily Report → opens an end-of-day summary. Includes: tickets served, average prep time, item counts, optional CSV export and email delivery.

Tips

Always tap each line up to Ready as you complete it — this is how stock gets deducted. Bump when everything is done and collected.
  • If the kitchen network drops, the board still works; orders queue locally.
  • Press ? to peek at every button label.
  • The board polls every 2 seconds — RUSH alerts and note changes from POS arrive within 2 seconds of being sent.
  • New tickets animate in smoothly (fade-up). If you see a flash of old cards on hard-reload, that means the screen was idle overnight and the business-window filter has already cleared them — normal behaviour.
  • Card sizes are uniform — short tickets stretch to match their neighbours in the same row so the grid always looks clean and nothing feels like a squeeze.
  • A tapped line or button shows a spinner while the server records the change, so a double tap cannot double-advance an item or bump a ticket twice.
  • The elapsed timers count continuously on every card. If you ever see them frozen, reload the board — but this is no longer expected behaviour.
