The Guest Website

What guests see and do on the hotel website, and where in the admin each page is looked after. Use this guide when a guest phones and says "I filled in the form" or "I cannot find the menu", so you know what they are looking at and where their message or booking lands.

Who uses it
Receptionist, Manager, Administrator, restaurant manager
Where to find it
On the hotel's public website: Book Your Stay, Check Booking Status, the room pages, Restaurant and Menu, Events, Conference, Gym and the gym schedule, Contact Us, Leave a review, and the website FAQ. The table below shows where each one lands in the admin.
Permission needed
None to read this guide. Each admin page named below has its own permission.

The Restaurant page and the printable menu

The Restaurant page shows the food, coffee and bar menus. It reads them straight from Restaurant & Bar → Menu (called Products when the restaurant is switched off), so there is nothing to type twice. Needs the Menu permission to change.

  1. Open Restaurant & Bar → Menu.
  2. Add or change the dish or drink, its price, its category and its description. Make sure it is marked available.
  3. Open the Restaurant page on the website and reload it. The item is there. An item marked unavailable (86) is hidden from the guest menu.
  • Printable menu. The guest Menu page shows the whole menu on one page with a Save as PDF button and a Back to Restaurant link. It is updated every time the Menu changes, and shows an "Updated" date. The Restaurant page also shows a QR code that opens it.
  • Your own PDF. If the hotel has its own menu file, paste its link in Settings → Hotel Settings → Hotel details → Menu PDF link (optional). Leave it empty to use the built-in menu page.
  • The photos and videos on the Restaurant page are managed in the Media Portal (see the Admin reference).

If the Menu has no available items, the menu list on the page stays empty. Money and stock are not touched by the website menu: it only shows prices, and orders are taken on the till.

The Contact form

Guests fill in Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number (optional), Subject and Message, tick the consent box and send.

  1. The guest sees "Thank you for reaching out. Our team will get back to you within 24 hours." and gets an email with a reference.
  2. The message appears in Website → Contact Inquiries as New / Unread, and an email goes to the hotel's reservations address (or the main hotel address if there is none).
  3. Open it, click Reply via Email, write the answer and click Send Reply. See Contact Inquiries.

Needs the Contact Inquiries permission. Nothing to do with money or stock. If the admin email does not arrive, the message is still saved: check Contact Inquiries instead of waiting for the email.

How a guest leaves a review

Guests use the review form on the website: they give their name and email, choose what they are reviewing (room, restaurant, conference, gym or service), give star ratings and write a title and comment.

  1. The guest sees "Thank You! Your review has been successfully submitted and is pending moderation."
  2. The review waits in Website → Reviews on the Pending tab. Nothing shows on the website yet.
  3. Click Approve & publish to show it, or Reject to keep it off. Click Reply to answer it in public.

Needs the Reviews permission (Managers and Receptionists have it). The automatic Post-stay review requests email, which asks guests to leave a review after check-out, is off until you switch it on in Automated Emails. See Reviews.

Room pages, Guest Services and the website FAQ

  • Room pages. Each room type has a page with photos, facilities, policies and a booking button. Change the text and photos in Settings → Rooms (Room prices, rate plans and packages) and the pictures in the Gallery.
  • Guest Services. A hub page that links to the gym, conference, restaurant, events and booking pages. It only lists the services that are switched on.
  • The website FAQ page. Its questions are fixed text. The hotel name, phone, email, check-in and check-out times and the service links are filled in from your settings (Hotel Settings and Footer), so change those, not the page. A service that is switched off is not linked.
  • Hotel name, phone, email, address, social links and opening hours on every page come from Website → Footer.

Rules the system enforces

  • Guests cannot see the admin, staff names, other guests, or anything marked unavailable or switched off.
  • Every guest form is protected: too many tries in a few minutes show "Too many submissions. Please wait a few minutes before trying again." (reviews) or the booking and look-up equivalents. Wait and try again.
  • A review is never shown until a member of staff approves it.
  • A booking from the website is Pending (or Tentative) until staff confirm it; a pending booking with no payment lapses after the number of hours set in Hotel details (48 by default).
