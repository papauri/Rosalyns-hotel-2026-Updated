POS Till, Menu and Tables

The POS Till is the touchscreen screen where staff take food and drink orders, send them to the Kitchen, Bar and Coffee Bar screens, take payment, charge orders to a guest's room, and close their shift. This guide also covers the Menu page (what the till sells) and the Tables page (the dine-in tables the till offers).

Who uses it
Waiters, cashiers and bar staff (till). Managers (refunds, voids, 86, shift reports). Anyone who maintains the menu or tables.
Where to find it
Admin menu → Restaurant & Bar → POS Till (/admin/pos.php). It opens in a new browser tab. The same group also has Menu (/admin/menu-management.php), Orders & Tabs and Tables (/admin/restaurant-tables.php).
Permission needed
POS Till (Touch) for the till. Menu for the Menu page. Ingredients & Recipes for the Tables page. Extra till powers are listed in the table "Who can do what" below.
Only shown when
The POS & Stations module is switched on in Settings → Modules. The Tables page also needs the Stock Management and Restaurant Page modules.

What is on the screen

Top bar

  • Recent — your last 10 orders in the current business day, with the amount, payment method, time and status. Click an order to open its receipt. A Refund button shows next to paid orders if you have the POS Refunds permission.
  • Tabs — the open (unpaid) tabs. The number on the button is how many are open.
  • Note — send a short note to the Kitchen, Bar or Coffee Bar.
  • My orders — every order you rang up today, with kitchen and payment status.
  • Messages — notes from the stations and their replies to yours.
  • More — a menu with: Close shift (Z-report); Opening float (only if you have POS Opening Float); Stations (only with POS Station Overview); 86 mode (only with POS Quick-86 Items); All orders (opens Orders & Tabs, only if you can open that page); Help mode; Sound settings; POS guide (opens this guide); Admin dashboard (only if you have the Dashboard permission).
  • Sign out.

Shift figures (second row): Orders is always shown. Revenue, Cash, Mobile and Card are shown only to people with the POS Accounting permission.

Menu side

  • Two buttons, Restaurant and Room Service, choose which items are shown. Choosing Room Service also sets the order type to Room.
  • A category list (All plus one entry per menu category), a Search menu… box, and a tile for each item.
  • Each tile shows the price and a stock line: N avail, N left (5 or fewer), Out of stock, Untracked (the item has no recipe, so no stock is counted) or 86'd (only people with the 86 permission see 86'd items).

Current order side

  • Order type buttons: Walk-in, Dine-in, Takeaway, Room.
  • For Dine-in a Select table... list appears. For Room a Select checked-in room... list appears.
  • Guest name (optional) and Covers (optional, how many guests are on the tab, 0 to 99).
  • The item lines. Click an item name to add a note to that line. The minus button becomes a bin when the quantity is 1. Clear empties the order.
  • Total, then two buttons: the Fire button and Pay. The Fire button changes its name to match the order: Fire to Stations (food and drink), Send to Bar, Send to Kitchen, Fire Order (empty order), or Add to followed by the tab reference when you are adding to a tab. If a station is closed its button shows a closed badge. Its tooltip says stock is not deducted when you fire: food comes off stock when the kitchen marks it Ready/Served (or it is bumped), and drinks when they are handed over at payment.
  • Repeat last round appears after you fire an order (or add a round to a tab) and the cart is empty. It reloads the same items so you can review and send them again.

How to start your shift and record the opening float

  1. Open POS Till from the admin menu.
  2. Click More → Opening float. This item only appears if you have the POS Opening Float permission.
  3. In Float amount type the cash put in the drawer, for example 5000.00, or click one of the quick buttons (2,000, 5,000, 10,000, 20,000).
  4. Optionally fill in Note, for example "Float handed over by manager".
  5. Click Record float. You will see "Float set: MWK 5,000.00" (the amount is shown with your currency symbol).

The float is added to the expected cash when you close your shift. When you close, count the whole drawer, float included (see How to close your shift). If you record the float again before closing, the latest amount replaces the earlier one. If no float was recorded, it counts as 0.

How to take an order and pay straight away

Use this for walk-in and takeaway sales where the guest pays at once.

  1. Choose the order type: Walk-in or Takeaway. (For Dine-in you must pick a table first.)
  2. Tap items on the menu. Tap an item name in the order to add a note such as "no onions" (up to 250 characters).
  3. Optionally type a Guest name.
  4. Click Pay. The Take payment window opens.
  5. Under Payment method choose one:
    • Cash — type the amount in Tendered (or click Exact). The change is shown. The Confirm payment button stays off until the tendered amount covers the total.
    • Mobile Money — choose the Provider (Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba or Other) and type the Transaction reference. Both are required.
    • Card (manual) — type the Card last 4 digits and the Authorisation code (from slip). Both are required.
    • Card POS is marked "soon" and cannot be used yet.
  6. Optionally fill in Email (for receipt), Phone (for WhatsApp) and Notes.
  7. If you have the POS Discounts permission, you can choose a discount (see Discounts below).
  8. Click Confirm payment. You will see "Paid ORD-... — MWK 12,000.00 · cash. Change: MWK 3,000.00."

What happens next: food items are sent to the Kitchen screen. Bar and Coffee Bar drinks are marked as handed over (served) straight away, so they do not wait on the Bar or Coffee Bar screens. Stock for those drinks comes off at that moment.

If a station is closed when you send an order, you see a window headed with the closed station, its opening hours and the word "Closed" (for example "Kitchen (06:00 – 23:00) Closed"), saying the ticket "will sit unseen on the station display until it reopens". Click Cancel to stop, or Fire Anyway to send it. For payment the same warning is shown inside the Take payment window.

How to open a tab (send now, pay later)

Use this for dine-in, where the guest eats first and pays later.

  1. Click Dine-in and choose a table in Select table.... Only active, free tables are listed. A table that already has an open order cannot be chosen again.
  2. Optionally fill in Guest name and Covers.
  3. Tap the items.
  4. Click the Fire button (Fire to Stations, Send to Kitchen or Send to Bar).
  5. A window Order Fired! shows the order reference and the lines. It says "Sent to Kitchen & Bar · MWK 18,500.00 · open tab" (the station names depend on the items). Its buttons are Print KOT (kitchen order ticket), Add more, View Tabs and New order.

The tab now waits in Tabs until someone settles it. Walk-in and takeaway orders can also be sent as tabs. The Tabs tray lists up to 50 open tabs. Tabs from an earlier day stay on the list until they are settled or cancelled, with a warning that they are from a previous shift.

Stock for a tab item is taken off when the Kitchen, Bar or Coffee Bar marks it Ready (or Served), not when you fire it.

How to add another round to a tab

  1. Click Tabs.
  2. On the tab card click Add items. You return to the menu with a banner saying you are adding to that tab. (The button is not shown once a split payment has started on that tab.)
  3. Tap the new items and click the Fire button, now called Add to followed by the tab reference.
  4. You will see "Added 2 item(s) to ORD-... — new total MWK 24,000.00." You stay in add mode so you can send more rounds.

You can only add to tabs you opened, unless you have POS All Cashiers' Tabs. To stop adding, finish by settling the tab or start a new order. While you are adding to a tab the Pay button is switched off; settle from the Tabs tray instead.

How to settle a tab (split bill, tip, discount)

  1. Click Tabs. Use the Search tabs… box to find the tab.
  2. Click Settle on the tab card. The Settle tab window opens with the Order total.
  3. To split the bill, click a number next to Split bill: Off, 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6. The window shows Each person.
  4. Choose a Tip: None, 5%, 10%, 15%, or type a Custom tip amount. When a bill is split, each person pays their share plus their own tip.
  5. If you have the POS Discounts permission and nobody has paid yet, choose a discount (see Discounts).
  6. Choose the Payment method (Cash, Mobile or Card) and fill in the same details as in "pay straight away" (Tendered; or Provider and Reference; or Card last 4 and Auth code).
  7. Click Take payment. On a split bill the button reads Pay split 1 of 3, then Pay split 2 of 3, and so on. After each person pays you see "Split 1/3 — cash MWK 4,000.00. Next person ready." and the window stays open for the next person.
  8. When the last person has paid, a Tab settled window shows the receipt options (see receipts).

On a split, the last person pays whatever is left, so rounding pennies are never lost (for example MWK 100.00 split three ways is 33.33, 33.33 and 33.34). Once the first person has paid, the split cannot be changed and only the next leg can be paid, even after a page refresh.

Drinks from the Bar and Coffee Bar are marked as served automatically when you settle, so an un-bumped drink ticket never blocks payment. Food is different: see How to settle a tab whose food was never served.

Discounts

A discount section only appears if you have the POS Discounts permission.

  • In the Take payment window (pay now) or the Settle tab window, click a preset (None, 5%, 10%, 15%, 25%) or type an Amount.
  • Optionally choose a Reason: Staff discount, Happy hour, Manager override, Complimentary, Loyalty discount or Event promo.
  • On a tab, a discount can only be given on the first payment, and only once. If the tab already has a discount, a second one is not applied. Once a split has started the discount section is hidden.
  • A discount can never take the total to zero or below: the lowest total is 0.01.
  • Deal discounts (set up under Deals & Promos) are applied automatically and listed in the payment window. They need no permission.

If you do not have the permission and a discount is sent anyway, you see "You do not have permission to apply discounts."

How to set up deals and promotions (Deals & Promos)

Deals are discounts the till applies by itself. Nobody has to remember them: every time the cart changes, the till checks the active deals and takes the discount off. Staff need no permission for a deal discount.

Open Admin menu → Restaurant & Bar → Deals & Promos (/admin/deals.php, page title Deals & Promotions). It needs the Ingredients & Recipes permission (managers and administrators by default) and the POS & Stations module.

How to add a deal

  1. Click Add Deal.
  2. Type the Deal Name (required, up to 100 characters, must be unique) and, if you want, a Short Description. The description is shown on the receipt.
  3. Under What kind of deal is this? pick the type card.
  4. Fill in the amounts for that type (see the table).
  5. Under Which items does this deal cover? choose Everything, By Category (tick one or more categories, for example only Drinks) or Specific Items (pick a category, then an item, then add it; repeat for more).
  6. If you want, limit it to some days of the week (none ticked = every day), a Start time and End time (both or neither), and, under Run between these dates, a Start date and End date (leave blank to run indefinitely).
  7. Tick This deal is active to start it straight away. Tick Exclusive deal if it must not combine with other deals on the same order. Evaluation order (lower number first) only matters when several deals could apply.
  8. Click Save Deal. You see "Deal created." (or "Deal updated." after an edit).

Switching off, editing and deleting

  • Each deal is a card. A pulsing green dot means it is running right now (active, inside its dates, days and hours).
  • The ON / OFF button switches a deal on or off at once, without deleting it.
  • Edit opens the same form. The bin button deletes the deal after "Delete this deal? It will stop applying immediately on the POS." Switch it off instead if you may want it again.

What the cashier sees

  • When a deal applies, a green line with a tag icon appears in the cart and in the payment window: the deal name, what it gave and the saving.
  • Deals are worked out when the order is created. Settling a tab later does not add new deal discounts.
  • A deal discount and a manual discount (see Discounts) can both apply; the order records both reasons together (for example Happy Hour + Staff discount).
  • The server checks every deal again before saving, so a deal that has ended or does not match the order is refused. Deals together can never take more than 90% off an order. Each deal discount is written to the order's audit log.

How to charge an order to a guest's room

Room orders are never paid at the till. They are added to the guest's room bill (folio) and paid at check-out or through Payments.

  1. Click the Room Service button above the menu (or click Room as the order type). The menu shows only items that are switched on for room service.
  2. In Select checked-in room... choose the room. Only rooms with a checked-in guest are listed. A room that already has an active room-service order is greyed out until that order is served, settled, cancelled or completed.
  3. Tap the items and click the Fire button.
  4. Each item is posted to the guest's folio straight away as a charge, and the ticket is sent to the Kitchen, Bar or Coffee Bar screen.

The Room Service page (Room Service guide) shows the same orders and lets staff mark them delivered. Room-service orders never show in the Tabs tray and never stop you closing your shift.

Do not use Pay for a room order. The system refuses it with "Room-service orders are charged to the guest room folio — use Park (Fire to Kitchen) to send the order to the kitchen." If the charge cannot be posted to the folio, the whole order is refused and nothing is sent.

How to cancel or void an order

Cancel is for an unpaid tab where nobody has started the food or drink. Void reverses an order even after work has started, or after it is paid.

Cancel an unpaid tab

  1. Click Tabs, then More on the tab card, then Cancel. The Cancel button only appears while every item is still pending.
  2. In the Cancel order window type a reason of at least 8 characters. The window counts "0 / 8 characters minimum" as you type.
  3. Click Cancel order. You will see "Order cancelled." or a message from the system. If the order was a room-service order, its folio charges are voided too.

Cancelling an order before the kitchen starts it does not need the POS Void Tabs permission. You can cancel your own tabs. Managers and admins can cancel any tab. Many tabs at once: tick them in the Tabs tray (Bulk tools → Select all, Select stale or tick each card) and click Bulk cancel. You see "3 tabs cancelled.", or "2 cancelled, 1 failed."

Void a tab

  1. You need POS Void Tabs (“Void open tabs from the till, one at a time or in bulk”) and Restaurant Orders (POS). Without POS Void Tabs the button is not shown.
  2. Click Tabs, More on the tab card, then Void.
  3. Type a reason of at least 8 characters (an optional follow-up note is also offered) and click Void order.
  4. You will see "Order voided." Stock is restored, the order is removed from the station screens, any payment is reversed with a matching refund line, and any room-service charge is taken off the folio.

Many tabs at once: select them and click Bulk void. You see "3 tabs voided." A void is permanent and is recorded with your name and reason.

A paid order cannot be voided from the Tabs tray because only open tabs are listed. Managers and admins can void a paid order from Orders & Tabs (see the Stock guide). A paid order can also be refunded from Recent.

How to refund a paid order

  1. Click Recent. Only people with POS Refunds see the Refund button next to paid orders. (Without that permission, ask a manager to do it.)
  2. Click Refund. The Process refund window explains the order will be marked as refunded and a negative payment record created, and that stock is not automatically restored.
  3. Type the Reason for refund (at least 5 characters, for example "Guest complaint, overcharge, duplicate order").
  4. Click Confirm refund. You will see "Refund processed for ORD-..."

The whole order is refunded, including every leg of a split bill, in the method it was paid with. Only paid orders can be refunded, and an order that is already fully refunded is refused. Because stock is not put back, a manager must correct stock by hand if needed.

How to 86 an item (mark it unavailable)

"86" means the item is finished. This hides it from the till for everyone.

  1. You need POS Quick-86 Items. Click More → 86 mode. You see "86 Mode ON — tap an item to toggle availability". While this mode is on, tapping a tile changes its availability instead of adding it to the order.
  2. Each tile shows Tap to 86 (available item) or Tap to enable (item already 86'd, shown with an 86 badge).
  3. Tap the item. You see "86'd: Item name" or "Re-enabled: Item name".
  4. Click More → 86 mode again to switch the mode off. You see "86 Mode OFF".

86'd items disappear from the till for staff who cannot 86. You can also change availability on the Menu page (see How to add or change a menu item).

This is different from "86 this item" on a Kitchen, Bar or Coffee Bar ticket. That removes one line from one order and alerts the cashier. See the Kitchen guide.

How to settle a tab whose food was never served

A tab cannot be settled while it has food that the Kitchen has not marked served. If the food was never bumped (for example from an earlier shift), a manager can force it.

  1. Click Settle on the tab and click Take payment. If food is not served you see "This tab still has 2 food items not yet served — complete kitchen service, or ask a manager to force-serve it before settling."
  2. If you have POS Force-Serve Tab the till retries by itself with the force option.
  3. If you do not, a Manager Authorisation window opens, showing the permission required. A different manager types their Manager username and Password and clicks Authorise. You see "Authorised by (manager name)" and the payment goes through.

The manager approval works once and lasts 5 minutes. You cannot approve your own action. After 5 wrong attempts in 10 minutes the till says "Too many attempts, wait N minute(s) before trying again." If the food's ingredients are not in stock, force-serve is refused with a stock message; receive stock first.

How to send a note to a station and read replies

  1. Click Note.
  2. Type the Message (up to 255 characters). Under Send to choose Kitchen, Bar or Coffee Bar. Tick Urgent only if it cannot wait.
  3. Optionally open Attach to an order & see its thread and pick an order.
  4. Click Send note. You see "Kitchen note sent." (or "[URGENT]" added for urgent notes). If the message is empty you see "Type a station note first."

Replies and notes from the stations appear under Messages and as alert cards. A station's "ready for collection" message appears as Ready for collection with the order reference. An alert from the station about an 86'd item tells you to inform the guest and offer an alternative.

How to print a kitchen ticket or send a receipt

  • Kitchen ticket (KOT): click Print KOT in the Order Fired! window, or click Tabs, More, KOT on a tab card.
  • Receipt after payment: the Payment received or Tab settled window offers Email (type the address and click Send email), WhatsApp, and Print receipt. Click Done when finished.
  • Receipt later: click Recent and click the order to open its receipt page (stock-receipt.php). There you can email it ("Receipt emailed to name@example.com."), print it, or download a PDF.

WhatsApp receipts work in one of two ways. If a WhatsApp provider is switched on in WhatsApp Settings, the receipt is sent automatically from the hotel's account and you see "Receipt sent by WhatsApp to ...". If not, you see "Opening WhatsApp with the receipt for ... — press Send in WhatsApp.": WhatsApp opens on your device with the receipt already typed in, and you must press Send in WhatsApp. If it does not open, click the Open WhatsApp link next to the message. If an automatic send fails you see "WhatsApp receipt could not be sent: ...".

On the receipt page, a manager or admin can apply a discount or service charge before an order is paid (Manual consolidation). After payment the totals can no longer be changed: "This order has been paid - its totals can no longer be changed. Use the refund flow and take a new sale instead."

How to close your shift (Z-report)

Close your shift at the end of your session. The count is blind: the till does not show you the expected amounts until you submit.

  1. First settle or cancel every open tab that you opened in this business day. Room-service orders do not count. If any remain, closing is blocked (see Problems).
  2. Click More → Close shift (Z-report).
  3. Count what you took, then enter all three amounts. Enter 0 if there is none:
    • Cash counted — all the cash in the drawer, including the opening float. The close window shows Opening float and the line "Cash to count in the drawer = opening float + cash sales − cash refunds. Include the float in your count." If no float was recorded it shows "(no opening float recorded for this shift, counted as 0)".
    • Mobile money totals — the total of mobile money payments you took.
    • Card totals — the total of card payments you took.
  4. Optionally type a Note, for example "Drawer started with X".
  5. Click Close shift.
  6. If each amount is within 1.00 of what the system expects, the shift closes and a Shift closed window shows a table with Tender, Expected, Declared and Variance for Cash, Mobile and Card, plus orders, voids, tips collected and tabs settled from earlier. Under the table a line reads Cash: Float ... + Cash sales ... − Cash refunds ... = Expected cash .... You will see "Shift closed. Paid orders: 24 (settled earlier tabs: 2). Variance — cash: 0.00, mobile: 0.00, card: 0.00."
  7. Click Print Z-report to print that window, or Continue.

If your count does not balance

  • Without POS Shift Override you see "Shift does not balance (variance 250.00). Recount the drawer or ask a supervisor to close on your behalf with an override." Recount and try again, or ask a supervisor.
  • A supervisor with POS Shift Override ticks Admin override (close despite variance), types a Reason (audit-logged) of at least 5 characters, and clicks Close shift. The reason is saved with the close and shown on the report.

Each time you close, the next close for you starts from this one, so a second close in the same day never counts the first one's sales again.

How to read the shift close report

Management can open the full printable report. You need the POS Accounting permission.

  1. Admin menu → Money → POS Accounting.
  2. Pick the Business date, click Apply, then click Z-report. This opens shift-close-report.php for that date.
  3. The day view is titled Shift Closes with the date. It shows Total Revenue, All Orders, Total Voids and Net Variance, and a table with Cashier, Closed, Revenue, Orders, Voids, Cash Var, Mobile Var, Card Var, Variance, Override and Report. If any cashier is short a banner reads "TOTAL SHORTFALL ACROSS ALL CASHIERS ... investigate before EOD sign-off." A Shift Close History table lists earlier closes.
  4. Click Report on a row for one cashier's report. It shows Total Revenue, Paid Orders, Voids, Settled Tabs, then Tender Reconciliation with a Cash: line (Float + Cash sales − Cash refunds = Expected cash; it says so if no float was recorded, and labels closes made before the float was included as not counting it) (Tender, System (Expected), Counted (Declared), Variance, Status, where Status is BALANCED, SHORTFALL or OVERAGE), Shift Notes, Top Items, Paid Orders, Refund / Void Payouts and Voided Orders.
  5. Use Print / Save PDF to print. A short cashier shows "SHORTFALL DETECTED — cumulative variance ... Management review required." An override close shows "Override applied." with the reason.

For day-end money checks across the whole hotel see Reports and end of day.

What happens when the internet drops

  • A header pill shows Offline, and a bar at the bottom says You are offline.
  • Only the Pay (pay straight away) form is saved on the device while offline. You see "You are offline — action saved locally and will sync automatically when the connection returns." The bar then reads Offline — queued with a count such as 1 queued.
  • When the connection returns the bar reads Back online — syncing and sends the saved orders. Retry now sends them straight away. If your session has expired you see "Session expired — log in again to sync N queued action(s)." Sign in again and the orders send.
  • Each saved order carries a unique number, so if it is sent twice the till answers "Order ... was already recorded — duplicate submission ignored, nothing charged twice."
  • Everything else (Fire, adding to a tab, settling a tab, refunds, close shift, notes) needs the connection. The till says "Network error — could not reach the server. Please retry." Do not assume it went through; check Tabs once you are back online.

Managers can see what was queued and when it synced: Admin menu → Restaurant & Bar → Offline Log (page title Offline Activity Log), which needs the Offline Activity Log permission.

How to check the Offline Log

Managers can see exactly what the till saved while offline and when it reached the server. Open Admin menu → Restaurant & Bar → Offline Log (/admin/offline-log.php, page title Offline Activity Log). It needs the Offline Activity Log permission.

  • The top shows Replayed actions, Unique submissions, Distinct users, Avg sync lag and Max sync lag (the time between saving on the device and reaching the server).
  • Filter by From / To date, User and Endpoint contains, then click Filter (Reset clears it). Most replayed endpoints shows which actions were queued most.
  • Each row shows who, When queued, When synced, the Lag (amber over 1 minute, red over 10 minutes), the action, a link to the order or record, and the Result. HTTP 200 means the server accepted it; anything else needs checking on that order.
  • "Unique submissions" lower than "Replayed actions" means the same order was sent more than once. The till recorded it once (see the duplicate message above), so nothing was charged twice.

How to add or change a menu item

Admin menu → Restaurant & Bar → Menu (/admin/menu-management.php). Permission: Menu. Only shown when the POS & Stations module is on.

The page has three buttons: Food Menu, Drinks Menu and Room Service (which jumps to the Room Service category). Food categories are Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks and Room Service. Drink categories are Coffee, Tea, Juice, Cocktails, Mocktails, Beer, Wine, Spirits, Soft Drinks and Room Service.

Add an item

  1. Open Food Menu or Drinks Menu and click Add Food Item / Add Drink Item (or Add Item beside a category).
  2. In Add New Menu Item choose a Category and fill in Item name, Description and Price. The price can be 0 or more.
  3. Optionally set Display order (left blank, the item goes to the end of its category) and, for drinks, Tags.
  4. Choose the Station (where this item is prepared): Kitchen (KDS), Bar (BDS) or Coffee Bar (CDS). This decides which screen receives the ticket.
  5. Tick Available (visible on menu), Show in POS / Till and Show in Room Service Dashboard as needed.
  6. Click Add item. You will see "Menu item added successfully!"

Change an item

  1. Edit the row directly: name, description, price, Routing (station and the POS and Room Service tick boxes) and Status (Available or Unavailable).
  2. Click Save on that row. You will see "Menu item updated successfully!" You can drag rows to change their order.
  3. Toggle flips Available/Unavailable straight away: "Menu item availability updated!"
  4. Recipe opens the stock recipe for the item (see the Stock guide). Feature posts the item to Facebook, if posting is switched on.
  5. Delete asks "Delete this menu item? Any attached stock recipe will also be removed." Click Delete to confirm: "Menu item deleted successfully!"

An item shows on the till only if it is Available and Show in POS / Till is ticked. It shows in room-service mode only if Show in Room Service Dashboard is ticked. The price on the menu is the price the guest pays, tax included. The till always uses the price saved here, not a price sent from the screen.

How to set up the dine-in tables

Admin menu → Restaurant & Bar → Tables (/admin/restaurant-tables.php). Permission: Ingredients & Recipes. The till only offers tables from this list and stops two open orders on the same table.

  1. Under Table Range fill in From and To (table numbers) and, if you wish, a default Capacity (seats).
  2. Tick Deactivate numeric tables outside this range if tables outside the range should be switched off.
  3. Click Generate / Update Range. You will see "Restaurant table range saved."
  4. In the table list, set each table's Capacity (optional), Notes (for example Window, patio, VIP) and the Active tick, then click Save Table Details. You will see "Table capacities and active flags saved."

The list shows each table's Live Status (Available, or Pending, Preparing, Ready, Collecting, Served, Awaiting Payment, Recalled, Active), its Current Order and its Last Payment. A summary shows total rows, active tables and total capacity. Open POS takes you to the till.

How to take payment for a table from the Tables page

You need Settle Restaurant Tables.

  1. Open the Tables page. Find the table with an open order.
  2. Click Take payment in its order box (POS details shows the order timeline).
  3. Choose Cash, Mobile money or Card and fill in Tendered, or Provider and Reference, or Card last 4 and Auth code.
  4. Click Take payment & close table. You will see "Table 5 settled — ORD-... · MWK 18,500.00 · cash." A Table settled window offers email, WhatsApp and print.

This takes the full amount at once. It has no split, tip or discount. If part of the bill has already been paid at the till you see "This tab is mid split-payment (1 of 3 paid) - finish it from the POS till."

Rules the system enforces

  • Prices always come from the saved menu, never from the till screen. A line quantity cannot be more than 1000.
  • A dine-in order needs a table, and a room order needs a checked-in room. A table or room that already has an active order cannot be used again until that order is settled, served, cancelled or completed.
  • Room orders are charged to the guest's folio and cannot be paid at the till.
  • The till keeps unique order numbers, so a repeated send or a re-sent offline order never charges twice.
  • A tab already settled cannot be charged again: "This tab is already paid by (name). Refreshing open tabs so it cannot be charged twice."
  • You can only settle or add to tabs you opened, unless you have POS All Cashiers' Tabs.
  • A tab with unserved food cannot be settled without force-serve. Bar and Coffee Bar drinks do not block settlement.
  • Cash payments need the tendered amount to cover the total plus tip. Mobile money needs a provider and reference. Card needs the last 4 digits and the authorisation code.
  • Cancel only works before any item has started. Void needs a reason of at least 8 characters. Refund needs a reason of at least 5 characters. Both are recorded with your name.
  • You cannot close your shift while you have open tabs from the current business day. All three amounts must be entered. A close that is out by more than 1.00 is blocked unless someone with POS Shift Override overrides it with a reason.
  • Manager approval cannot be given by yourself, works once, expires after 5 minutes, and is limited to 5 failed attempts in 10 minutes.
