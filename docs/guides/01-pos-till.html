Guide 01 · Cashier

POS Till

Your home screen for the restaurant, bar and coffee shop. Every order starts here.

URL /admin/pos.php Role restaurant_staff · manager · admin Read time 5 min

Layout (left → right)

Pane Purpose
Left — Categories All menu sections (Starters, Mains, Drinks…). Tap one to filter.
Centre — Items Tap an item to add it to the cart. Counts (e.g. 23 items) are visible per category.
Right — Cart & Pay Current order, quantities, totals, payment buttons, park-as-tab.

The top Till Bar always shows: cashier name, today's order count, today's revenue, today's cash collected, plus links to Stations (KDS/BDS/CDS feed), Restaurant Orders, and Sign Out.

Take an order

  1. Pick the destination — choose a Table number (dine-in), Room number (room charge / room service) or leave blank for takeaway.
  2. Pick a category on the left.
  3. Tap items in the centre — each tap adds 1; tap + / − in the cart to adjust qty.
  4. Review the cart total at the bottom-right.
  5. Choose action:
    • Pay & Send (walk-in/takeaway) — pay now, then fire immediately to KDS/BDS/CDS.
    • Fire to Stations (or Send to Kitchen / Send to Bar, based on cart mix) — opens the order as a tab to settle later.
    • Settle from Tabs (dine-in) or Room Folio (room service) — the pay button guides you to the correct settlement path.
    • Open tabs can be re-opened later from the Tabs panel and add more items, then settle.
  6. Optionally split the bill or add a tip in the payment modal before choosing a method (see sections below).
  7. Choose payment method: Cash · Mobile Money · Card.
    • Mobile Money requires provider + transaction reference.
    • Card requires last 4 digits + authorisation code.
    • For cash, enter the tendered amount — change due is calculated automatically.
  8. The success overlay shows the order reference. Print or hand the receipt to the guest.

Tabs (parked orders)

  • A parked tab stays open until you settle it.
  • The Tabs tray shows: reference, table/room, order type, item count, total, age timer (turns red after 30 min), fired time, served time, and current kitchen status (pending / prep / ready / collection / served counts).
  • Tap Settle to open the payment modal — where you can optionally split the bill and add a tip before choosing a payment method.
  • Tap Details to open the full order detail modal. Inside you'll see:
    • Order Lifecycle track — five milestone steps (Placed → Fired → Ready → Served → Paid) with icons, timestamps, and colour-coded dots: green for completed steps, primary colour for the current active step.
    • Item Status stepper — how many items are at each kitchen stage (pending / preparing / ready / collecting / served).
    • Line items table with per-item kitchen status and timestamps.
    • Audit & event log — every action on this order in chronological order.
  • For settled tabs, use Receipt to open the printable receipt page.
  • Admin/manager tab cards also show a Lifecycle link that opens /admin/order-lifecycle.php in a full page with Cancel and Void actions.
  • Search the open tabs from the box in the tray header (or press / while the tray is open) — filter by reference, customer name, or table number. Handy on a busy night with many tabs.

Running bar tabs — add another round

A real bar tab grows through the night: the same tab accumulates every round and is settled once at the end. Instead of ringing a brand-new order for each round, append to the existing tab.

  1. Open the Tabs tray and tap Add items on the tab you want to grow (also offered as Add more on the "Order Fired" confirmation right after you open a tab).
  2. You're returned to the menu with an orange "Adding to TAB-####" banner above the total. The Fire button now reads Add to TAB-####.
  3. Tap the new drinks/food and press Add to TAB-####. The items are appended, the tab total updates, and the new lines fire to the Bar / Kitchen displays as fresh tickets.
  4. Keep adding rounds as needed — the banner stays until you tap the ✕ on it, settle the tab, or it is cancelled/voided.
  5. Settle once at the end from the Tabs tray as usual.
Adding items recalculates the tab's subtotal, total, cost and VAT automatically. You cannot add to a tab that is already mid split-payment — finish settling it first. Restaurant staff can only add to tabs they opened.

Repeat last round

After you fire or add a round, a Repeat last round button appears under the Fire/Pay buttons (only while the cart is empty). Tap it to re-load the exact same items into the cart so you can fire the same round again in one tap — review, then Fire or Add to TAB.

Covers (guest count)

Use the Covers field in the order panel to record how many guests are on the tab. It shows as a pill on the tab card and is stored against the order for per-head spend reporting. Optional — leave it at 0 to skip.

Settling drink tabs (bar auto-serve)

Drinks are handed to the customer immediately, so the till does not wait for the Bar Display to "bump" them before you can settle. When you settle a tab, any un-bumped Bar and Coffee Bar lines are automatically marked served and their stock is deducted at that moment.

Food still waits. Kitchen items must be served (bumped on the Kitchen Display) before a tab containing them can be settled — this protects against billing for food that never arrived. The settle dialog also has quick +500 / +1k / +5k / Exact cash-tender buttons to speed up change.

Splitting a bill

When a group wants to pay separately, use the Split Bill selector inside the Settle / Pay modal before processing any payment.

  1. Tap Settle on the tab (or Pay & Send for a new order).
  2. In the payment modal, find the Split Bill row and choose the number of ways: 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 · 6. Choose Off for a single payer.
  3. The modal shows Person 1 of N and the share amount (order total ÷ ways).
  4. Optionally add a tip for this payer (see Tips section below), then choose a payment method and complete the payment as normal.
  5. A green confirmation strip confirms that Person 1's share is paid and the modal advances automatically to Person 2 of N.
  6. Repeat for each payer — each can use a different payment method and add their own tip.
  7. After the last payer the order closes and the receipt is generated.
The bill is split equally — each person pays the same share of the food total. Tips are per-person on top of the share. You cannot assign specific items to specific payers.
Do not close the modal between payers. If the browser is refreshed mid-split the order stays placed and can be re-opened from the Tabs tray to continue from the correct leg.

Tips & gratuity

Tips are collected per payer inside the payment modal, on top of the food total (or share).

  • Four preset buttons: None · 5% · 10% · 15%. Tap one to apply it instantly.
  • Or type any amount in the Custom tip field.
  • The Amount due display updates live — share + tip — so the cashier always sees exactly what to collect.
  • For cash payments the tendered field pre-fills to the amount due; adjust it if the guest hands over more.
  • Tips appear as a separate green line on the printed receipt, with a Grand Total (food + tip).
  • The full tips total for the shift is shown in the Z-report summary under Tips collected.

How stock works

  • Stock is not deducted when you fire an order. It is deducted when the kitchen/bar/coffee station marks each item Ready (preparation complete).
  • This means a Cancel Before Prep never needs to restore stock — nothing was taken yet.
  • A Void on a partially-prepared order only restores stock for items that had already been marked Ready.
  • The POS shows a ready alert from each station when all their items are ready or in the collection queue.

My Orders tracker

The My Orders panel (bottom-left of the POS till) shows every live order you have fired this shift, refreshed every second so KDS/BDS/CDS changes appear almost immediately.

Element What it means
⚡ RUSH badge This order has been marked high-priority. All stations see it highlighted in red.
Station pips (🍳 · 🍺 · ☕) One pip per station. Green tick = all items at that station done. Blue spinner + count = items still in progress.
Amber italic note The order note currently visible on all station screens.
Small item summary Shows the ordered items in small text. Long orders show the first few items plus a remaining count.
Rush button Toggles priority on/off. All stations get an instant URGENT inbox message when you set or clear Rush.

Rush and Edit note are only available while the order is in placed status (kitchen not yet done). Once all stations have served, the buttons disappear.

Station replies & notifications

  • When kitchen/bar/coffee sends a note back, you get an in-app notification and sound alert on POS.
  • Use Sounds / Sound Settings to choose the normal alert, urgent alert, and volume for the till device.
  • Direct station messages (for your user) appear in the POS inbox as Station → You.
  • When all items at a station are Ready or in Collection, POS shows a ready-collection alert with the item summary. The cashier who placed the order gets a vibrating urgent banner ("🔔 Your order is ready!"). Other POS sessions get a quieter green banner.
  • Ready and station-message popups stay on POS for 2 minutes and can be closed immediately with the × button.
  • If a tab was already paid elsewhere, stock is short, or there is nothing pending, POS shows a friendly dialog and refreshes the affected tray instead of leaving the cashier guessing.
  • Important action buttons include a brief click lock to prevent accidental double taps.

Cancelling an order before prep

If you realise a mistake immediately after firing — and the kitchen/bar has not started yet — you can cancel without involving a manager.

  1. Open the Tabs tray.
  2. Find the order. If all items are still pending (none started), a red Cancel (Before Prep) button appears.
  3. Enter a reason of at least 8 characters (logged for audit).
  4. The order is removed from all station boards. Because no stock was deducted yet, nothing needs restoring.
Once the kitchen or bar taps Start or Ready on any item, the Cancel Before Prep button disappears. You must use Void (manager) instead.

Voiding an order manager only

  1. Open the Tabs tray and find the order, or open it from Restaurant Orders.
  2. Tap Void & restore stock.
  3. Type a reason of at least 8 characters (saved for audit).
  4. Stock is restored for any items that had already been prepared (marked Ready on KDS). Payment is cancelled and the order is removed from KDS/BDS/CDS automatically.

Closing your shift (Z-report)

  1. Tap Close Shift in the till bar (visible from cashier role and above).
  2. Type your counted cash drawer total. Include any cash tips in this count — the system adds expected tips to the expected cash total automatically.
  3. The variance is calculated automatically.
  4. If variance > tolerance, an admin override + reason ≥ 5 chars is required.
  5. Confirm. A Z-report is saved and you are signed out.
If tips were collected during the shift, the Z-report summary shows a Tips collected line with the total. Expected cash, mobile, and card amounts already include tips, so declare your full collected amount including tips.

POS permissions overview

Each POS action is controlled by a granular permission key that can be granted or revoked per user in User Management → Permissions. The defaults by role are:

Permission Admin Manager Restaurant Staff
pos_till — Access POS✓✓✓
pos_float — Opening float✓✓✓
pos_discount — Apply discounts✓✓✓
pos_86 — Quick-86 items✓✓—
pos_refund — Process refunds✓✓—
Permissions can be individually overridden per user — a restaurant staff member can be granted pos_refund if needed, or a manager can have it revoked.

Manager in-session authorisation

If a logged-in cashier attempts an action they don't have permission for (e.g. a refund), the system shows a Manager Authorisation overlay instead of blocking with an error. This lets a manager approve the action on the spot — without requiring the cashier to log out.

  1. The cashier taps the action button (e.g. Refund or 86).
  2. The dark Manager Authorisation modal appears, showing which permission is required.
  3. A manager (or any user with the required permission) enters their own username and password.
  4. On success, a one-use session token is issued — valid for 5 minutes — and the action proceeds with both the cashier and the authorising manager recorded in the audit log.
The authorising manager must be a different user account than the cashier — self-authorisation is blocked.

Opening float pos_float

Record the cash placed in the drawer before your first order so the Z-report variance accounts for it.

  1. Tap Float in the till bar. If you don't have the pos_float permission, the Manager Authorisation overlay appears first.
  2. Enter the float amount — quick-fill buttons for common values (2 000, 5 000, 10 000, 20 000).
  3. Optionally add a note (e.g. "Float handed over by manager").
  4. Tap Record float. The entry is saved to stock_shift_opens and written to the system activity log.
The float is purely a declaration — it does not affect order processing. When reconciling, expected cash = float + sales. The float record helps explain any surplus in the drawer.

Discounts at the till pos_discount

Apply a percentage or fixed discount to an order before taking payment — available on both new orders and tab settlements. The discount section is hidden if the logged-in user lacks pos_discount.

  1. Open the Pay modal (new order) or tap Settle on a tab.
  2. In the Discount section, choose a preset: None · 5% · 10% · 15% · 25% — or type a custom amount.
  3. Pick a reason from the dropdown (Staff discount, Happy hour, Manager override, Complimentary, Loyalty, Event promo).
  4. The total display updates live — confirm payment as normal.
  5. The discount is saved on the order as discount_amount / discount_reason, logged to the activity log, and printed on the receipt.
For split bills, discounts can only be applied on the first leg. Once any split payment has been recorded, the discount section is hidden to prevent changing the agreed total.

Quick-86 item toggle pos_86 — manager

Take a menu item off sale instantly — without leaving the POS — when you run out or need to pull it for any reason. Restaurant staff who lack pos_86 will see the Manager Authorisation overlay when they tap the 86 button.

  1. Tap the 86 button in the till bar.
  2. The menu enters 86 Mode — the button turns red and all tiles show "Tap to 86" or "Tap to enable".
  3. Tap any item tile to toggle its availability:
    • An available item gets an 86 badge and is greyed out — it disappears from cashier screens.
    • An 86'd item (highlighted with green border) is re-enabled and returns to all screens.
  4. Tap 86 again to exit 86 Mode. Tapping items goes back to adding them to the cart.
Changes take effect immediately — regular cashiers see the item disappear as soon as the page next renders. Each toggle is written to the order audit log and the system activity log.

POS refunds pos_refund — manager

Process a refund for a paid order without voiding it — the audit trail is preserved and a negative payment record is created. Cashiers without pos_refund will see the Manager Authorisation overlay before the refund modal opens.

  1. Open the Recent orders panel (till bar).
  2. Find the paid order and tap the purple Refund button on the right.
  3. If you need manager auth, enter the manager's credentials in the overlay that appears first.
  4. In the refund modal, enter a reason (minimum 5 characters).
  5. Tap Confirm refund. The order status changes to Refunded, a negative payment entry is created in the ledger, and the event is logged to the system activity log (including the authorising manager's name if applicable).
Stock is not automatically reversed. If physical stock was already used, record a manual stock adjustment in the Stock section. The negative payment record is sufficient for financial reconciliation.

Tips

The Till works offline — your orders queue locally and sync when the internet returns.
  • All hard buttons are at least 44px tall — designed for touch.
  • Press ? on a keyboard to see the help bubbles.
  • Fullscreen kiosk mode — on first load a small banner at the bottom says "Tap anywhere to go fullscreen". Tap anywhere and the browser enters full-screen, hiding tabs and the address bar. If you accidentally exit (e.g. back-swipe), the banner reappears and the next tap re-enters it automatically.
