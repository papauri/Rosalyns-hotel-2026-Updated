Stock & Stock Orders

Every drink, ingredient, and consumable is tracked here. The KDS, BDS, CDS and Room Service stations automatically deduct stock when they mark each item prepared.

URL /admin/stock-orders.php Role stock_manager · manager · admin Read time 5 min

Pages you'll use

Page Purpose
Stock Orders stock-orders.php All restaurant / bar / coffee / room-service orders, voids, reconciliation.
Stock Ingredients stock-ingredients.php Master list of every item: name, unit, par level, current count, supplier.
Stock Receipts Goods-in (delivery from supplier) — restocks ingredients.

A typical day

1. Morning — receive deliveries

  1. Go to Stock Receipts → New Receipt — or use Stock → Barcode Receive on mobile to scan deliveries with your phone camera (faster for large deliveries).
  2. Pick the supplier.
  3. Add each item delivered with quantity and unit cost.
  4. Save. Stock is added to the relevant ingredients automatically.

2. During service — automatic deduction

  • Stock is not deducted when the till fires an order. It deducts when the kitchen/bar/coffee station marks each item Ready (tap the Ready button on the station screen).
  • This means orders cancelled before any item is marked Ready cost no stock — nothing has been used yet.
  • Voids on partially-prepared orders restore only the stock that was deducted at the Ready step for items that were prepared.
  • The Bump action catches any items that were never individually marked Ready and deducts their stock at that point.

3. End of day — variance check

  1. Go to Stock Variance.
  2. The page lists every ingredient with system count vs counted count.
  3. Walk the bar / kitchen, count physically, type counts in.
  4. Save. The variance % is logged. Anything over 5% is flagged red.

Cancelling an order (before prep)

Cashiers can cancel an order themselves if preparation has not started yet.

  1. Open the order (or use the Cancel Before Prep button on the POS Tabs tray).
  2. Tap Cancel, enter a reason ≥ 8 characters.
  3. The order is removed from all station boards. No stock is restored because nothing was deducted yet.
Cancel is blocked once any item reaches preparing, ready, collection, or served status on any station. Use Void (admin/manager) in those cases.

Voiding a stock order

  1. Open the order on Stock Orders.
  2. Tap Void.
  3. Reason ≥ 8 characters required.
  4. Stock is restored for any items that had been marked Ready (deducted at KDS), the folio is reversed (if the order was charged to a room), and KDS/BDS/CDS stations are cleared.
Void works at any stage of an order, regardless of whether prep has started. Only admin and manager roles can void.

Order Lifecycle Viewer /admin/order-lifecycle.php

Every stock order has a dedicated full-page audit trail at /admin/order-lifecycle.php?id=<order_id>. Open it from:

  • Stock Orders list → click the order reference.
  • KDS / BDS / CDS ticket footer → Log button (opens in new tab).
  • POS → Tabs tray → Details → Lifecycle link (admin/manager only).

Actions available on the lifecycle page

ActionRoleConditions
Cancel order admin · manager Status is placed / new and kitchen has not started any item (all still pending).

The lifecycle page is read-only for restaurant_staff. Cancel and Void are gated by role and order state — the buttons only appear when the action is permitted.

Order item lifecycle

Each item on an order passes through these statuses on the station display:

Status Meaning Stock effect
pending Order fired, not yet started in the kitchen/bar. None.
preparing Station tapped Start on this item. None.
ready Item prepared, at the pass. Deducted here.
collection Runner has taken the item for delivery. Already deducted at ready.
served Delivered to guest. Ticket bumped. Already deducted.
void Voided. Stock restored if it had been deducted. Restored (if was ready/collection/served).

Barcode stock receiving Camera scanner

When a delivery arrives, instead of typing each item manually you can scan product barcodes with your phone camera. The system matches the barcode to an existing ingredient (or offers to register a new one) and fills in the receipt line automatically.

Opening the scanner

  1. Go to Stock → Barcode Receive (/admin/stock-barcode-receive.php).
  2. Tap Start Scanner — your phone camera activates and shows a viewfinder.
  3. Point the camera at a product barcode. A beep and vibration confirm a successful read.
  4. The matched item is added to the receipt list. Scan the next product immediately.

First-time scan of an unregistered product

If the barcode hasn't been seen before, a registration dialog appears:

  1. Choose whether this product is an Ingredient (kitchen/bar stock, e.g. a beer keg) or a POS Item (retail product sold directly from the till, e.g. a can of Coca-Cola).
  2. Fill in the name, unit, and unit cost.
  3. Save — the barcode is linked to the new record and the scanner continues immediately.
POS Items registered here appear on the Retail Items category on the POS grid straight away and can be scanned at the till using the camera barcode scanner on mobile.

Quick reference

SituationWhat to do
Camera permission deniedOpen browser site settings and allow camera for this site, then refresh.
Barcode not scanningEnsure good lighting or toggle the Torch button. Hold the camera 10–20 cm from the barcode.
Wrong quantity receivedTap the quantity field on the receipt line and correct it before saving.
Duplicate scanA 2-second cooldown prevents double-counting. Quantity increments automatically if you scan the same barcode twice.
Scanner keeps closingEnable Keep open in the scanner footer — the camera stays on between scans.

Tips

Always void, never edit and re-save. The audit trail depends on it.
  • Set par levels on every ingredient — the system will warn when stock falls below the par.
  • Print the Variance Report at month-end and file it.
