Stock, Recipes and Orders

This guide covers keeping track of ingredients: receiving deliveries, recipes, buying, counting, wastage, batches and expiry, reports, and the Orders & Tabs page that lists restaurant orders. It also explains how every sale takes stock off the shelf.

Who uses it
Store keepers, chefs, managers, accountants.
Where to find it
Admin menu → Stock: Stock Overview, Ingredients, Recipes, Receive Stock, Buying, Stock Count, Wastage. Admin menu → Advanced: Batch Tracker, Suppliers, Purchase Orders, Stock Reports. Admin menu → Restaurant & Bar: Orders & Tabs.
Permission needed
See the table "Who can do what".
Only shown when
The Stock & Inventory module is switched on in Settings → Modules. Recipes and Tables also need the Restaurant Page module.

How a sale takes stock off the shelf

Stock only comes off for items that have a recipe (see recipes). An item with no recipe sells normally and shows Untracked on the till.

  • Food and drink on a tab or an order sent to a station: stock comes off when the Kitchen, Bar or Coffee Bar marks the line Ready, Served, or bumps the ticket. Firing the order does not take stock.
  • Drinks paid for at the till, or on a tab that is settled: the till marks Bar and Coffee Bar drinks as handed over, and stock comes off then.
  • Room service: stock comes off when the charge is posted to the guest's bill, at the moment the order is placed.
  • How much comes off: the recipe quantity for one portion, times the number sold, divided by the yield %. For example 150 g of chicken at 75% yield takes off 200 g.
  • Which batch: the oldest batch is used first. The ingredient's total and its average cost update.
  • If there is not enough: the station action is refused with "Stock check stopped this action: (ingredient) short by (amount) ..."
  • Put back: cancelling before work starts, voiding an order, and 86 of an item that was not started all put the stock back. A refund does not put stock back.
  • Other ways stock goes down: wastage, expired or recalled batches, approved stock count shortages, and manual adjustments by a manager.

What the Stock Overview shows

Open Admin menu → Stock → Stock Overview. It checks for expired batches each time you open it and removes them from stock.

  • A banner: either "All good! Inventory is healthy — no urgent alerts right now.", or Heads up / Action needed with what needs attention.
  • Cards: Settled Today, Open Tabs, Inventory Value, Wastage (30-day total, with today's figure). Each card opens the related page.
  • Needs Attention, in groups you can open: Out of Stock, Expiring Soon (within 3 days), Expired Batches Still Active, Running Low and Pending Reconciliation (room-service charges that were posted without stock being tracked).
  • Recipe Coverage for the Food menu and Drinks menu: how many items have a recipe. More coverage means more accurate stock and cost figures.
  • Top Items Sold Today, Most Wasted Ingredients (30 days), and quick links to Ingredients, Recipes, Batches, Orders, Wastage and Reports.

The buttons in Needs Attention (View, Review, Dispose, Process) open the matching page. The Order button on an out-of-stock or low item opens Orders & Tabs, not a purchase order. To buy stock use Buying.

How to add and change ingredients

Open Admin menu → Stock → Ingredients. Each ingredient keeps a running quantity and an average cost. Stock arrives in batches, each with its own expiry date.

Add an ingredient

  1. Click Add Ingredient.
  2. Fill in Name and Unit (both required). Units are Grams (g), Kilograms (kg), Millilitres (ml), Litres (L), Pieces (pcs), Slices, Teaspoons (tsp), Tablespoons (tbsp). Optionally set Category.
  3. Min quantity (low-stock threshold): when stock is at or below this, the ingredient shows Low.
  4. Default yield %: the usable part after trimming or cooking. 100 means no loss. It can be 0.1 to 100.
  5. For buying: Reorder point, Par level (order up to), Preferred supplier and Supplier lead time (days). Notes are optional.
  6. Click Save. You will see "Ingredient "Chicken breast" added."

Other actions on each row

  • Edit — change the details. "Ingredient updated."
  • Stock In — see the next section.
  • Batches — the batches for that ingredient. Use Edit Batch to fix an expiry date, alert days or notes ("Batch updated."). Use Discard to write off a batch as expired, recalled or wasted. You are asked "This will subtract the remaining quantity from your total stock and close the batch. Continue?" and then see "Wasted — batch closed and stock adjusted." (or "Expired ...", "Recalled ...").
  • Adjust — a manual correction, for Admin and Manager roles only. Choose Direction (Add stock (+) or Subtract stock (−)), Quantity and a Reason of at least 8 characters, then Save Adjustment. "Manual adjustment recorded." For anything else use a stock count.
  • Archive ("Archive this ingredient?") hides an ingredient: "Ingredient archived." Choose Archived in the status filter to find it, then Restore ("Ingredient restored.") or Delete ("Permanently delete? This cannot be undone."). An ingredient used in any recipe cannot be deleted.
  • Export to Excel downloads the list.

Use the category and status filters (Low, Critical, Expiring soon, Archived) to narrow the list. The Status column shows OK, Low, Expiring (within 7 days), Expired or Critical (nothing left).

How to receive stock by hand (Stock In)

  1. On the Ingredients page click Stock In on the row.
  2. Type Quantity received. Fill in Cost per unit, choose the Supplier (or — Other / one-off —), and set Expiry date and Alert me ___ days before expiry if the goods expire.
  3. Click Receive Stock. If the quantity is more than 5 times what you hold, or the expiry date is in the past, the page asks you to confirm.
  4. You will see "Stock received: 5.000 units. New avg cost: MWK 120.00/unit."

A new batch is created with the next batch number, and the ingredient's average cost is recalculated from the old stock and the new delivery.

How to receive a delivery with Receive Stock

Open Admin menu → Stock → Receive Stock. This page is built for phones and tablets and works with a camera or a USB barcode scanner.

  1. Click the Scanner: OFF button so it reads Scanner: ON. The Camera button and the box Type or scan barcode here… appear. The camera needs a secure (https) connection and your permission.
  2. Optionally type the supplier in Supplier name (optional) and check the date field (it starts as today).
  3. Scan each product. Each scan adds one pack to Scanned Items. Scan again to add another pack, or use the plus and minus buttons. Type the Cost per unit on each line (if you leave it blank the current average cost is used).
  4. If a barcode is not known, Unknown Barcode opens and asks What is this barcode for?:
    • Ingredient — choose the Ingredient, the Pack Size and a Pack Label, and click Save & Add to Batch.
    • Item for Sale — type the Item Name, the Sale Price and optionally a Category (blank uses "Retail Items"), and click Register on POS.
  5. Click Receive into Stock. You will see a message that the batches were received into stock. A line that could not be saved is listed: "2 received — not saved: (names)".

Each line becomes a new batch, like Stock In by hand. A barcode already linked to another item is refused: "This barcode is already registered to another ingredient."

How to build a recipe

A recipe says how much of each ingredient goes into one portion of a menu item. Open Admin menu → Stock → Recipes.

  1. On the left use All, Food, Drinks or No recipe, or Search menu items..., and click a menu item. Each shows its food cost %.
  2. On the right fill in the ingredient lines: Ingredient, Qty / portion (in the ingredient's own unit, for example 0.05 kg is 50 g) and Yield %. Click Add ingredient line for more lines. If an ingredient is missing click + Add new ingredient (without leaving page) and fill in Name, Category, Unit, Cost / unit.
  3. Watch Total recipe cost, Sale price, Food cost % and Gross margin / portion. Under 30% is healthy, 30 to 40% is acceptable, over 40% means re-price or rework.
  4. Click Save recipe. You will see "Recipe saved with 4 ingredient(s)." Saving with no ingredients removes the recipe: "Recipe removed (no ingredients)."
  5. To remove a recipe click Delete recipe. "Recipe deleted."

You can also open an item's recipe from the Menu page (Recipe button). The same screen shows Sell price, Plate cost and Food cost %.

How to add a supplier

  1. Open Admin menu → Advanced → Suppliers and click Add Supplier.
  2. Fill in Supplier name (required, must be unique), Contact person, Phone, Email, Lead time (days), Payment terms, Account ref, Address, Notes, and tick Active.
  3. Click Save Supplier. You will see "Supplier added." or "Supplier updated."

The list shows Items (ingredients that name this supplier as preferred) and Total purchased. The pencil icon edits a supplier. The power icon switches it on or off ("Toggle this supplier's status?", then "Supplier status updated.").

How to see what to buy (Buying)

Open Admin menu → Stock → Buying. It lists every ingredient at or below its reorder point (or its minimum quantity if no reorder point is set). Ingredients with neither set are not listed.

  1. Read the summary: Items to reorder, Supplier orders, Est. purchase value.
  2. Each supplier group lists On hand, Reorder pt, Par, On order, Suggested and Est. cost. Suggested is the par level (or twice the reorder point if no par is set) minus what you hold and minus what is already on open purchase orders.
  3. Click Create draft PO to turn the group into a draft purchase order. The button is off for the Unassigned group: give those ingredients a preferred supplier first.

If nothing is low the page says "Nothing to reorder — every tracked item is above its reorder point."

How to raise and receive a purchase order

Open Admin menu → Advanced → Purchase Orders. Orders move from draft to sent, then partly or fully received, then closed (or cancelled).

  1. Click New PO (or use Create draft PO from Buying). Choose the Supplier, Order date, Expected date and Notes, then Create draft. "Draft purchase order PO-... created."
  2. While it is a draft, use Add line: choose an Ingredient (or — Custom / non-stock —), a Description, Unit, Qty and Unit cost, then Add. "Line added." The bin icon removes a line.
  3. Click Mark as sent ("Mark this PO as sent to the supplier?"). It needs at least one line. "Purchase order marked as sent."
  4. When goods arrive, fill in Receive now (and Expiry) on each line you received, then click Receive stock ("Receive the entered quantities into stock?"). "Stock received against purchase order."
  5. The order becomes partly received until every line is complete, then received. A line cannot receive more than is outstanding.
  6. Click Close when finished (outstanding lines will not be received) or Cancel for a draft or sent order. Print prints the order.

Receiving creates stock batches linked to the supplier and the order. Lines for custom or non-stock items are recorded but add no stock. Once an order is received, closed or cancelled its lines cannot be changed.

How to do a stock count

Open Admin menu → Stock → Stock Count. Every shortage must be explained and approved by a manager.

  1. Under Start a Count choose the Scope: Full count (every ingredient), By category (then pick a Category) or Spot check (specific items) (pick ingredients with Ctrl/Cmd-click). Optionally add a Shift label and Notes.
  2. Click Start count. The system saves the quantity it expects for each ingredient at that moment. "Count ... started. Enter your physical readings."
  3. Enter the Actual Qty you counted for each line. The page shows Variance and Cost Impact as you type. For every shortage choose a Reason: Spillage, Expired, Staff meal, Sampling, Prep waste, Theft suspected, Correction or Other, and add Notes if useful.
  4. Click Save readings ("Count readings saved."). Use the filters to find shortage-only rows.
  5. Click Submit for approval ("Submit count for approval? Once submitted you cannot edit it."). "Count submitted for approval. Shortage cost: MWK ..."
  6. A manager or admin opens the count and clicks Approve & post variances ("Approve count and post variances to stock? This cannot be undone.") or Reject & send back (type a reason of at least 8 characters).
  7. On approval the differences are applied to stock, with the approver's name and time kept. "Count ... approved. Variances applied to stock." A rejected count shows the reason and can be reopened with Reopen for re-count.

The page also shows Theft Radar (last 30d), the ingredients losing stock to shortages or manual adjustments that no sale or wastage explains, and Recent Counts. Only the person who started a count, or a manager, can change its readings. Surpluses need no reason.

How to record wastage

  1. Open Admin menu → Stock → Wastage.
  2. Under Record wastage check the Date (today or earlier). On each row choose the ingredient (Select ingredient...), type the Quantity, and choose a reason: Spoiled, Breakage, Over-prepared, Customer return, Trim/peel waste, Burnt, Spilled or Other. Click Add row for more.
  3. Click Save wastage. You will see "3 wastage entry(ies) recorded."

The stock comes off at once at the real cost of the batches used. Recent entries lists date, ingredient, quantity, cost, reason and who recorded it. The cards at the top (This month wastage, Last month, Days since last entry) open a detail window. A banner reminds you if no wastage has been logged for some days.

How to deal with expiring and recalled batches

Open Admin menu → Advanced → Batch Tracker. Each time the page opens, active batches past their expiry date are removed from stock automatically: "3 batch(es) automatically marked as expired."

  1. Use the cards Expiring ≤ 3 days (with Value at risk), Expiring 4–7 days, Expiring 8–30 days and Total active batches, or the filters (ingredient, status Active, Depleted, Expired, Wasted, Recalled, All, and expiry range).
  2. For spoiled or damaged stock click Mark Wasted, give a Reason, and click Confirm Wastage. "Batch marked as wasted." It is logged as wastage.
  3. For a supplier problem click Recall, give the Recall reason, and click Confirm Recall. "Batch recalled and removed from stock."
  4. Receive Stock at the top takes you to the delivery page.

How to read Stock Reports

Open Admin menu → Advanced → Stock Reports. Tabs: Inventory, Stock-In, Usage, Wastage, Yield, Adjustments, Expiry.

  • Inventory and Expiry are a snapshot of now. The other tabs cover a date range (the last 30 days to start with): set From and To and click Apply.
  • Every tab has a CSV download and a "how to read this report" hint with an example.
  • Columns: Inventory (Ingredient, Category, Current qty, Cost / unit, Stock value, Active batches); Stock-In (Date, Ingredient, Batch, Qty, Unit cost, Total, Supplier, By); Usage (Ingredient, Quantity used, Cost); Wastage (Date, Ingredient, Qty, Cost, Reason, By); Yield (Ingredient, Used, Wasted, Expired, Yield %, Loss cost); Adjustments (When, Ingredient, Source, change in quantity, Reason, By); Expiry (Batch, Ingredient, Expiry date, Days left, Remaining, Status).

How to use Orders & Tabs

Open Admin menu → Restaurant & Bar → Orders & Tabs (page title Restaurant Orders). Orders are taken at the POS Till, not on this page. This page is the office view of all orders.

  • Top cards: Orders Today, Revenue Today, Cash, Mobile Money, Card, Voids and Needs Review. Click a card to filter the list. End-of-Shift has a Run Count link to Stock Count.
  • Operational Insights: revenue and orders against yesterday, average ticket, live open orders (walk-in and room service), cash, mobile and card collected, voids today, peak hour and cashier totals. Click a card for detail.
  • Recent Orders table: Reference, Type, Customer, Table, Table Status, Room / Folio, Items, Total, Payment, Status, Health, Cashier, Created, Actions. Filter chips show what is applied, with Clear filters.

Buttons on each row:

  • Timeline — the order's full history. Items — the lines. Receipt — for paid, voided, cancelled and room-service orders. Review — for orders whose items, payments and room charges do not balance.
  • Open and Settle — on an open order. Settle asks for the payment method (Cash, Mobile Money or Card (Manual / Slip)) and the same details as at the till. "Order ORD-... settled — MWK ... via cash." It is not offered for room-service orders, and a part-paid (split) tab must be finished at the till.
  • Cancel ("Cancel unpaid order and restore stock?") — an unpaid order that nobody has started.
  • Void — Admin and Manager roles only, for open or paid orders. A reason of at least 8 characters is required. "Order ORD-... voided. Stock restored. Reason logged."
  • Reconcile (in the review window) — Admin and Manager roles only. "Order reconciled: ..."

The Receipt page can be printed, saved as PDF or emailed to the guest. Manual consolidation on it lets a manager add a discount (with a reason of at least 4 characters) or a service charge before payment. Once paid, totals are final.

Rules the system enforces

  • Stock never goes below what exists: wastage, adjustments and station actions are refused if there is not enough.
  • Manual adjustments are for Admin and Manager roles only and need a reason of at least 8 characters.
  • Every stock count shortage needs a reason and a manager's approval. The approved count cannot be undone.
  • An ingredient used in a recipe cannot be deleted; archive it instead.
  • Expired batches are removed from stock automatically when the Stock Overview or Batch Tracker opens.
  • A purchase order's lines can only change while it is a draft. Receiving is only for sent or partly received orders. You cannot receive more than is outstanding.
  • Supplier names must be different from each other.
  • Wastage cannot be dated in the future.
  • A paid order's totals cannot be changed. Use refund or void.
