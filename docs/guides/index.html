Open in browser

Universal sign-in

  1. Open the browser and go to /admin/login.php.
  2. Enter your username and password.
  3. After 5 wrong attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes.
  4. Always Sign Out at end of shift — every action is logged per user.

Help mode (POS / KDS / BDS / CDS)

  • A Help pill is pinned to the bottom-right of the screen.
  • Tap it to turn on Help Mode — yellow ? markers appear next to every button.
  • Hover (or long-press on touchscreens) any button to read what it does.
  • Press the ? key on a keyboard to toggle Help mode.
  • Setting is remembered per browser.

Offline mode

Stations keep working when the internet drops. Place orders normally; they are queued locally with a unique ID. The moment connectivity returns, the queue uploads automatically and a green confirmation appears.

Where to ask for help

  • If a button does nothing, refresh the page (Ctrl+R / pull-to-refresh).
  • If the screen looks broken, sign out and back in.
  • For stuck orders or stock counts, escalate to the manager — never delete data.
