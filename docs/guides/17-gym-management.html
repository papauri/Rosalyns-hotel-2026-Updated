Guide 17 · Gym Staff / Manager

Gym Management

From a website inquiry to a scanned check-in: packages, members, barcode cards, attendance, renewal reminders and reports.

URL /admin/gym-members.php Role gym_staff · manager · admin Read time 10 min

The gym pipeline at a glance

Stage Where What happens
1. Packages Gym Packages Define what you sell — name, price, and duration in days (30 = monthly, 365 = yearly, etc., which drives each member's expiry date). Tick Complimentary for a free package (e.g. hotel-guest access). Package prices are the accounting source of truth for member fees.
2. Inquiry Gym Inquiries A visitor fills the gym form on your website (package choices come from your live packages). The lead lands here with status new; you can also send quotations and record payments per inquiry.
3. Enrolment Gym Members Click Enrol on the inquiry — the enrolment form opens pre-filled and the lead is marked converted. The member gets a unique number (GM-XXXXXX) and their barcode card by email.
4. Visits Gym Check-In Members scan in and out with their emailed barcode. Attendance feeds the analytics.
5. Retention Gym Members / Reports Renewal reminders go out automatically before expiry; segments and peak-hour data drive your marketing.

Members register gym

Each row is one person with a live membership picture: status, package, a colour-coded days-to-expiry pill (red ≤ 7 days, amber inside the reminder window, green beyond), visit frequency over the last 30 days, their personal peak workout slot, and a marketing segment chip.

  • Enrolment is package-driven. Pick a package from the dropdown and it fills in the membership name, the fee, and — from the start date plus the package's duration — the expiry date automatically. You choose the start date; the system works out when it ends (monthly = +30 days, yearly = +365, a 5-day pass = +5, and so on). No more picking a from/to range by hand.
  • Fees follow the package. The price comes straight from the package; only a manager (gym_financials) can override the fee or the auto expiry, or enrol a "Custom" member without a package. Ordinary staff must always pick a package.
  • Free entry for hotel guests. When the gym is part of a hotel (not the standalone Gym preset), a Complimentary option appears — enrol a staying guest with zero fee. You can also mark any package itself as complimentary in Gym Packages so it's always free.
  • Names are protected against churn. Renaming a member needs a manager, requires a typed reason (recorded in the log), and is rate-limited — no more than two name changes per 180 days unless an administrator overrides. Memberships belong to one person and are not transferable, so name edits are for genuine legal corrections only.
  • Full change history. Every enrolment, edit, name change, reprice, status change and deletion is written to an audit log. Managers with Gym Member History (gym_logs) see a History button on each member showing who changed what, when, and the before/after values. Deleting a member also needs gym_financials — mark them cancelled instead to keep their record.
  • Resend card re-emails the member's barcode card any time.
Segments explained: Loyal regular (12+ visits / 30 days — reward them), At risk — 14d+ absent (used to visit, has stopped — reach out before they lapse), Win-back — lapsed (membership over — send a comeback offer), New member (joined under 2 weeks — onboard them), On hold (suspended).

Barcode check-in gym_checkin

On enrolment every member with an email receives their digital membership card — a CODE-128 barcode of their member number, embedded in the email and attached as an image they can save to their phone.

  1. Open Gym Check-In at reception (works on phones, tablets and desktops).
  2. Scan the member's barcode with a USB scanner, the camera button, or type the member number manually.
  3. Green card = checked in (with their expiry date; amber note if it is within 7 days). Scanning again on the way out checks them out and shows the visit duration.
  4. Red card = refused — expired or suspended memberships cannot check in.
Pass-sharing guard: a member can check in at most 2 times per day (setting gym_max_daily_visits). A third scan is refused with a warning and logged — verify the person at the desk actually matches the membership.
Forgot to scan out? Anyone still marked "in the gym" from a previous day is automatically checked out at closing time (setting gym_close_time, default 22:00) — the next morning's scan is always a fresh check-in and the "In gym now" count stays truthful.

Fitness report per member

Click the Visits count on any member row to open their personal fitness report:

  • Headline stats — total visits, last 30 / 7 days, average and longest session, lifetime gym hours, current & best attendance streak, and cost per visit (total paid ÷ visits) — a concrete renewal talking point.
  • Training habits — a part-of-day breakdown (early / morning / midday / afternoon / evening) showing when this member actually trains.
  • Monthly momentum — a 6-month visit trend so you can spot members losing steam before they lapse.
  • Weekly consistency — a bar per week for the last 8 weeks.
  • Peak training time — their hour-by-hour histogram and weekday preference (e.g. "Evenings 18:00–20:00 · mostly Tue/Thu") — ideal for targeted class invites and offers.
  • The full visit log with in/out times and durations, paginated 10 per page so long histories stay quick to browse.

Renewal reminders

The Renewal Reminders strip on Gym Members controls automatic expiry emails:

  1. Switch reminders on and choose how many days before expiry to send (1–30, default 3). Save.
  2. Set the daily scheduler once: run scripts/gym_membership_reminders.php from cron (the file header shows the exact crontab line). No cron? Use the Send due reminders now button — it does the same sweep.
  3. Each member gets exactly one warm renewal email per expiry — never duplicates, even if the sweep runs twice. A renewal (new expiry date) makes them remindable again.

Calendar & slot booking gym

An optional booking calendar that lets members plan their visit and keeps the floor from getting overcrowded. Turn it on in Gym Schedule → Slot Booking Settings; it is off by default.

  • Set your opening hours. On the Gym Schedule admin page, set open/close times for each weekday (untick a day to close it). The system divides each open day into time slots automatically.
  • Set the slot rules. Capacity per slot (how many people are allowed on the floor at once), slot length in minutes (default 60), and how many days ahead members can book (default 7).
  • Members book from their phones — no login. When enabled, a "View gym schedule" button appears on the public gym page linking to gym-schedule.php. There, anyone sees a clear day-by-day calendar showing how full each hour is, and reserves a slot with just their name and a phone or email (member number optional). Full slots can't be over-booked — capacity is enforced.
  • See the day at a glance (admin). Gym Schedule shows every hour of the chosen day with a fill meter and the list of who's booked in. Managers (gym_packages) can mark a reservation attended or cancel it; anyone with gym access can view the day.
Opening hours and slot capacity are stored live and take effect immediately on the public page. Classes you've set up under Gym Packages / classes show up against their hour on both the public and admin calendars.

Gym Reports gym_reports

The date-filtered operations report for the owner: members by status, new joiners and churn, expiring soon, unpaid members with total outstanding, attendance analytics (busiest check-in and check-out hours, busiest weekday, average visit duration, daily trend) and the gym revenue strip. Gym payments also appear across the normal finance pages — payments, receipts, invoices — labelled as memberships.
