Gym Management

This guide covers everything for the gym at Rosalyns Beach Hotel: the public booking form, inquiries, the member register, the check-in scanner, the schedule and slot bookings, classes, packages and gym reports.

Who uses it
Gym front-desk staff, managers
Where to find it
Admin menu → Gym → Members, Check-In, Inquiries, Schedule. Admin menu → Advanced → Gym Packages, Gym Classes, Gym Reports. (The Advanced group starts folded — click its name to open it.)
Permission needed
Gym Inquiries — Members, Inquiries, Schedule and Gym Classes pages.
Gym Check-In Scanner — Check-In page.
Manage Gym Packages — Gym Packages page, and the edit controls on Schedule and Gym Classes.
Gym Reports — Gym Reports page.
Gym Invoicing & Pricing — money and manager actions (see the rules below).
Gym Member History — the change history of a member.
Only shown when
The Gym & Fitness module is switched on in Settings → Modules.

Each member has a number like GM-A1B2C3. The member's barcode on the emailed membership card is this number. An inquiry (a request) is not a member until you enrol it.

How guests send a gym booking request

You do not do this yourself, but you need to know what guests see. On the public gym page the guest clicks a Book Package, Book a Class or Book a Session button. A form titled Request a Session opens.

  1. The guest fills in Full Name, Email and Phone (all required).
  2. They can enter Guests (1 to 10), then pick Preferred Date and Preferred Time (both required; the date cannot be in the past).
  3. They choose Select Package from the list of active packages with their prices. If there are no active packages, the only choice is Custom Request.
  4. They may write Fitness Goals / Notes (up to 1000 characters).
  5. They tick "I agree to be contacted about this booking request." The Send Booking Request button stays greyed out until they do.
  6. The guest is taken to a page headed Wellness Booking Received with a reference like GYM-1A2B3C4D. They also get a confirmation email, and the hotel gets a notification email.

The request appears in Gym → Inquiries with status New. The guest is told the team will contact them within 24 hours. A guest can send at most 5 requests in 10 minutes.

How to work an inquiry

  1. Open Gym → Inquiries. The tabs at the top filter by status: All, New, Contacted, Converted, Closed, Cancelled, each with a count.
  2. To find one, type in the search box ("Search by name, email, phone, or reference...") and click Search. Click Clear to go back.
  3. Click View on a row. The Gym Inquiry Details window shows reference, name, email, phone, membership type, preferred date and time, number of people, status, consent, the guest's message and the money figures.
  4. To contact the guest by email, click Email on the row (or Email Member in the details window). Fill in Subject and Message (both required; subject is cut at 200 characters) and click Send Email. You will see "Message emailed to name@example.com."
  5. When you have spoken to the guest, change the status in the Status column. The choices are New, Contacted, Confirmed (paid), Converted, Closed / Completed and Cancelled. The change is saved straight away.
    • Contacted: the guest is emailed. You will see "Inquiry marked as Contacted. Notification email sent to ..."
    • Converted: the guest gets a welcome email. You will see "Inquiry converted to member. Welcome email sent to ..."
    • Any other change shows "Inquiry status updated successfully!"
  6. You can also use the buttons in the details window: Confirm (only for New inquiries; asks "Confirm this gym membership?"; emails the guest; shows "Gym membership confirmed successfully! Confirmation email sent.") and Mark Completed (only for Confirmed inquiries; asks "Mark this membership as completed?"; shows "Gym membership marked as completed!").
  7. To keep private notes, type in the "Internal notes..." box in the details window and click Save Notes. You will see "Notes updated successfully!"
  8. To turn the guest into a member, click the green Enrol button on the row (not shown for cancelled inquiries). The Members page opens with the name, email, phone and package already filled in. Go on to How to enrol a member.

If the inquiry has a required deposit that is not fully paid, Confirm, Mark Completed and the Confirmed (paid) and Converted statuses are blocked with "Required gym deposit has not been fully paid yet."

How to quote, invoice and record payment for an inquiry

You need the Gym Invoicing & Pricing permission. Without it you do not see the three controls below, and if you try you get "You do not have permission to handle gym invoicing or pricing."

  1. Click View on the inquiry. Look at the Accounting part: Total Amount, Amount Paid, Amount Due and Payment Status.
  2. Type the price (for example 30000) in Set Total Amount and click Save Amount. You will see "Total amount updated successfully!" The amount must be a number, 0 or more.
  3. To send a price quote first, type the number of days it stays valid (default 7) and click Send Quotation. A PDF quotation is emailed to the guest. You will see "Gym membership quotation sent to name@example.com." The system also tries to send it by WhatsApp and adds "WhatsApp delivered." when that works.
  4. When the guest pays in the gym, click Record Payment & Send Invoice. The button is greyed out until a total amount is set.
  5. The system records a payment of the outstanding balance as cash, issues a receipt, and emails the invoice. You will see "Payment recorded successfully! Invoice sent to name@example.com".

This button always records a cash payment for the full amount still owed. It cannot be used for card, mobile money or part payments. For those, record the payment on the Payments screen instead (see the finance guide). If the inquiry is already fully paid, the button only resends the invoice: "Payment already recorded. Invoice resent to ..."

How to cancel or delete an inquiry

  1. To cancel, click View, then Cancel Membership. Read the question: "Cancel this membership? The bill is voided; any payment already recorded stays as credit owed back (refund it from the Payments screen)." Confirm.
  2. You will see "Gym membership cancelled successfully! Cancellation email sent." If money had been paid, the message adds how much stays as credit owed back. Refund it from the payment's Refund screen.
  3. To delete a mistaken inquiry, click Delete on its row and confirm "Are you sure you want to delete this inquiry?" You will see "Gym inquiry deleted successfully!"

A cancelled inquiry cannot be reopened. The guest must send a new request. An inquiry that has any payment recorded cannot be deleted — cancel it instead.

How to enrol a member

  1. Open Gym → Members and click Enrol Member. (If you came from an inquiry, the window is already open and filled in.)
  2. Type Full name (required, up to 255 characters).
  3. Optionally fill in Email and Phone. If you give an email, it must be valid.
  4. Choose Membership package. Each choice shows the package name, its length and its price, for example Monthly · 30 days · MWK30,000. Free packages show (Free). The fee and the expiry date fill in by themselves.
  5. If the bookings module is on, a tick box Complimentary — free entry for a hotel guest is also shown. Tick it for a free membership. The fee becomes 0.
  6. Check Start date (today by default). Expiry is worked out from the start date and the package length. A package with no length has no expiry.
  7. Leave Status as Active. Add Notes if you need to.
  8. Click Save Member. You will see "Member enrolled — GM-A1B2C3. Membership card emailed." The card goes out only if you gave an email. If the email fails you see "(Card email failed: ...)" but the member is still saved.

If the member came from an inquiry, that inquiry is marked Converted automatically. One inquiry can be enrolled only once: a second try shows "This inquiry is already enrolled as member GM-A1B2C3."

Staff without the Gym Invoicing & Pricing permission can only enrol against a real package. The Expiry and Fee (MWK) boxes are greyed out for them. A manager with that permission also sees the choice Custom (manager) — set fee & dates manually, and can type a different fee and expiry date.

How to edit a member, renew, suspend or reactivate

The member list shows tabs All, Active, Expiring ≤30d and Expired with counts. Members expiring first are listed first. Under each expiry date a small label shows 30d left, Expires today or Lapsed 4d ago.

  1. Find the member. Click the pen button (Edit) on their row. The window is titled Edit Member.
  2. Change what you need and click Save Member. You will see "Member updated."
  3. To renew, change Start date or choose the package again so the expiry date moves forward, and make sure Status is Active. A manager can also type the new Expiry date directly.
  4. To pause a member, click the pause button (Suspend) on their row. You will see "Member marked suspended."
  5. To bring them back, click the play button (Reactivate). You will see "Member marked active."

If you only change contact details, the expiry date stays as it was, as long as the package and start date are unchanged. Status can be Active, Expired, Suspended or Cancelled. Use Cancelled instead of deleting, so the history is kept.

Changing a name. A membership belongs to one person and cannot be passed on. Only a manager (with Gym Invoicing & Pricing) can change a name, and only to correct a legal name. When you change the name and click Save Member, a box appears asking for Reason for name change (at least 5 characters, for example Legal name correction — marriage). Type it and click Save Member again. Non-administrators can change a given member's name at most twice in 180 days.

Visit history. Click the number in the Visits column. A window titled Fitness Report — member name opens with total visits, visits in the last 30 and 7 days, average and longest session, streak, peak training time, and a list of visits 10 per page (« Prev / Next »).

Change history. If you have Gym Member History, the edit window shows View change history. It lists every enrolment, update, name change, status change and deletion with who did it and when.

How to resend a membership card

  1. On the member's row, click the envelope button (Resend membership card email). It only shows when the member has an email address.
  2. The card with their barcode is emailed again. If the member has no email you see "This member has no email address on file."

How to set up renewal reminders

  1. On Gym → Members, click Renewal reminders to unfold the box.
  2. Tick Email members and type how many days before expiry to send (day(s) before expiry, from 1 to 30).
  3. Click Save. You will see "Renewal reminders on — members are emailed 3 day(s) before expiry." (with your number of days), or "Renewal reminders switched off."
  4. To send now instead of waiting, click Send due reminders now. You will see for example "Checked 4 due membership(s): 3 reminder(s) sent, 1 already reminded." It is safe to click more than once; a member is not reminded twice.

When no reminder has been sent yet, the line on the right of the box says reminders go out automatically (set up under Automated Emails). It also shows when the last reminder was sent. If reminders are switched off, Send due reminders now shows "Reminders are switched off — enable them first."

How to remove a member

  1. Prefer setting the member's Status to Cancelled. This keeps their history.
  2. To delete for good, a manager clicks the bin button (Delete) on the row and confirms Delete member "name"? This cannot be undone. with Yes, continue.
  3. You will see "Member deleted."

Without the Gym Invoicing & Pricing permission you get "Deleting a member requires a manager. Mark them cancelled instead to preserve their history."

How to check members in and out

Open Gym → Check-In. The top bar shows how many people are in gym, visits today and active members.

  1. Use a USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner while the Scanner: ON button is on (it is on by default). Scan the barcode on the member's card. You do not need to click in a box first.
  2. Or click Camera to scan with the device camera. Allow camera access if asked. The torch (flash) button shows if the camera supports it. The camera only works on a secure (https) address.
  3. Or type the member number (for example GM-A1B2C3) into the box and click Check (or press Enter).
  4. Read the big result card. A green card "Welcome name — membership expires Dec 31, 2026." means checked in. An amber card adds "membership expires soon!" when expiry is within 7 days. A blue card "Goodbye name — visit duration 1h 20m." means checked out. A beep and a short vibration confirm each result.
  5. Scanning the same card again checks the member out.

The lists below show Currently in the gym (who, since when, how long) and Today's activity (each visit; IN GYM or the length of the visit). They refresh every minute.

Click Scanner: ON to turn it off (then it shows Scanner: OFF). The camera stays shut for 3 seconds on the same barcode so one scan is not counted twice.

What to do when someone forgot to scan out

  1. On Gym → Check-In, find the person under Currently in the gym.
  2. Click Out next to their name. They are checked out and the list updates.
  3. If nobody does this, the system closes any visit still open from a previous day automatically at the gym closing time, 22:00 (fixed — there is no screen to change it), so yesterday's visit never turns today's arrival into a check-out.

A member who is already inside can always check out, even if their membership has lapsed or been suspended since they came in.

How to use the Gym Schedule and slot bookings

The Gym → Schedule page shows one day at a time. Guests reserve slots on the public page Plan Your Workout (gym-schedule page on the website) when slot booking is switched on. If it is off, guests see "Slot booking isn't available" and are asked to contact the front desk.

  1. Open Gym → Schedule. The title bar shows Booked today: N (or Booked this day) and Booking ON or Booking OFF. Public page opens the guest page in a new tab.
  2. Move between days with « Prev, Next », the date box, or Today.
  3. Each row is an hour slot with a bar and a count like 12 / 20. A full slot shows FULL. Class names running in that slot are listed. Under it are the people booked in, with ×2 after the name for a party of 2. Hover on a name to see phone, email and reference.
  4. When a booked person arrives, click the tick button (Mark attended) next to their name. To release their place, click the cross button (Cancel). You need Manage Gym Packages for both.

You will see "Marked attended." or "Reservation cancelled." If the gym is closed on that weekday you see "The gym is closed on Monday (set opening hours below to open it)."

What guests do on the public page: they click a day, then Reserve on a slot (slots that have gone show Passed; full ones show Fully booked). In Reserve your slot they enter Your name, Phone (or email), Email (optional), Member no. (optional), People, and click Confirm reservation. They see "You're booked in!" with a reference.

A cancelled reservation cannot be switched back ("This reservation is cancelled and cannot be changed."). The guest has to book again.

How to set opening hours and slot rules

These two boxes appear at the bottom of Gym → Schedule only if you have Manage Gym Packages.

  1. In Slot Booking Settings, tick Enable public slot booking to show the schedule page and reserve buttons on the gym site.
  2. Set Capacity per slot (maximum people at once, 1 to 1000), Slot length (minutes) (15 to 240) and Bookable days ahead (0 to 60).
  3. Click Save settings. You will see "Slot booking settings saved."
  4. In Opening Hours, tick each weekday the gym is open and set its opening and closing times.
  5. Click Save hours. You will see "Opening hours saved."

Closing time must be after opening time, otherwise you get "Closing time must be after opening time for Tuesday." and nothing is saved.

How to manage gym classes

Open Advanced → Gym Classes. Each class appears on its own card (hidden classes are shown faded with a Hidden badge). The same classes show on the public gym page.

  1. Click Add Class (needs Manage Gym Packages).
  2. Fill in Class title (required, up to 150 characters), Description (optional, up to 500), Days (required, for example Tuesday & Thursday) and Time (required, for example 7:00 AM).
  3. Choose Level (you can type your own, or pick All Levels, Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) and Order (lower numbers first).
  4. Leave Active — show on the public schedule ticked to show it to guests. Untick to hide it.
  5. Click Save Class. You will see "Class created."
  6. To change a class, click Edit on its card, change it and click Save Class: "Class updated."
  7. To remove a class, click the bin button and confirm Delete class "name"? Enrolments are removed too. This cannot be undone. You will see "Class deleted."

How to enrol members in a class and send reminders

  1. On the class card click Roster (or the N enrolled badge). Anyone with the Gym Inquiries permission can look at the roster.
  2. To add someone, pick them in "— Add an active member —" (members with no email are marked no email) and click Enrol. You will see "name enrolled."
  3. To take someone out, click the remove button next to their name. You will see "Member removed from class."
  4. To email everyone enrolled, click Remind on the card, or Send reminder to all in the roster. Confirm. You will see for example "5 reminders sent · 1 skipped (no email)." If nobody is enrolled: "No enrolled members to remind."

Enrolling, removing, editing, deleting and reminding need Manage Gym Packages. Otherwise you get "You do not have permission to manage classes." Only members with an active, unexpired membership can be enrolled.

How to manage gym packages

Open Advanced → Gym Packages (needs Manage Gym Packages). Packages appear as cards with price, length, what is included and an Active or Inactive label. The page has View Inquiries and Add Package buttons.

  1. Click Add Package. The window is titled Add Gym Package.
  2. Fill in Package Name (required, up to 150 characters, must be unique) and Price (required, 0 or more). Currency Code is MWK by default.
  3. Enter Duration (days), for example 30 for monthly, 365 for yearly. This decides the member's expiry date. Leave blank or 0 for no expiry. Duration Label is the display text; if you leave it empty it is filled from the days (for example Monthly).
  4. Tick Complimentary (free — e.g. hotel guest) for a free package. The price is ignored and no fee is recorded.
  5. Type What's Included, one item per line. Set Display Order (lower first). Icon Class, CTA Button Text and CTA Link change how the package appears on the public gym page.
  6. Tick Active to offer it, and Featured to highlight it.
  7. Click Add Package. You will see "Gym package added successfully!"
  8. To change one, click Edit Package, then Save Changes: "Gym package updated successfully!"
  9. On a card, the toggle button switches Activate / Deactivate, and the star button switches Mark as featured / Unfeature.
  10. To delete, click the bin button and confirm Delete "name"? This cannot be undone. The system answers "Package deleted."

Deactivate a package instead of deleting it if you only want to stop offering it. If Facebook posting is switched on and Show share button on Gym packages is ticked in Facebook Settings, each card also has a Share on Facebook button and the page has Share All Packages. See the Facebook guide.

How to read Gym Reports

  1. Open Advanced → Gym Reports (needs Gym Reports).
  2. Choose the period at the top right: Today, 7 days, 30 days or 90 days. The dates are printed under the title.
  3. Read the first row of boxes: Members (total, with active, suspended and expired), New Members (joined in the period), Expiring ≤30 Days (link Renewal follow-ups opens the Members page filtered to those) and Outstanding (money owed, number of unpaid accounts, and members lost in the period).
  4. Read the second row: Check-Ins and unique members, Avg Visit and busiest day, In Gym Now (link to the scanner), and Gym Revenue (payments in the period, link ledger to Payments).
  5. Below are Busiest Check-In Hours, Busiest Check-Out Hours and Daily Visits — Last 14 Days (check-ins, unique members, average duration).
  6. Unpaid / Outstanding Balances lists each unpaid account with Total, Paid and Due. Click Follow up to go to Inquiries. If everyone has paid you see "No outstanding membership balances — everyone is paid up."

Rules the system enforces

  • A member can check in only if their status is Active, the expiry date has not passed, and the start date has arrived. Otherwise the card is red.
  • A member can check in at most 2 times a day (fixed — there is no screen to change it). A third scan is refused as possible pass sharing and is logged. The person at the desk must check the face matches the membership.
  • Scanning a member who is still checked in always checks them out.
  • Memberships are not transferable. Only a manager with Gym Invoicing & Pricing can rename a member, with a written reason, and the change is recorded.
  • Staff without Gym Invoicing & Pricing cannot type a price or an expiry date, cannot enrol without a package, cannot delete a member and cannot edit an inquiry's amounts, send quotations or invoices.
  • Every change to a member (enrolment, edits, status, deletion) is written to the member's change history.
  • Two packages cannot have the same name.
  • A cancelled inquiry cannot be reopened and cannot have its amount changed. An inquiry with payments cannot be deleted.
  • Cancelling an inquiry does not refund automatically (unless your refund settings say so). What was paid stays as credit owed back.
  • Only active members with an unexpired membership can be put in a class.
