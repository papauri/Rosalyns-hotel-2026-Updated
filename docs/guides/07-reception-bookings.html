Guide 07 · Front desk

Reception & Bookings

Complete front-desk playbook: walk-ins, online bookings, check-in, folio charges, payments, invoices, refunds, room assignment, tentative holds, blocked dates, and everything in between.

URLs /admin/bookings.php · /admin/create-booking.php Role receptionist · manager · admin Read time ~12 min

Booking statuses

Every booking moves through a defined lifecycle. Only valid transitions are allowed — the system blocks illegal jumps (e.g. you cannot check out a cancelled booking).

pending Initial state when a guest submits the online form. Awaiting admin confirmation. Room not yet reserved.
tentative Temporary hold with an expiry countdown (default 48 h). Room held for the guest but not confirmed. Auto-expires if not converted.
confirmed Booking locked in. Room availability decremented. Confirmation email sent to guest.
checked-in Guest has arrived. Room marked occupied. Folio charges begin accumulating here.
checked-out Stay complete. Room returned to cleaning status. Final invoice generated.
cancelled Booking voided. Room availability restored. Cancellation email sent. Audit trail preserved.
no-show Guest never arrived past the check-in date. Triggers auto-refund rules if configured. No-show email sent.
Payment statuses are separate from booking statuses: unpaid · partial · paid · completed · pending · failed · refunded · partially_refunded

Create a booking — walk-in or phone reservation

Go to /admin/create-booking.php or tap + New booking on the bookings list.

  1. Room & occupancy
    Pick the room type. If multiple physical rooms exist for that type, optionally assign an individual room now — or leave it for later (auto-assign happens when the booking is paid). Select occupancy tier: Single / Double / Triple. Each tier can have its own nightly rate set in Room Management.
  2. Guest counts
    Enter adults and children (under 12). Children are charged at a configurable multiplier (e.g. 50% of the adult nightly rate). The system enforces capacity limits per room.
  3. Dates
    Pick check-in and check-out dates. The system calculates nights automatically. Blocked dates and already-booked dates are unavailable.
  4. Guest details
    Full name (required), email (required), phone (required), country, address, special requests.
  5. Booking status
    • Pending — online submission, needs confirmation.
    • Tentative — holds the room for the configured expiry period (default 48 h) pending deposit or confirmation.
    • Confirmed — reserves immediately.
    • Checked-in — use this for a walk-in who is occupying the room right now.
  6. Payment status & method
    Choose unpaid or paid. If paid, select the payment method (cash, card, mobile money, bank transfer, cheque, other) and an optional transaction reference.
  7. Price override (optional)
    The system auto-calculates the total using: nightly rate × nights + child supplement ± rate plan discount + tourism levy. You can override the total manually if needed (e.g. a negotiated rate).
  8. Admin notes — internal memo visible only to staff (e.g. "Phone booking — guest requested early check-in").
  9. Send confirmation email — tick to auto-send the booking confirmation to the guest on save.

How the price is calculated

Component Detail
Base rate Nightly rate for selected occupancy tier × number of nights.
Child supplement nightly_rate × (child_multiplier%) × num_children × nights
Rate plan discount If a rate plan is active, it adjusts the nightly rate. See Guide 09.
Tourism levy Optional percentage (default 1%) added to the room subtotal.
VAT Configurable percentage applied to the total if VAT is enabled.
Rates & packages are configured separately in Guide 09 (Packages & Rate Plans). Rate plans can lower or raise the nightly rate on specific date ranges.

Booking details page — actions & tabs

Click any booking reference to open it. All management happens here.

Status action buttons

Buttons shown depend on the current status:

Current status Available actions
pending Confirm — locks the booking in, sends confirmation email, decrements availability.
tentative Convert to Confirmed · Cancel
confirmed Assign Room · Check In · No-Show · Cancel
checked-in Check Out · Cancel Check-in (reverts to confirmed)
checked-out · cancelled · no-show Read-only — no further status transitions.
Check-in prerequisites: payment must be at least partial, an individual room must be assigned, and the check-in date must be today or in the past. All three must be true before the Check-in button is enabled.

Quick actions (always available)

  • Edit Booking — full edit form (dates, guest info, room, price).
  • Extend Stay — move check-out forward; system recalculates charges and creates a pending additional payment if more is owed.
  • Adjust Dates — change both check-in and check-out; recalculates delta and creates payment if owed.
  • Assign Room — pick or change the specific individual room.
  • Resend Email — resend any template (booking received, confirmed, cancelled, invoice) with optional CC recipients.
  • Add Charge — post an extra item to the folio.
  • Add Payment — record a new payment against this booking.
  • Generate / Resend Invoice — create or email the PDF invoice.

Folio & charges

The folio is the running tab for a guest's stay. Everything billable posts here.

Charge types

room room_service food drink minibar laundry breakfast service custom other

Adding a charge

  1. Tap Add Charge on the folio tab.
  2. Select charge type, enter description, quantity, and unit price.
  3. The system calculates the line total and applies VAT if enabled.
  4. Save — the charge posts immediately to the folio and the balance due updates.

Adding a menu item directly to the folio

  1. Tap Add Menu Item.
  2. Select food or drink category, then item, then quantity.
  3. The item price is pulled from the menu — no manual entry needed.
  4. Stock is deducted when the KDS station marks it as served.

Voiding a charge

  1. Click the void icon next to the charge.
  2. Enter a reason (required).
  3. The charge is soft-deleted — it shows as struck-through with the void reason, never truly deleted for audit purposes.

Folio summary

Line How it's calculated
Room base Booking total_amount (room + child supplement).
Packages total Sum of all package add-ons on the booking.
Extras total Sum of all non-voided folio charges.
Subtotal Room base + packages + extras.
VAT Subtotal × VAT rate (if enabled).
Grand total Subtotal + VAT.
Amount paid Sum of all completed payments.
Balance due Grand total − amount paid.

Check in a guest

  1. Open the booking (search by name or reference).
  2. Verify: booking is confirmed, an individual room is assigned, and at least a deposit has been received.
  3. Tap Check In. If the room has a children restriction but the booking includes children, you'll be asked for an override note.
  4. Status changes to checked-in. The individual room status moves to occupied.
  5. Room service and restaurant charges will now post automatically to this folio.
For walk-ins, set the booking status to Checked-in directly at creation — no need to create as confirmed first.

Room assignment

A booking can exist without a specific individual room assigned. Assignment can happen:

  • Automatically — when a payment is recorded and the booking is confirmed.
  • Manually — via the Assign Room button on the details page.
  • At creation — if you pick an individual room in the create form.

The assignment picker shows only available rooms of the correct type on the booking dates. Rooms in maintenance, out-of-order, or already booked on those dates are excluded.

You can reassign a room anytime before check-out — the old room is freed and the new one is reserved.

Recording a payment

  1. Open the booking → Payments tab → Add Payment.
  2. Select payment method: cash · mobile money · credit card · debit card · bank transfer · cheque · other.
  3. Enter amount, payment date, and an optional transaction reference (e.g. bank ref or cheque number).
  4. Optionally add CC email recipients who should receive this invoice.
  5. Save — a receipt number is generated, an invoice PDF is created via TCPDF, and the email is sent to the guest (and any CC addresses).
Invoice numbering: sequential format INV-YYYY-NNNNNN. Each payment generates its own invoice. The final check-out generates a consolidated invoice.

Check out

  1. Open the booking → tap Check Out.
  2. The system presents the folio summary: room nights + all extras + VAT + tourism levy − payments received = balance due.
  3. If a balance is outstanding, take payment now (the Add Payment modal opens inline).
  4. Confirm check-out. The room status moves to cleaning. Status becomes checked-out.
  5. A final consolidated invoice is auto-generated as a PDF and emailed to the guest.

Invoices

All invoices live on the Invoices tab of the booking details page.

Action What it does
Generate Invoice Creates a PDF without sending — useful to review before sending.
Send Invoice Emails the PDF to the guest + any CC recipients.
Send via WhatsApp Sends the invoice download link via WhatsApp (requires WhatsApp integration enabled).
Regenerate Invoice Force-recreates the PDF — use if folio charges were added after the original was issued.

Issuing a refund

Always use the Refund button on the original payment — never manually adjust amounts or create a negative payment. Refunds must link back to their source payment.
  1. Payments tab → find the payment → tap Refund.
  2. Enter refund amount (up to the remaining refundable balance — prior refunds are already subtracted).
  3. Select reason: early checkout · cancellation · service issue · overpayment · other.
  4. Set refund status: pending (processing by provider) or completed (cash/bank already returned). For mobile money, keep as pending until the provider confirms.
  5. Save. The system:
    • Creates a refund record linked to the original payment.
    • Pro-rates VAT on the refunded amount.
    • Recalculates the booking's amount paid and balance due.
    • Marks the original payment as refunded if fully refunded.
    • Logs the action in the audit trail.

Tentative bookings — /admin/tentative-bookings.php

A tentative booking holds a room for a configurable period (default 48 h) while the guest arranges a deposit or confirmation. Use it for phone enquiries where the guest isn't ready to commit immediately.

How it works

  1. Create the booking with status set to Tentative. The expiry timer starts.
  2. An email is sent to the guest with a link to confirm or the hotel's payment details.
  3. The dedicated tentative page shows all holds with a countdown. Ones expiring within 24 h are flagged.
  4. When the guest pays or confirms → tap Convert to Confirmed. The booking locks in, room auto-assigns if paid, and a confirmation email is sent.
  5. If the guest doesn't confirm before expiry → tap Cancel. The room becomes available again. Expired tentatives cannot be converted — cancel and create a new booking if the guest comes back.
Expiry does not automatically cancel the booking — it only flags it. A manager must manually cancel expired tentatives to release the room.

No-show

If a confirmed guest fails to arrive after the check-in date:

  1. Open the booking → tap No-Show.
  2. The status changes to no-show.
  3. If a no-show auto-refund policy is configured in Booking Settings, a refund is triggered automatically.
  4. A no-show notification email is sent to the guest.
  5. The room is freed and the audit trail is updated.

Editing a booking — what can and cannot change

Can be edited

  • Guest name, email, phone, country
  • Check-in and check-out dates (nights recalculate)
  • Number of adults and children
  • Occupancy tier (single / double / triple)
  • Room type + individual room assignment
  • Special requests
  • Total amount (manual override)
  • Admin notes

Cannot be edited

  • Booking reference — immutable, used on all invoices and emails
  • Booking status — changed via status action buttons only
  • Payment status — updated automatically when payments are recorded
  • Amount paid / balance due — recalculated from the payments ledger
  • Created at — system-set timestamp
To change dates on a checked-in booking, use Extend Stay (moves check-out forward) or Adjust Dates (changes both). Both recalculate charges and create a pending payment if additional charges apply.

Blocked dates — /admin/blocked-dates.php

Block dates to prevent new bookings on specific rooms or across all rooms (e.g. maintenance, deep clean, private event).

Block scopes

Scope Effect
All room types No room can be booked on that date — hotel-wide close.
Room type All physical rooms of that type are blocked on the date.
Individual room Only one specific room is blocked — other rooms of the same type remain bookable.

Block types

manual · maintenance · event · fully_booked

Block a date range

  1. Tap Block Date Range.
  2. Choose scope (all / room type / individual room).
  3. Pick start and end date in the calendar picker.
  4. Select block type and optionally enter a reason.
  5. Save — every date in the range is blocked.
  6. To unblock: find the row in the list and tap Unblock.
Blocked dates prevent new bookings only. Existing confirmed bookings on those dates are unaffected.

Cancelling a booking

  1. Open the booking → tap Cancel.
  2. Choose a reason from the list (or type a custom reason).
  3. Confirm. The system:
    • Sets status to cancelled.
    • Restores the room's availability on all affected dates.
    • Sends a cancellation email to the guest.
    • Logs the action in the audit trail.
  4. If a refund is due, go to the Payments tab and issue it via the Refund button on the original payment.
Never manually delete a booking — always Cancel. Deletions destroy the financial and audit trail. Cancelled bookings remain visible as a permanent record.

Resending emails

  1. Open the booking → Resend Email.
  2. Choose the template: booking received · booking confirmed · tentative confirmed · booking cancelled · invoice.
  3. Optionally add CC recipients for this send only.
  4. Send. The action is logged in the activity timeline.

Common mistakes to avoid

Don't manually delete a booking — always Cancel. Deletes destroy the audit and financial trail.
  • Don't change dates by editing the booking while the guest is in-house — use Extend Stay or Adjust Dates so the price recalculates correctly.
  • Don't issue a refund by manually editing a payment amount — use the Refund button so the refund links back to its source payment.
  • Don't leave expired tentatives sitting — cancel them promptly so the room shows as available to other guests.
  • Don't skip the room assignment before check-in — if the system blocks check-in, check that an individual room is assigned.
  • Don't regenerate an invoice after the guest has a copy unless the folio has actually changed — this overwrites their PDF.

Daily reception checklist

Morning (start of shift)

  • Filter → Check-in today — confirm all arrivals are assigned a room and payment is on file.
  • Review tentative-bookings.php — convert any ready ones, cancel any expired ones.
  • Check calendar.php for the full picture of today's occupancy.

Afternoon (before close)

  • Filter → Check-out today — prepare folios, add any outstanding extras.
  • Process all check-outs and confirm final invoices were sent.
  • Review any no-show candidates for confirmed bookings from yesterday or earlier.

End of day

  • Verify all in-house guests show status checked-in and not confirmed — a confirmed status with today's check-in date means someone forgot to press Check In.
  • Confirm balance due is zero for all guests checking out tomorrow (chase payment today).

Take a walk-in / phone booking

  1. Tap + New booking (top-right).
  2. Pick the room type → if multiple physical rooms exist, pick the individual room.
  3. Occupancy: Single, Double, or Triple — price updates automatically.
  4. Enter adults and children counts. Children are charged the multiplier set on the room (default 50%).
  5. Pick check-in and check-out dates.
  6. Fill guest details: name, email, phone, country.
  7. Choose status:
    • Tentative → reserves the room for 48h pending deposit/confirmation.
    • Confirmed → reserves immediately.
  8. Choose payment status: unpaid, partial, paid.
  9. Save. A confirmation email is sent to the guest.

Check in a guest

  1. Open the booking.
  2. Tap Check in.
  3. The room availability decrements. Status moves to checked-in.
  4. Any extras (room service, restaurant) added later post to the same folio.

Add a payment

  1. Open the booking → Add Payment.
  2. Choose method: cash, mobile money, card, bank transfer, cheque.
  3. Enter amount + reference (where applicable).
  4. The system generates a sequential invoice number (INV-YYYY-NNNNNN), creates a PDF, attaches it to the email and sends it to the guest with the configured CC list.

Check out

  1. Open the booking → Check out.
  2. The system calculates: room nights + folio charges (room service, restaurant, laundry) + VAT + tourism levy.
  3. If the balance is positive, prompt to take payment now.
  4. If zero, confirm.
  5. A final consolidated invoice is generated and emailed automatically.

Cancel a booking

  1. Open the booking → Cancel.
  2. Choose a reason from the list (or type custom).
  3. Confirm. The room availability is restored, status moves to cancelled, and the guest receives a cancellation email.

Tentative bookings

  • A separate page /admin/tentative-bookings.php lists all tentatives.
  • Each one has an expiry timer (default 48h). At expiry the booking is auto-cancelled.
  • To convert a tentative to confirmed: open it → Confirm (or take a deposit, which auto-confirms).

Common mistakes to avoid

Don't manually delete a booking — always Cancel. Deletes break the audit trail.
  • Don't change check-in dates while a guest is in-house — cancel and rebook instead.
  • Don't issue a refund through the payments table — use the Refund button on the original payment so it links back.

Tips

  • Use Filter → checkin_today at the start of every shift to see who's arriving.
  • Use Filter → checkout_today before lunch to prepare folios.
  • The Calendar view (/admin/calendar.php) gives a colour-coded month view of every individual room.
