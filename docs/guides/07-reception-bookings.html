Reception and Bookings

This guide covers everything reception does with room bookings at Rosalyns Beach Hotel: finding a booking, creating one, checking a guest in and out, changing a booking, charging extras, cancelling, blocking dates and assigning physical rooms. It also explains the guest's side (the public booking form and the booking look-up page) so you can help a guest over the phone.

What is on the screen (the Bookings list)

The Bookings list has these parts:

  • Search box. Placeholder: "Search ref, guest, phone, room no, payment ref...". Results update as you type.
  • All Statuses dropdown: Pending, Tentative, Confirmed, Checked In, Checked Out, Cancelled, No-Show, Deleted.
  • Two date boxes. The first is Check-in from, the second is Check-out to.
  • Filter button, and a Clear button that appears when a filter is on.
  • Export CSV (downloads the list with your filters applied), Tentative (opens the tentative holds page, with a number badge when there are any) and New Booking.
  • Tabs: All, Today's Check-ins, Today's Check-outs, In-House, Pending, Tentative, Unpaid. A tab called Expiring Soon appears only while a tentative hold runs out within 24 hours, and a Deleted tab appears once any booking has been deleted. Each tab shows a count. Deleted bookings never appear in the other tabs or counts.
  • Red banner Missed Check-ins!: confirmed bookings whose check-in date has passed without a check-in. Amber banner Overdue Checkouts!: checked-in guests past their check-out date, or due today and still in after the check-out time (marked Late checkout on the row). Each has a View Details button.
  • The table: Ref, Guest Name, Room, Check In, Check Out, Nights, Guests, Total, Status, Payment, Created, Actions. The list shows 10 bookings per page, with Prev and Next buttons.

Round buttons in the Actions column. Only the actions that make sense for that booking's status, dates and money are shown (and only those your permissions allow), so a checked-out booking never offers Check-in and a pending booking never offers Checkout:

How to find a booking

  1. Go to Front Desk → Bookings.
  2. Type part of the reference, guest name, phone number, room number or payment reference in the search box. Or use the top-bar Search button (Ctrl+K), which also finds bookings by email.
  3. To narrow the list, pick a status, set the date boxes and click Filter. Click Clear to start again.
  4. Click a tab to see only today's arrivals, today's departures, in-house guests, pending, tentative or unpaid bookings.
  5. Click More → Full details to open the booking page.

If nothing matches you will see "No room bookings match your current search filters." The Status column values are the same words as the status filter. Payment values include Paid, Partial and Unpaid.

The Booking Details page (one booking in full)

Click More → Full details on the Bookings list, or click a booking on the Calendar. The page is called Booking followed by the reference. Use it to see a guest's bill, what they have paid and everything that was done to the booking. Needs View Bookings. Each button on it needs the same permission as on the list (see the table at the top).

  • Across the top: the reference, the guest, the dates and nights, the status, and Outstanding balance when money is owed. The buttons shown depend on the status: Confirm Booking or Convert to Confirmed, Mark No-Show, Upgrade / Change Type, Cancel Booking (for rooms booked together as a group: Cancel Whole Group (N rooms) and Cancel This Room Only) and Restore Booking (deleted bookings).
  • Stay details: guest, room, dates, guests, special requests. Adjust Stay Dates changes the dates (see How to change the stay dates).
  • Folio / Charges: the guest's bill. Add Custom Charge, Add Menu Item to Folio and Void Charge are explained in How to add or void folio charges. Food and drinks charged to the room from the till or room service also appear here.
  • Payment breakdown: every payment, refund and credit, and what is still owed. See How to take a payment.
  • Invoices: documents sent to the guest. Guest history: the guest's other stays. Internal notes: notes only staff see. Activity timeline: who did what and when (see How to add a note and read the timeline).

Times on this page (Created, Updated, the timeline) are shown in your own device's time zone. If they look wrong, check the clock and time zone on your phone or computer.

How to use the Calendar

Go to Front Desk → Calendar. It shows one month at a time, with one block per room and the bookings and blocks on each day. Use it to see which rooms are free (available or vacant) on any day: a room with no booking bar and no colour is free to sell.

  1. Click Previous or Next to change month.
  2. Use the filters: Room number or name… search, All types, All rooms, All statuses (Available, Occupied, Reserved, Cleaning, Maintenance, Out of order, Inspection) and All floors. A clear button appears once a filter is set.
  3. Click a booking bar to open that booking. Click a blocked day to open Blocked Dates.

The legend shows the room colours: Available (no booking), Reserved (future stay), Occupied (checked-in / active stay), Cleaning, Maintenance, Out of order, Blocked date (room-type or individual).

  • Occupied means a guest is checked in. A confirmed guest who has not arrived yet (including a missed arrival) shows as Reserved, and their name is underlined in red when the arrival date has passed.
  • A guest still in after their departure date keeps the room until checked out; those nights show the name in italics (overdue departure).
  • Cleaning and inspection only describe today. Maintenance and Out of order show from today until the room is fixed. Free nights in the past and future show as Available.
  • A booking on a blocked night is shown in red (CONFLICT): move the booking or remove the block.
  • A booking that holds two joined rooms shows on both rooms.
  • Bookings with no room assigned yet are listed in the yellow Bookings without a room this month box at the top, with an Assign room link.
  • Today (between Previous and Next) jumps back to the current month.

How to create a booking

Use this for walk-ins, phone bookings and agent bookings. Click New Booking on the Bookings page (/admin/create-booking.php, needs Create Bookings). The form has six steps along the top: Stay Details, Rooms, Guest Info, Status, Payment, Review. You can click a step to jump to it, or use the next and back buttons.

  1. Stay Details. Fill in Check-in Date (not before today), Check-out Date (must be after check-in), Total Guests (1 to 20) and Children (under 12). At least one guest must be an adult. Add Special Requests if the guest has any. Click Rooms & Availability.
  2. Rooms. Availability cards appear once both dates are set. Use Rooms needed (the minus and plus buttons) to add one line per room. On each line pick the Room Type and the Occupancy: Single, Double (the default) or Triple. The price is worked out from the occupancy you pick. Price override is optional and only administrators and managers may use it. Use Remove to delete a line.
  3. For a one-room booking you also see Room Assignment: Auto-assign (recommended) or Pick a specific room. With several rooms the system always auto-assigns.
  4. Guest Info. For a returning guest type a name, email or phone in Returning Guest Lookup ("Search by name, email or phone…") and click the guest to fill the form. Otherwise fill in Full Name, Email Address and Phone Number (all required), and Country and Address (optional).
  5. Status. Pick Pending, Confirmed (the default), Tentative (hold) or Checked In (walk-in). Tentative only appears when tentative bookings are switched on. See the rules section for what each one means.
  6. Payment. The Payment to Collect table shows the room rate, nights, child supplement, tourism levy and VAT (only the ones that are switched on), and Total to collect. If the guest pays now, tick Payment Received Now, choose a Payment Method (Cash, Bank Transfer, Mobile Money, Card, Cheque, Other) and type the Amount Collected. Type the full total for full payment or a smaller amount for a deposit. If the guest holds a credit note, click Check available credit to apply it.
  7. Review. Optionally fill in Group / Company / Agent Reference and Internal Notes. Send confirmation email to guest is ticked by default. What the guest is sent: if they paid now, the confirmation email and a receipt for each payment (no invoice, because they have paid); if nothing was paid, the confirmation email and a quotation with the agreed price and the balance due. If the status is Tentative, a Quotation box appears: tick Send quotation PDF to guest, choose how long it is valid for (1, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days) and add a note if you like.
  8. Click the create button at the bottom. You will see "Booking [reference] created successfully!" followed by the email result, and you land on the booking page.

If you click the create button twice by mistake you will see "Booking already created (reference ...). Duplicate submission ignored." and go to the first booking.

How to confirm a pending booking

  1. Open the booking (or find it on the list).
  2. Click Confirm Booking on the booking page, or the confirm button on the list. On the booking page you are asked "Confirm this booking and send the guest confirmation email?"
  3. You will see "Booking confirmed." plus "Confirmation email sent." when the email went out. If the booking is paid and has no room yet, the system picks a room for you and tells you which.

A tentative booking has a Convert to Confirmed button instead. It asks "Convert this tentative booking to confirmed and send the conversion email?" and then shows "Tentative booking converted to confirmed!"

How to assign or change a room

Assigning a physical room works only for Confirmed bookings. A late arrival (confirmed, but the arrival date has passed) can be given a room too, because it needs one before it can be checked in.

  1. Open the booking page and click Assign Room (or Change Room if one is already assigned). On the list use More → Assign room or Change room.
  2. The Assign Room window shows the booking reference and dates, and lists every room of the booked room type under Select Individual Room, not only the free ones. A line at the top says how many can take the stay (for example "3 of 8 rooms can take this stay."). Rooms you can use come first.
  3. Each room has a badge. Available: free and ready. Turnover: free for the stay, but housekeeping is not finished with it. The note says "Being cleaned" or "Previous guest still in the room" and "it must be marked clean before check-in". Needs note: the booking has children and the room does not accept them (see the next step). Current: the room the booking already holds. Unavailable: greyed out, with the reason underneath, for example the booking that holds it ("Booked: [booking reference] (12 Oct - 14 Oct)"), a blocked date, or maintenance. A room that a joined-room booking still needs (that booking has no rooms yet and no other free pair is left) is greyed out as "Needed for joined-room booking [reference] (assign that booking its pair first)"; it is not offered or auto-assigned to anyone else until that booking has its pair.
  4. Click a room that is not greyed out. If the booking has children and the room does not accept children, tick the override box and type a note explaining why.
  5. Click Assign Room and confirm. You will see "Room assigned successfully".

A room that housekeeping is still turning over is never hidden: it stays on sale, and you can assign it. Only the check-in itself waits for a clean room (see How to check a guest in).

Joined-room bookings

When the booked room type is a joined room (two adjoining rooms sold together, for example a family suite), the window lists the pairs instead, for example "Rooms 101 + 102", and you assign both rooms in one go. The button reads Assign Rooms and the confirmation is titled Confirm joined-room assignment. You will see "Joined rooms 101 + 102 assigned". A pair is greyed out with a reason if either room is booked, blocked or under maintenance, or if the room type of either room is blocked ("The room type of one of these rooms is blocked on these dates"). A joined-room booking always holds a whole pair: it is never given a single room ("This is a joined-room booking ..."), and the full edit page never splits the pair: it shows Joined room with a link to Change rooms (or Assign rooms).

Pressing Assign on one room of a pair in Individual Rooms also assigns the whole pair to a joined-room booking. Checking in (from Bookings or the dashboard) marks both rooms occupied, room service and POS room charges can go to either room and charge the one booking, the calendar shows the booking on both rooms with the pair named, and occupancy reports count a booking of a joined-only pair as one room per night (the pair is one room everywhere: rooms list, calendar, blocked dates, availability, dashboard and End of Day). A booking of a pair that is also sold on its own counts as two rooms per night, because both rooms are taken. Blocking a joined room blocks both of its rooms, or neither.

Moving an in-house guest

To move a guest to a different room type (upgrade or downgrade) see How to upgrade or downgrade a room type. An in-house guest can be moved to another room of the same type from the full edit page; their old room goes to Cleaning. Because the guest moves in now, the new room must be clean. An unclean room is listed as Not clean yet, or you will see "Room 12 is not clean yet, so the guest cannot move in now. Open Housekeeping and mark the cleanup as completed, or pick a clean room."

Assign Room, Change Room, Upgrade / Change Type and Check Out & Settle open on the Bookings list. When you start them from a booking page, you return to that booking when the action is done or you close the window. You do not have to find the booking again.

How to check a guest in

  1. Open the booking. The status must be Confirmed.
  2. Make sure at least a partial payment is recorded and, on the booking page, that a room is assigned. If the check-in button is greyed out, read the red hint under the buttons. It gives the exact reason.
  3. Click Check In (on the booking page you are asked to confirm). On the list a window opens with Prerequisites and an Optional Note.
  4. You will see "Guest checked in successfully." on the booking page, with "Room marked as occupied." and, for a late arrival, "Note: late check-in (N day(s) overdue)."

Check-in is the one step that waits for housekeeping: a room being turned over can be assigned, but the guest cannot be checked in until it is clean. If housekeeping has not marked the room clean, check-in is blocked: "Check-in blocked: Room 101 (cleaning pending) is not marked clean yet. Ask housekeeping to finish the room, or ask someone with the "Check-in to Room Not Clean" permission." A person who has that permission sees "... Confirm to check the guest in anyway (recorded in the booking timeline)." and can go ahead. The check-in is then recorded in the timeline as "Checked in to a room not marked clean".

To undo a check-in, use More → Undo check-in on the list (needs Check-in Guests; not shown once the guest is overdue for check-out). The booking goes back to Confirmed. It is refused while the guest still has an open tab or room-service order: "Order ORD-... is still open - close it in Orders & Tabs first." Close or settle that order first, then undo the check-in.

A room that is still Occupied is freed and sent to Cleaning (the guest was in it), not straight to Available. A room that is in Maintenance or Out of Order is left as it is: "Check-in cancelled (reverted to confirmed)."

How to take a payment

  • At booking time: use Payment Received Now and Amount Collected on the Payment step.
  • Pay everything in one click (cash): on the booking page click Mark as Paid in the Payment breakdown box. You are asked "Mark this booking payment as paid and email the guest a receipt?" It records the outstanding balance as a completed cash payment and emails the guest a receipt with a PDF. The button is not shown once the booking is Paid.
  • Part payments or other methods: use Money → Payments → Record Payment (or Money → Add Payment), or More → Consolidation on the list (needs Add Payments). The one-click Record payment as paid button on the list is hidden once any payment exists, so use these for a booking that already has a deposit.
  • What the guest is sent: every payment saved as Paid or Completed (any of the ways above, including the extra-night payment at check-out) emails the guest a receipt with a PDF, once. A payment never sends an invoice. The green message tells you what happened, for example "Receipt emailed to the guest." or "Receipt not emailed: guest has no email address - use Email receipt on the payment." See What the guest is sent, and when.
  • If the guest pays more than the bill, the extra is recorded as an automatic refund line ("Overpayment") and the booking shows Paid with zero balance. The Payment breakdown also shows "Guest has overpaid by ..." with an Issue a credit note link when applicable.

The Payment breakdown shows Room base, any rate plan discount, packages, Extras, Subtotal, Tourism Levy, VAT, Total, Paid and Balance. When the guest has paid too much the last line reads Credit owed to guest.

How to add or void folio charges

The Folio / Charges box on the booking page holds extras such as minibar or laundry. It is locked on cancelled, no-show and checked-out bookings.

Add a charge

  1. Click Add Charge.
  2. Choose the Charge Type: Custom, Service, Minibar, Laundry, Room Service, Breakfast or Other.
  3. Type a Description (up to 255 characters), a Quantity (more than 0) and a Unit Price (not negative).
  4. Click Add Charge. You will see "Charge added successfully. Line total: ..."

Add a menu item

  1. Click Add Menu Item.
  2. Choose the Food Menu or Drinks tab and click an item.
  3. Set the Quantity and click Add to Folio. You will see "Menu item added to folio. Line total: ..." If you do not pick an item you will see "Please select a menu item."

Void a charge

  1. Click the void button on the charge's row.
  2. Type the Reason for Voiding (required, up to 255 characters).
  3. Click Void Charge. You will see "Charge voided successfully."

After check-out the folio is locked: "Folio charges cannot be modified after check-out. Raise a credit note if an adjustment is needed." On cancelled or no-show bookings: "Folio charges cannot be modified on a cancelled booking."

How to change the stay dates (extend a stay by a night)

  1. On the booking page click Adjust Stay Dates. The button is not shown for cancelled, checked-out or no-show bookings.
  2. Set New Check-in Date and New Check-out Date. The Adjustment Preview shows the new nights, new total and the amount difference.
  3. Type the Reason for Adjustment (required).
  4. Click Confirm Adjustment. You will see "Stay dates adjusted successfully." with the extra amount due or the refund or credit. The guest is emailed about the change when an email address is on file.

For an in-house guest who is staying longer, the list has Extend stay for every checked-in guest. Extra nights are charged at the guest's booked rate. Administrators can use More → Change checkout date for an overdue guest and must type a reason.

How to edit a booking

What you can change depends on where the booking is in its life, exactly like at a real front desk:

Booking isYou can changeUse instead
Pending, tentative, confirmedGuest details, check-in and check-out dates, occupancy, number of guests, children, the specific room, special requests, admin notesA different room type: Upgrade / change room type
Checked in (in-house)Guest details, special requests, admin notes, and a move to another room of the same typeLonger or shorter stay: Extend stay / Adjust Stay Dates. Different type: Upgrade / change room type
Checked out, cancelled, no-show, expiredGuest contact details only (the page is called Correct Guest Details)Money corrections: a credit note
  1. On the booking page click Edit Booking (or More → Full edit page). Needs Edit Bookings.
  2. Make the changes. The Price box shows the current bill, the bill after your change and the difference. You cannot type a price: date changes are charged at the guest's booked nightly rate, and occupancy or children changes add or remove the standard-rate difference on top of that rate, so a guest's discount is never lost by an edit.
  3. Leave Email the guest ticked to tell them about date, guest, room or price changes.
  4. Save. You go back to the booking page with "Booking updated." Every changed field is written to the Audit log.

To give a discount or correct a price, issue a credit note from the booking's Payment breakdown. It keeps the invoice, the books and the audit trail consistent, which typing over the price would not.

Quick modify on the list is a smaller window for guest details, dates (before arrival) and the number of guests. It needs both Edit Bookings and Quick Modify Bookings. It does not change status, payment status or price: use the booking's actions (confirm, check-in, checkout, cancel), record payments, and use the full edit page when the number of children changes (it changes the price).

How to upgrade or downgrade a room type

Use this when a guest moves to a different room type: they buy an upgrade, you upgrade them as a courtesy, or you have to move them down because their room type is unavailable (overbooking, maintenance). Works for pending, tentative and confirmed bookings before arrival, and for in-house guests (the move then applies from tonight).

  1. On the list click More → Upgrade / change room type, or on the booking page click Upgrade / Change Type. Needs Edit Bookings.
  2. Pick the new room type. Each one is labelled Upgrade, Downgrade or Same tier. Types that are full for the stay, or too small for the party, cannot be chosen and say why.
  3. Choose the billing:
    • Upgrade → Paid upgrade: the guest pays the difference between the two room types' standard rates for each night, on top of the rate they booked (their discount or package is kept).
    • Upgrade → Complimentary upgrade: the bill does not change. The value given away is recorded in the audit log and timeline. Needs a Manager or Administrator, or the Edit Booking Financials permission.
    • Downgrade → Reduce the bill (the default): the guest pays for the lower room. If they have already paid more than the new bill, the difference shows as a credit to refund or carry forward.
    • Downgrade → Keep the original rate: for example a non-refundable rate where the guest asked to move. Needs a Manager or Administrator, or Edit Booking Financials.
  4. Pick the Reason (guest request, hotel availability, loyalty / VIP, service recovery, other). A short Note (who authorised it and why) is required for complimentary upgrades, for every downgrade, and whenever the reason is Other.
  5. Check the Current bill, New bill and Change figures, leave Email the guest ticked if they should be told, and click Apply room change.

The system then picks a free room of the new type. For an in-house guest the old room goes to Cleaning and the new one is marked occupied. Nights the guest has already slept stay at the booked rate. The change, the money effect and the reason are written to the audit log and the booking timeline.

How to send an invoice or quotation

  1. On the booking page go to the Invoices box.
  2. An invoice is for asking for money, so send one when a balance is due. It can be sent before any payment has been made. When the guest pays, they are sent a receipt by themselves; you do not need to send an invoice for that.
  3. Click Generate Invoice to make the PDF without sending it ("Invoice generated successfully: [number]"), or Send Invoice to email it ("Invoice sent successfully to guest."). Send via WhatsApp appears only when WhatsApp is switched on, and needs a guest phone number.
  4. For a quotation click Send Quotation at the top of the page. Choose Quotation valid for (1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 14, 21 or 30 days), add an optional Note to guest and send. The page then shows Quotation last sent with the date.

From the list use More → Send invoice, Send quotation or Resend email. In the Resend email window choose the Email Type (Booking Received, Booking Confirmed, Tentative Booking Confirmed, Tentative Converted to Confirmed, Booking Cancelled, Invoice, and a late Check-in Reminder when it applies) and optional CC Emails.

All quotations are listed under Money → Quotations (permission Create Bookings). Filter by type (Room, Conference, Event, Gym) and status (Sent, Accepted, Expired, Declined). A row can be re-sent, marked accepted or marked declined. An accepted room quotation confirms the booking: "Quotation marked as accepted and booking confirmed." An expired quotation cannot be accepted.

Invoices and quotations cannot be sent for a tentative booking: "Invoices cannot be generated or sent for tentative (unconfirmed) bookings. Convert the booking to confirmed first." Quotations are the tool for tentative holds.

How to add a note and read the timeline

  1. On the booking page find Internal notes.
  2. Type in "Add a note about this booking..." and click Add Note. You will see "Note added successfully." Guests never see internal notes.
  3. The Activity timeline lists everything that happened: confirmations, payments, check-in and check-out, room changes, overrides and cancellations. Guest history shows the guest's other stays.

How to check a guest out

  1. Open the booking (status Checked In). Check the Balance due. Take payment first if money is owed (see above).
  2. On the booked departure day, click Check Out on the booking page (you are asked "Check out this guest and generate the final invoice where applicable?") or the checkout button on the list ("Check out booking [reference]?").
  3. If the guest leaves on any other day, the booking page shows Check Out & Settle instead, which opens the list's settlement window. A plain checkout is refused on another day so the extra or unused nights are always dealt with.
  4. If the guest leaves late or early, the list opens the Checkout Settlement window. A late guest sees Overdue Checkout — Extra Charges with extra nights, rate and Amount to charge. A late checkout works like Extend stay: the stay is extended to today at the rate the guest booked, the bill and the check-out date change together, and no separate late-checkout charge is added. The amount shown is exactly the increase in the bill. An early guest sees Early Checkout — Refund with unused nights. Choose the payment or refund method (Cash, Bank Transfer, Credit Card, Debit Card, Mobile Money, Other), add optional Notes and click Charge & Checkout, Refund & Checkout or Checkout (no refund). Tick Skip settlement — checkout without recording a charge or refund to check the guest out without a charge or refund. Use it only if you dealt with the money outside the system.
  5. On success the booking becomes Checked Out, the room goes to Cleaning for housekeeping, and the guest is emailed one document (from the list as well as from the booking page). If they still owe money it is a Final Invoice. If nothing is owed it is a Stay Statement - paid in full, which lists the stay and every payment but does not ask for money. You will see Final invoice generated. or Stay statement (paid in full) generated. If the email could not go you will see (Final invoice email not sent.) or (Stay statement (paid in full) email not sent.)

Late checkout

When a guest stays past the booked departure day, Check Out & Settle treats the extra time as a late checkout. It extends the stay at the guest's booked nightly rate, exactly like Extend stay. The bill and the check-out date change together. There is no separate late-checkout charge on the folio, and Amount to charge is simply how much the bill goes up.

If the guest still owes money and you do not have Check-out With Balance you will see "Checkout blocked: the guest still owes MWK 50,000.00. Record the payment first, or ask someone with the "Check out with balance" permission." If you do have it, you are asked to confirm (on the list "Check out with balance?" with a Check Out with Balance button; on the booking page "This guest still owes ... Check out anyway with the outstanding balance?"). It is recorded in the timeline as "Checked out with outstanding balance".

How to cancel a booking or mark a no-show

Cancel

  1. Click Cancel Booking on the booking page, or the cancel button on the list (needs Cancel Bookings). Checked-in, checked-out, cancelled and no-show bookings cannot be cancelled.
  2. The confirmation says what will happen to the money: "Cancel this booking, release the room, and send the guest cancellation email? Cancellation handling: ...". The handling follows the setting in Hotel Settings: void the bill and refund everything paid, void the bill and keep payments as guest credit, or charge the first night and refund the rest.
  3. On the list you may type a Cancellation Reason (Optional). Confirm. You will see "Booking cancelled." followed by a summary of any refund or credit.

Group bookings. If the booking was made together with other rooms, the window first asks "This room was booked together with other rooms. Cancel:". Choose Whole group (N rooms) (the default: every room booked together is cancelled) or Only this room. The same choice is asked when you delete. A group action is all or nothing: if one room cannot be cancelled or deleted, none of the rooms change, and the message names the room that blocked it, for example "Group of 3 bookings (BR-...): nothing was cancelled. BR-... could not be cancelled: ..."

Cancelling also closes everything linked to the booking, all in one go. Nothing is left running:

  • Room tab and room-service orders still open on the booking are closed. Food nobody started is cancelled: its stock goes back and its room charge comes off the bill. Food already started stays charged (see the note below the warning).
  • The room is released straight away.
  • A sent quotation for the booking is marked expired.
  • Payments made by credit note go back to that same credit note, never out as cash. The rest is refunded or kept as the Hotel Settings cancellation rule says. See the Finance guide.

If a paid booking was cancelled and a refund is waiting, use the refund button on the list.

The cancel is refused, and nothing changes, only if an order on the booking is part-paid, or a delivered room-service order has been paid. You will see one of these messages. Settle or refund that order, then cancel again.

  • "Order ORD-... is part-paid. Settle or refund it in Orders & Tabs first, then try again." Someone has paid part of the order. Settle or refund it in Orders & Tabs first.
  • "Order ORD-... has a payment on it. Settle or refund it in Orders & Tabs first, then try again." A paid room-service order must be settled or refunded first.

Food already started: the cancel goes ahead. Food the kitchen or bar has already started or served stays charged to the guest's bill, and the refund is reduced by that amount. Food nobody has started is cancelled and its stock goes back. A station that had started part of the order gets a note "STOP - order ORD-...: do NOT make ... Booking closed; items already started still go out." The items already started still go out.

No-show

  1. The no-show button appears only once the check-in date has passed. On the booking page click Mark No-Show; on the list click the no-show button and add an optional note.
  2. Confirm "Mark this booking as no-show and release the assigned room?"

A no-show does the same clean-up as a cancel: open room tab and room-service orders are closed, the room is released, and a sent quotation is marked expired. It is one step, so either all of it happens or none of it does. If an order is part-paid you see the same plain message as for a cancel, and the booking stays as it was. Food already started stays on the guest's bill, as for a cancel.

If the check-in date has not passed you will see "No-show can only be marked after the check-in date has passed." Cancelled, no-show and checked-out bookings cannot be changed back.

How to delete and restore a booking

Delete is for bookings that should not be there at all: duplicates, test bookings, bookings made in error. A cancellation is different: it is a real booking the guest called off, and it stays in the cancellation figures. Deleting needs the Delete & Restore Bookings permission. Administrators always have it and can give it to others in Staff Access.

  1. On the list click More → Delete booking, or Delete Booking on the booking page.
  2. If the booking was made together with other rooms, choose Whole group (N rooms) (the default) or Only this room.
  3. Type why it is being deleted (required). Confirm.
  4. The booking moves to the Deleted tab (also the Deleted status filter). Any room it held is released straight away. Open room tab and room-service orders are closed in the same step (the same way as a cancel), and a sent quotation is marked expired. Nothing is sent to the guest.

The Deleted tab shows who deleted each booking, the exact date and time, and the reason. The booking page shows the same in a banner and is read-only. The deletion is also in the audit log and timeline.

To bring it back, click Restore booking on the Deleted tab or the booking page. It returns with the status it had before. If it held a room, the room type must still be free for its dates. If not, you are told and the booking stays deleted.

A booking cannot be deleted while the guest is checked in, or while money is on it (payments or folio charges). Refund or void the payments and void the charges first. This keeps the accounts complete: money is never left attached to a booking nobody can see. The same goes for a room order that is part-paid: you see the same plain message as for a cancel, and nothing is deleted. Settle or refund that order in Orders & Tabs first. Food the kitchen has already started does not block a delete: it stays on the guest's bill, and food not yet started is cancelled.

Tentative bookings

A tentative booking holds a room without payment for a set time. The length is a hotel setting (48 hours unless changed). Open Tentative from the Bookings page toolbar (/admin/tentative-bookings.php).

  • Tabs: All Active, Expiring Soon, Reminder Overdue. Below is Active Tentative Holds (Reference, Guest, Room, Dates, Expires, Reminder, Actions) and Recently Expired Holds.

Buttons on each hold:

  • Confirm Booking: asks "Convert this tentative booking to confirmed and send a confirmation email?" You will see "Booking converted to confirmed!"
  • Send expiry reminder email (or Resend reminder email to guest once sent).
  • Extend Tentative Hold: choose +12, +24, +48 or +72 hours and click Extend Hold.
  • Send Quotation: choose how long it is valid and add an optional note.
  • Hold Activity: the history of the hold.
  • Cancel Tentative Hold: asks "Cancel this tentative hold and release the room?" You will see "Tentative booking cancelled and room hold released." and the guest is notified.

A tentative hold expires automatically when its time runs out. Nobody has to do anything: the room is released, any sent quotation is marked expired, and the guest gets the "Tentative Booking Expired" email (if that email is switched on in Booking Settings). If a hold has money on it, cancel the booking instead so the money is refunded or kept properly.

To turn a pending booking into a hold, use More → Make tentative on the list. This is only possible while the booking is Pending with no payment: "Cannot make tentative: Bookings with payments cannot be made tentative." A confirmed booking cannot go back to tentative.

On this page only users with the Administrator or Manager role can confirm or cancel a hold. Others see "Your role does not have permission to manage tentative bookings."

How to block dates

Block dates (close dates, stop selling) when a room type or one physical room must not be sold, for example for repairs or an event. To block one room, choose Individual Room Level (Specific room) below. To take a room out of sale until it is fixed, with no end date, set its status to Maintenance or Out of Order on Individual Rooms (see Individual rooms). Go to Front Desk → Blocked Dates (/admin/blocked-dates.php, needs Blocked Dates to open the page and Block Rooms to block or unblock). Managers, Receptionists and Housekeeping have Block Rooms by default.

Without Block Rooms you can look at the list, but Block Single Date, Block Date Range and Unblock are greyed out. Hover over one to read "Needs the Block Rooms permission". If the request still reaches the server it is refused with "You need the Block Rooms permission to do this. Ask a manager to do it or to give you the permission." Ask a manager to do it, or to give you the permission in Staff & Access.

  1. Click Block Single Date or Block Date Range.
  2. Choose the Block Level: Room Type Level (All rooms of type) or Individual Room Level (Specific room), then pick the Room Type or Individual Room.
  3. Pick the Date to Block, or for a range the Date Range (the Selected Dates are listed).
  4. Choose the Block Type: Manual Block, Maintenance, Event or Fully Booked, and type a Reason (Optional).
  5. Save. You will see "Room type date blocked successfully.", "Individual room date blocked successfully." or "Successfully blocked N date(s)."

The list shows Date, Scope, Room, Block Type, Reason, Created By, Created At. Filter by Room Type, Individual Room, Start Date and End Date. Click Unblock on a row to free the date: "Date unblocked successfully".

In the room lists, individual rooms are grouped under their room type. Room types that are sold as joined pairs are labelled "- joined pairs", and a room that belongs to a pair is labelled "Room 101 - part of joined room Family Suite (with 102)". What a block does to joined rooms:

  • Blocking a room type also stops the joined pairs that use rooms of that type.
  • Blocking a joined-pairs room type stops only the pairs. The two rooms can still be sold on their own.
  • Blocking a single room that is part of a pair also takes that pair off sale on those nights. Its row in the list says "Also blocks joined room ...".

A block does not cancel or move existing bookings. If bookings already sleep on the blocked nights you will see "Note: N existing booking(s) already sleep on these nights (...). Blocks do not cancel or move bookings; reassign or contact those guests." Only today or later dates can be blocked.

Individual rooms

Room types (for example "Executive") are what guests book. Individual rooms are the physical rooms with numbers. Go to Front Desk → Individual Rooms (/admin/individual-rooms.php, needs Individual Rooms).

  • Filter by room type, status (Available, Occupied, Cleaning, Maintenance, Out of Order) and floor.
  • Each row has Details, Edit, Assign and Status. Tick several rooms and use Change status to... to change them together.
  • How to block a room: to take one room out of sale for set nights, use Blocked Dates with Individual Room Level. To take it out of sale until it is fixed, click Status and choose Maintenance or Out of Order: the room is left out of availability and no new booking can be given that room. Set it back to Available when it is ready. Existing bookings are not moved. Choosing Maintenance or Out of Order (in Status, in Change status to..., or when adding or editing a room) needs the Block Rooms permission. Without it those two choices are greyed out and say "(needs Block Rooms permission)". A maintenance request in Room Maintenance still puts its room into Maintenance by itself, whoever you are.
  • Add Individual Room: Room Type and Room Number are required (room numbers must be unique: "Room number already exists. Please use a unique room number."). Optional: Room Name, Floor, Guest Capacity Override, Status, Display Order, Child Supplement Override (%), occupancy overrides, Children Allowed Override, Notes.
  • Duplicate room numbers are flagged while you type: "This room number is already used by another room." If the save is still refused, the form stays open with what you typed and the reason shown.
  • A room with a live or tentative booking cannot be deleted. You see "Room 101 has 2 live booking(s) (BR-..., BR-...). Move or cancel them first, then delete the room."
  • A room that has booking or housekeeping history is not deleted. It is kept and made inactive: "Room 101 has booking or housekeeping history, so it was kept and made inactive instead of deleted. It is no longer sold." Its photos, amenities and blocked dates go with a room that is really deleted, but not with one that is kept.
  • A room that belongs to a joined pair shows a blue badge under its room type: "Also joined: Family Suite with 102" (the room is still sold on its own) or "Joined only: ..." (sold only as the pair). A pairing that is switched off is greyed and ends with (inactive).

Joined room combinations

Two adjoining rooms can be sold together as one room type, for example a family suite. Click Room Combinations at the top of Individual Rooms (the badge shows how many pairings exist) to open Joined Room Combinations.

  1. Under Add / Edit Combination fill in Combination Name, Book As Room Type, First Room and Second Room (two different active rooms). Optional: Rate Override, Max Guests, Notes and the Active tick.
  2. Under How are these two rooms sold? choose On their own and as the joined room (each room keeps its own room type; booking the pair takes both rooms, and booking either room makes the pair unavailable) or Only as the joined room (both rooms move to the Book As room type, are no longer sold on their own and show as one room everywhere). Only as the joined room is ticked by default for a new combination. The second choice is refused while a booking of another room type holds either room: "These rooms cannot be sold only as the joined room yet: ..." Move those bookings first.
  3. Click Save Combination. You will see "Joined-room combination added successfully!" or "Joined-room combination updated successfully!". Clear empties the form.

Existing Combinations lists Name, Book As, Rooms, Sold (Alone and as the pair or Only as the pair), Capacity, Rate and Status (with the number of bookings that used it). The buttons on each row:

  • Edit: loads the pairing into the form.
  • Deactivate: stops selling the pair ("Stop selling ...?"). Bookings that already hold the rooms keep them. An inactive pairing shows Reactivate instead.
  • Reactivate: sells the pair again ("Joined-room combination reactivated.").
  • Delete: removes a pairing that no booking has ever used. Only the pairing goes; the two rooms stay as they are. A pairing that bookings used cannot be deleted (the button is greyed out, "Used by bookings - kept for their history. Deactivate it instead.").

A room can belong to only one active pair: "One of these rooms is already part of the active joined room ... Deactivate or delete that combination first." The same two rooms cannot be joined twice: "These two rooms are already joined as ...".

Room cleaning and repairs are covered in the Housekeeping guide. Prices are covered in the Packages and Rates guide.

The guest's side: booking form and look-up

Book Your Stay (booking.php)

Guests book on the website. They choose a Check-in Date and Check-out Date, Number of Guests (1 to 20) and Of whom children (under 12), pick a room (the page then moves to Guest Information and stays there), enter Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number (required), Country, Address and Special Requests (Optional), choose any add-on packages, and click Confirm Booking.

  • The guest chooses a Booking Type. Standard Booking creates a Pending booking: the hotel confirms it within 24 hours and gives payment details. Tentative Booking (shown only when switched on) holds the room for the hotel's hold time with no payment.
  • The guest lands on a confirmation page and gets an email with the booking reference.
  • Guest names with digits are rejected: "Name contains invalid characters". A past check-in date is rejected: "Check-in date cannot be in the past".
  • Other messages: "Security token invalid. Please refresh the page and try again.", "Too many booking attempts. Please wait a few minutes before trying again.", "A booking already exists for these dates and room. Please check your existing bookings.", "Children are not allowed for the selected room type."

New online bookings arrive as Pending (or Tentative). A receptionist must confirm them, see the section on confirming above.

Check Booking Status (booking-lookup.php)

The guest enters their Booking Reference and the Email Address they booked with and clicks Look Up Booking. They see the room, guest name, check-in, check-out, nights, guests, total amount, booking status, payment status, booked-on date and special requests.

  • Wrong details: "No booking found with that reference and email combination. Please check your details and try again."
  • Too many tries (10 in 10 minutes): "Too many lookup attempts. Please wait a few minutes before trying again."
  • Pending, confirmed and tentative bookings show Need to Cancel? with a reason box and Cancel My Booking. Success: "Your booking has been cancelled successfully. A confirmation email has been sent to your email address." If a refund is owed and refunds need approval: "Any refund due is pending approval by our team and will be processed shortly." Staff then handle the refund.
  • If the hotel has set a cancellation notice period the guest sees "Online cancellations are not available within N day(s) of check-in. Please contact us directly." Checked-in, checked-out, cancelled and no-show bookings cannot be cancelled online.

Rules the system enforces

  • Status flow: Pending or Tentative → Confirmed → Checked In → Checked Out. Confirmed can also become Cancelled or No-Show. Checked In can be undone back to Confirmed. Checked Out, Cancelled and No-Show are final.
  • Check-in needs status Confirmed, at least a partial payment, and the check-in date to have arrived ("Check-in date has not been reached yet"). An unpaid booking is refused: "Cannot check in: booking must have at least partial payment." (list) or "At least a partial payment must be recorded before check-in" (booking page).
  • Check-in is blocked while an assigned room is not marked clean, unless you hold Check-in to Room Not Clean.
  • Check-out is blocked while money is owed unless you hold Check-out With Balance. Small rounding differences are ignored.
  • A plain check-out is only for the booked departure day. Leaving late or early always goes through the Checkout Settlement window, so extra nights are charged (or waived on purpose) and unused nights refunded (or kept on purpose).
  • A guest is overdue once their departure date has passed, and a late checkout when due today and still in after the check-out time set in Hotel Settings. An overdue guest keeps the room (it is not sold to anyone else) until checked out.
  • Prices are never typed in on the edit pages: changes reprice at the guest's booked rate, room-type changes go through Upgrade / change room type, and price corrections through a credit note.
  • Deleted bookings are hidden everywhere except the Deleted tab, hold no room, and cannot be changed until restored.
  • Cancel, delete, no-show and expiry never leave anything running on the booking. Open room tabs and room-service orders are closed, the room is released and a sent quotation is expired, all in one step. If one order cannot be closed (only a part-paid order stops it), the whole action is refused and nothing changes. Food already started stays on the bill.
  • Undo check-in is refused while the guest has an open order.
  • After check-out the room goes to Cleaning and the folio is locked.
  • Housekeeping never takes a room off sale and never removes it from the Assign Room list: a room being turned over still shows, marked Turnover. Only check-in waits for a clean room (an in-house room move also needs a clean room).
  • A joined pair is sold only when both rooms are free and neither room's own room type is blocked for those dates. A room can be in only one active pair.
  • Status options on Create Booking: Pending and Confirmed save the booking with that status. Tentative (hold) saves a hold, disables payment collection and offers a quotation. Checked In (walk-in) saves the booking as already checked in.
  • Payment status on a new booking is worked out from the amount collected: the full total (or more) gives Paid, less gives Partial, nothing gives Unpaid.
  • When you adjust stay dates, the new stay must be at least one night ("Booking must be for at least one night.") and no more than 30 nights ("Booking cannot exceed 30 nights. Please contact management for extended stays.").
  • Adjusting dates cannot move a not-yet-started booking into the past.
  • A confirmed, paid booking with no room gets one assigned automatically where possible.
  • Occupancy (Single, Double, Triple) must be switched on for the room type, and children only go in rooms that allow them. Every room needs at least one adult.
  • The booking reference starts with the prefix set in Hotel Settings, followed by the year and six digits.
