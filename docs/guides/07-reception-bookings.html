Reception and Bookings

This guide covers everything reception does with room bookings at Rosalyns Beach Hotel: finding a booking, creating one, checking a guest in and out, changing a booking, charging extras, cancelling, blocking dates and assigning physical rooms. It also explains the guest's side (the public booking form and the booking look-up page) so you can help a guest over the phone.

What is on the screen

The Bookings list has these parts:

  • Search box. Placeholder: "Search ref, guest, phone, room no, payment ref...". Results update as you type.
  • All Statuses dropdown: Pending, Tentative, Confirmed, Checked In, Checked Out, Cancelled, No-Show.
  • Two date boxes. The first is Check-in from, the second is Check-out to.
  • Filter button, and a Clear button that appears when a filter is on.
  • Export CSV (downloads the list with your filters applied), Tentative (opens the tentative holds page, with a number badge when there are any) and New Booking.
  • Tabs: All, Today's Check-ins, Today's Check-outs, In-House, Pending, Tentative, Unpaid. A tab called Expiring Soon appears only while a tentative hold runs out within 24 hours. Each tab shows a count.
  • Red banner Missed Check-ins!: confirmed bookings whose check-in date has passed without a check-in. Amber banner Overdue Checkouts!: checked-in guests past their check-out date. Each has a View Details button.
  • The table: Ref, Guest Name, Room, Check In, Check Out, Nights, Guests, Total, Status, Payment, Created, Actions. The list shows 10 bookings per page, with Prev and Next buttons.

How to find a booking

  1. Go to Front Desk → Bookings.
  2. Type part of the reference, guest name, phone number, room number or payment reference in the search box. Or use the top-bar Search button (Ctrl+K), which also finds bookings by email.
  3. To narrow the list, pick a status, set the date boxes and click Filter. Click Clear to start again.
  4. Click a tab to see only today's arrivals, today's departures, in-house guests, pending, tentative or unpaid bookings.
  5. Click More → Full details to open the booking page.

If nothing matches you will see "No room bookings match your current search filters." The Status column values are the same words as the status filter. Payment values include Paid, Partial and Unpaid.

How to use the Calendar

Go to Front Desk → Calendar. It shows one month at a time, with one block per room and the bookings and blocks on each day.

  1. Click Previous or Next to change month.
  2. Use the filters: Room number or name… search, All types, All rooms, All statuses (Available, Occupied, Reserved, Cleaning, Maintenance, Out of order, Inspection) and All floors. A clear button appears once a filter is set.
  3. Click a booking bar to open that booking. Click a blocked day to open Blocked Dates.

The legend shows the room colours: Available (no booking), Reserved (future stay), Occupied (checked-in / active stay), Cleaning, Maintenance, Out of order, Blocked date (room-type or individual).

How to create a booking

Use this for walk-ins, phone bookings and agent bookings. Click New Booking on the Bookings page (/admin/create-booking.php, needs Create Bookings). The form has six steps along the top: Stay Details, Rooms, Guest Info, Status, Payment, Review. You can click a step to jump to it, or use the next and back buttons.

  1. Stay Details. Fill in Check-in Date (not before today), Check-out Date (must be after check-in), Total Guests (1 to 20) and Children (under 12). At least one guest must be an adult. Add Special Requests if the guest has any. Click Rooms & Availability.
  2. Rooms. Availability cards appear once both dates are set. Use Rooms needed (the minus and plus buttons) to add one line per room. On each line pick the Room Type and the Occupancy: Single, Double (the default) or Triple. The price is worked out from the occupancy you pick. Price override is optional and only administrators and managers may use it. Use Remove to delete a line.
  3. For a one-room booking you also see Room Assignment: Auto-assign (recommended) or Pick a specific room. With several rooms the system always auto-assigns.
  4. Guest Info. For a returning guest type a name, email or phone in Returning Guest Lookup ("Search by name, email or phone…") and click the guest to fill the form. Otherwise fill in Full Name, Email Address and Phone Number (all required), and Country and Address (optional).
  5. Status. Pick Pending, Confirmed (the default), Tentative (hold) or Checked In (walk-in). Tentative only appears when tentative bookings are switched on. See the rules section for what each one means.
  6. Payment. The Payment to Collect table shows the room rate, nights, child supplement, tourism levy and VAT (only the ones that are switched on), and Total to collect. If the guest pays now, tick Payment Received Now, choose a Payment Method (Cash, Bank Transfer, Mobile Money, Card, Cheque, Other) and type the Amount Collected. Type the full total for full payment or a smaller amount for a deposit. If the guest holds a credit note, click Check available credit to apply it.
  7. Review. Optionally fill in Group / Company / Agent Reference and Internal Notes. Send confirmation email to guest is ticked by default. If the status is Tentative, a Quotation box appears: tick Send quotation PDF to guest, choose how long it is valid for (1, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days) and add a note if you like.
  8. Click the create button at the bottom. You will see "Booking [reference] created successfully!" followed by the email result, and you land on the booking page.

If you click the create button twice by mistake you will see "Booking already created (reference ...). Duplicate submission ignored." and go to the first booking.

How to confirm a pending booking

  1. Open the booking (or find it on the list).
  2. Click Confirm Booking on the booking page, or the confirm button on the list. On the booking page you are asked "Confirm this booking and send the guest confirmation email?"
  3. You will see "Booking confirmed." plus "Confirmation email sent." when the email went out. If the booking is paid and has no room yet, the system picks a room for you and tells you which.

A tentative booking has a Convert to Confirmed button instead. It asks "Convert this tentative booking to confirmed and send the conversion email?" and then shows "Tentative booking converted to confirmed!"

How to assign or change a room

Assigning a physical room works only for Confirmed bookings.

  1. Open the booking page and click Assign Room (or Change Room if one is already assigned). On the list use More → Assign room or Change room.
  2. In the Assign Room window pick the room under Select Individual Room. Only rooms of the booked room type that are free for those dates are offered.
  3. If the booking has children and the room does not accept children, you must tick the override box and type a note explaining why.
  4. Save. You will see "Room assigned successfully".

To move a guest to a different room type, use More → Upgrade room on the list (confirmed bookings that are not overdue). Pick the new room type under Select New Room Type.

How to check a guest in

  1. Open the booking. The status must be Confirmed.
  2. Make sure at least a partial payment is recorded and, on the booking page, that a room is assigned. If the check-in button is greyed out, read the red hint under the buttons. It gives the exact reason.
  3. Click Check In (on the booking page you are asked to confirm). On the list a window opens with Prerequisites and an Optional Note.
  4. You will see "Guest checked in successfully." on the booking page, with "Room marked as occupied." and, for a late arrival, "Note: late check-in (N day(s) overdue)."

If housekeeping has not marked the room clean, check-in is blocked: "Check-in blocked: Room 101 (cleaning pending) is not marked clean yet. Ask housekeeping to finish the room, or ask someone with the "Check-in to Room Not Clean" permission." A person who has that permission sees "... Confirm to check the guest in anyway (recorded in the booking timeline)." and can go ahead. The check-in is then recorded in the timeline as "Checked in to a room not marked clean".

To undo a check-in, use More → Undo check-in on the list (needs Check-in Guests; not shown once the guest is overdue for check-out). The booking goes back to Confirmed and the room to Available: "Check-in cancelled (reverted to confirmed)."

How to take a payment

  • At booking time: use Payment Received Now and Amount Collected on the Payment step.
  • Pay everything in one click (cash): on the booking page click Mark as Paid in the Payment breakdown box. You are asked "Mark this booking payment as paid and send the payment invoice email?" It records the outstanding balance as a completed cash payment. The button is not shown once the booking is Paid.
  • Part payments or other methods: use Money → Payments → Record Payment (or Advanced → Add Payment), or More → Consolidation on the list (needs Add Payments). The one-click Record payment as paid button on the list is hidden once any payment exists, so use these for a booking that already has a deposit.
  • If the guest pays more than the bill, the extra is recorded as an automatic refund line ("Overpayment") and the booking shows Paid with zero balance. The Payment breakdown also shows "Guest has overpaid by ..." with an Issue a credit note link when applicable.

The Payment breakdown shows Room base, any rate plan discount, packages, Extras, Subtotal, Tourism Levy, VAT, Total, Paid and Balance. When the guest has paid too much the last line reads Credit owed to guest.

How to add or void folio charges

The Folio / Charges box on the booking page holds extras such as minibar or laundry. It is locked on cancelled, no-show and checked-out bookings.

Add a charge

  1. Click Add Charge.
  2. Choose the Charge Type: Custom, Service, Minibar, Laundry, Room Service, Breakfast or Other.
  3. Type a Description (up to 255 characters), a Quantity (more than 0) and a Unit Price (not negative).
  4. Click Add Charge. You will see "Charge added successfully. Line total: ..."

Add a menu item

  1. Click Add Menu Item.
  2. Choose the Food Menu or Drinks tab and click an item.
  3. Set the Quantity and click Add to Folio. You will see "Menu item added to folio. Line total: ..." If you do not pick an item you will see "Please select a menu item."

Void a charge

  1. Click the void button on the charge's row.
  2. Type the Reason for Voiding (required, up to 255 characters).
  3. Click Void Charge. You will see "Charge voided successfully."

After check-out the folio is locked: "Folio charges cannot be modified after check-out. Raise a credit note if an adjustment is needed." On cancelled or no-show bookings: "Folio charges cannot be modified on a cancelled booking."

How to change the stay dates

  1. On the booking page click Adjust Stay Dates. The button is not shown for cancelled, checked-out or no-show bookings.
  2. Set New Check-in Date and New Check-out Date. The Adjustment Preview shows the new nights, new total and the amount difference.
  3. Type the Reason for Adjustment (required).
  4. Click Confirm Adjustment. You will see "Stay dates adjusted successfully." with the extra amount due or the refund or credit. The guest is emailed about the change when an email address is on file.

For an in-house guest who is staying longer, the list also has Extend stay (next to Checkout now when overdue). Administrators can use More → Change checkout date for an overdue guest and must type a reason.

How to edit a booking

  1. On the booking page click Edit Booking (or More → Full edit page). Needs Edit Bookings.
  2. You can change guest Full Name, Email, Phone, Country, Room, Occupancy Type, Check-in Date, Check-out Date, Number of Guests, Children (under 12), the final price, Special Requests and Admin Notes. The price box needs the Edit Booking Financials permission.
  3. Save. You will see "Booking updated successfully."

Quick modify on the list is a smaller window for fast changes and needs both Edit Bookings and Quick Modify Bookings. Every change is written to the Audit log (More menu).

How to send an invoice or quotation

  1. On the booking page go to the Invoices box.
  2. Click Generate Invoice to make the PDF without sending it ("Invoice generated successfully: [number]"), or Send Invoice to email it ("Invoice sent successfully to guest."). Send via WhatsApp appears only when WhatsApp is switched on, and needs a guest phone number.
  3. For a quotation click Send Quotation at the top of the page. Choose Quotation valid for (1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 14, 21 or 30 days), add an optional Note to guest and send. The page then shows Quotation last sent with the date.

From the list use More → Send invoice, Send quotation or Resend email. In the Resend email window choose the Email Type (Booking Received, Booking Confirmed, Tentative Booking Confirmed, Tentative Converted to Confirmed, Booking Cancelled, Invoice, and a late Check-in Reminder when it applies) and optional CC Emails.

All quotations are listed under Advanced → Quotations (/admin/quotations.php, permission Create Bookings). Filter by type (Room, Conference, Event, Gym) and status (Sent, Accepted, Expired, Declined). A row can be re-sent, marked accepted or marked declined. An accepted room quotation confirms the booking: "Quotation marked as accepted and booking confirmed." An expired quotation cannot be accepted.

Invoices and quotations cannot be sent for a tentative booking: "Invoices cannot be generated or sent for tentative (unconfirmed) bookings. Convert the booking to confirmed first." Quotations are the tool for tentative holds.

How to add a note and read the timeline

  1. On the booking page find Internal notes.
  2. Type in "Add a note about this booking..." and click Add Note. You will see "Note added successfully." Guests never see internal notes.
  3. The Activity timeline lists everything that happened: confirmations, payments, check-in and check-out, room changes, overrides and cancellations. Guest history shows the guest's other stays.

How to check a guest out

  1. Open the booking (status Checked In). Check the Balance due. Take payment first if money is owed (see above).
  2. On the booking page click Check Out. You are asked "Check out this guest and generate the final invoice where applicable?" Confirm with Check out. (This page does no late or early settlement.)
  3. On the list click the checkout button instead. Checkout is available from one day before the scheduled check-out date. If the guest leaves on the scheduled day, a window asks "Check out booking [reference]?" Click Check Out.
  4. If the guest leaves late or early, the list opens the Checkout Settlement window. A late guest sees Overdue Checkout — Extra Charges with extra nights, rate and Amount to charge. An early guest sees Early Checkout — Refund with unused nights. Choose the payment or refund method (Cash, Bank Transfer, Credit Card, Debit Card, Mobile Money, Other), add optional Notes and click Charge & Checkout, Refund & Checkout or Checkout (no refund). Tick Skip settlement — checkout without recording a charge or refund to check the guest out without a charge or refund. Use it only if you dealt with the money outside the system.
  5. On success the booking becomes Checked Out and the room goes to Cleaning for housekeeping.

If the guest still owes money and you do not have Check-out With Balance you will see "Checkout blocked: the guest still owes MWK 50,000.00. Record the payment first, or ask someone with the "Check out with balance" permission." If you do have it, you are asked to confirm (on the list "Check out with balance?" with a Check Out with Balance button; on the booking page "This guest still owes ... Check out anyway with the outstanding balance?"). It is recorded in the timeline as "Checked out with outstanding balance".

How to cancel a booking or mark a no-show

Cancel

  1. Click Cancel Booking on the booking page, or the cancel button on the list (needs Cancel Bookings). Checked-in, checked-out, cancelled and no-show bookings cannot be cancelled.
  2. The confirmation says what will happen to the money: "Cancel this booking, release the room, and send the guest cancellation email? Cancellation handling: ...". The handling follows the setting in Hotel Settings: void the bill and refund everything paid, void the bill and keep payments as guest credit, or charge the first night and refund the rest.
  3. On the list you may type a Cancellation Reason (Optional). Confirm. You will see "Booking cancelled." followed by a summary of any refund or credit.

If a paid booking was cancelled and a refund is waiting, use the refund button on the list.

No-show

  1. The no-show button appears only once the check-in date has passed. On the booking page click Mark No-Show; on the list click the no-show button and add an optional note.
  2. Confirm "Mark this booking as no-show and release the assigned room?"

If the check-in date has not passed you will see "No-show can only be marked after the check-in date has passed." Cancelled, no-show and checked-out bookings cannot be changed back.

Tentative bookings

A tentative booking holds a room without payment for a set time. The length is a hotel setting (48 hours unless changed). Open Tentative from the Bookings page toolbar (/admin/tentative-bookings.php).

  • Tabs: All Active, Expiring Soon, Reminder Overdue. Below is Active Tentative Holds (Reference, Guest, Room, Dates, Expires, Reminder, Actions) and Recently Expired Holds.

Buttons on each hold:

  • Confirm Booking: asks "Convert this tentative booking to confirmed and send a confirmation email?" You will see "Booking converted to confirmed!"
  • Send expiry reminder email (or Resend reminder email to guest once sent).
  • Extend Tentative Hold: choose +12, +24, +48 or +72 hours and click Extend Hold.
  • Send Quotation: choose how long it is valid and add an optional note.
  • Hold Activity: the history of the hold.
  • Cancel Tentative Hold: asks "Cancel this tentative hold and release the room?" You will see "Tentative booking cancelled and room hold released." and the guest is notified.

To turn a pending booking into a hold, use More → Make tentative on the list. This is only possible while the booking is Pending with no payment: "Cannot make tentative: Bookings with payments cannot be made tentative." A confirmed booking cannot go back to tentative.

On this page only users with the Administrator or Manager role can confirm or cancel a hold. Others see "Your role does not have permission to manage tentative bookings."

How to block dates

Block dates when a room type or one physical room must not be sold, for example for repairs or an event. Go to Advanced → Blocked Dates (/admin/blocked-dates.php, needs Blocked Dates).

  1. Click Block Single Date or Block Date Range.
  2. Choose the Block Level: Room Type Level (All rooms of type) or Individual Room Level (Specific room), then pick the Room Type or Individual Room.
  3. Pick the Date to Block, or for a range the Date Range (the Selected Dates are listed).
  4. Choose the Block Type: Manual Block, Maintenance, Event or Fully Booked, and type a Reason (Optional).
  5. Save. You will see "Room type date blocked successfully.", "Individual room date blocked successfully." or "Successfully blocked N date(s)."

The list shows Date, Scope, Room, Block Type, Reason, Created By, Created At. Filter by Room Type, Individual Room, Start Date and End Date. Click Unblock on a row to free the date: "Date unblocked successfully".

A block does not cancel or move existing bookings. If bookings already sleep on the blocked nights you will see "Note: N existing booking(s) already sleep on these nights (...). Blocks do not cancel or move bookings; reassign or contact those guests." Only today or later dates can be blocked.

Individual rooms

Room types (for example "Executive") are what guests book. Individual rooms are the physical rooms with numbers. Go to Advanced → Individual Rooms (/admin/individual-rooms.php, needs Individual Rooms).

  • Filter by room type, status (Available, Occupied, Cleaning, Maintenance, Out of Order) and floor.
  • Each row has Details, Edit, Assign and Status. Tick several rooms and use Change status to... to change them together.
  • Add Individual Room: Room Type and Room Number are required (room numbers must be unique: "Room number already exists. Please use a unique room number."). Optional: Room Name, Floor, Guest Capacity Override, Status, Display Order, Child Supplement Override (%), occupancy overrides, Children Allowed Override, Notes.
  • A room with a current or future pending, confirmed or checked-in booking cannot be deleted: "Cannot delete room with active bookings."
  • Joined Room Combinations: two rooms that can be sold together as one room type, for example a family suite. Needs a name, a room type to book as, and two different active rooms.

Room cleaning and repairs are covered in the Housekeeping guide. Prices are covered in the Packages and Rates guide.

The guest's side: booking form and look-up

Book Your Stay (booking.php)

Guests book on the website. They choose a Check-in Date and Check-out Date, Number of Guests (1 to 20) and Of whom children (under 12), pick a room, enter Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number (required), Country, Address and Special Requests (Optional), choose any add-on packages, and click Confirm Booking.

  • The guest chooses a Booking Type. Standard Booking creates a Pending booking: the hotel confirms it within 24 hours and gives payment details. Tentative Booking (shown only when switched on) holds the room for the hotel's hold time with no payment.
  • The guest lands on a confirmation page and gets an email with the booking reference.
  • Guest names with digits are rejected: "Name contains invalid characters". A past check-in date is rejected: "Check-in date cannot be in the past".
  • Other messages: "Security token invalid. Please refresh the page and try again.", "Too many booking attempts. Please wait a few minutes before trying again.", "A booking already exists for these dates and room. Please check your existing bookings.", "Children are not allowed for the selected room type."

New online bookings arrive as Pending (or Tentative). A receptionist must confirm them, see the section on confirming above.

Check Booking Status (booking-lookup.php)

The guest enters their Booking Reference and the Email Address they booked with and clicks Look Up Booking. They see the room, guest name, check-in, check-out, nights, guests, total amount, booking status, payment status, booked-on date and special requests.

  • Wrong details: "No booking found with that reference and email combination. Please check your details and try again."
  • Too many tries (10 in 10 minutes): "Too many lookup attempts. Please wait a few minutes before trying again."
  • Pending, confirmed and tentative bookings show Need to Cancel? with a reason box and Cancel My Booking. Success: "Your booking has been cancelled successfully. A confirmation email has been sent to your email address." If a refund is owed and refunds need approval: "Any refund due is pending approval by our team and will be processed shortly." Staff then handle the refund.
  • If the hotel has set a cancellation notice period the guest sees "Online cancellations are not available within N day(s) of check-in. Please contact us directly." Checked-in, checked-out, cancelled and no-show bookings cannot be cancelled online.

Rules the system enforces

  • Status flow: Pending or Tentative → Confirmed → Checked In → Checked Out. Confirmed can also become Cancelled or No-Show. Checked In can be undone back to Confirmed. Checked Out, Cancelled and No-Show are final.
  • Check-in needs status Confirmed, at least a partial payment, and the check-in date to have arrived ("Check-in date has not been reached yet"). An unpaid booking is refused: "Cannot check in: booking must have at least partial payment." (list) or "At least a partial payment must be recorded before check-in" (booking page).
  • Check-in is blocked while an assigned room is not marked clean, unless you hold Check-in to Room Not Clean.
  • Check-out is blocked while money is owed unless you hold Check-out With Balance. Small rounding differences are ignored.
  • After check-out the room goes to Cleaning and the folio is locked.
  • Status options on Create Booking: Pending and Confirmed save the booking with that status. Tentative (hold) saves a hold, disables payment collection and offers a quotation. Checked In (walk-in) saves the booking as already checked in.
  • Payment status on a new booking is worked out from the amount collected: the full total (or more) gives Paid, less gives Partial, nothing gives Unpaid.
  • When you adjust stay dates, the new stay must be at least one night ("Booking must be for at least one night.") and no more than 30 nights ("Booking cannot exceed 30 nights. Please contact management for extended stays.").
  • Adjusting dates cannot move a not-yet-started booking into the past.
  • A confirmed, paid booking with no room gets one assigned automatically where possible.
  • Occupancy (Single, Double, Triple) must be switched on for the room type, and children only go in rooms that allow them. Every room needs at least one adult.
  • The booking reference starts with the prefix set in Hotel Settings, followed by the year and six digits.
