Finance and Payments

How to record money received at Rosalyns Beach Hotel, refund it, find it again, and produce the documents that go with it: receipts, invoices, credit notes and quotations.

Who uses it
Accounts staff, managers, and front desk staff who take payments
Where to find it
Admin menu → Money → Payments, Invoices, Accounting. Add Payment, Receipts, Quotations and Credit Notes are in the same group.
Permission needed
Payments list and details: View Payments. Add or edit a payment: Add Payments. Refunds: Issue & settle refunds. Invoices and Credit Notes: Invoices. Quotations: Create Bookings. Receipts: Receipts. Accounting dashboard: Accounting. VAT settings: Change VAT & refund settings.
Only shown when
The Finance & Accounting module is switched on in Settings → Modules. Invoices and Quotations also need Bookings, Conference or Gym switched on. Credit Notes need Bookings or Conference.

What is on the screen

Every payment is one line in the Payments Ledger. A refund is its own line, marked as a refund, linked to the payment it refunds.

StatusMeaning
Pending, PartialRecorded but not received yet. It does not reduce what the guest owes.
Paid, CompletedMoney received. It reduces the balance and gets a receipt number.
Refunded, Partially refundedSet only by the refund process. Money was received and some or all has gone back.
Failed, CancelledDoes not count as money received.

A refund line counts against the guest only once it is Processing or Completed. A Pending or Failed refund does not change the balance yet.

The Payments page shows these buttons at the top: Record Payment, Export CSV, Dashboard, and Invoices (Invoices only appears when invoicing is switched on for your hotel). Below them are four figures: Collected (filtered), Pending / Partial, Today (with the last 7 days) and Refunds (filtered). A fold-out called Breakdown by source, method and status splits the same figures three ways. The figures follow whatever filters you have set.

How to find a payment

  1. Go to Money → Payments.
  2. Use the quick date buttons: All time, Today, Last 7 days, This month, Last 30 days, YTD.
  3. Or use the filter boxes: Booking Type (Room, Conference, Gym, Event, and the till), Booking ID, Quick Search, Status, Payment Method, Start Date, End Date.
  4. Click Filter. Click Reset to clear everything.
  5. Click the eye button on a row to open the payment.

Quick Search matches payment reference, receipt number, invoice number, booking reference, transaction reference, method, status, the booking number, the amount, and the guest or company name, email, phone and reference. Results show 50 to a page.

The list normally shows only the kinds of payment your hotel has switched on. If older records are hidden you will see "Showing records for your active modules only" with a link Show all history. Click it to see everything.

Pending refunds show up when you set Status to Pending, because a pending refund has the status Pending. They are marked as refunds in the Type and Reference columns. To list refunds by their refund stage, add refund_status=pending to the Payments page address (also processing, completed, failed or rejected). Only refund lines are then shown, and the page counts it as an active filter.

Each row has these buttons: the eye (View Details), the invoice button (only when invoicing is on), the undo arrow (Process Refund, only for Paid or Completed payments that are not refunds), the pencil (Edit, only for payments that are not yet Paid or Completed), and a WhatsApp button when the guest has a phone number. The Receipt / Txn column shows the receipt number, or Missing if a Paid or Completed payment has none.

Click Export CSV to download the filtered list as a spreadsheet.

How to record a payment

  1. Click Record Payment on the Payments page. You can also open Money → Add Payment, or click Collect next to an account on the outstanding balances list.
  2. Step 1, Booking: choose the type: Room Booking, Conference, Gym or Event. Only types your hotel uses are shown.
  3. Type at least 2 characters of the guest or contact name, reference, or email in the search box. Click the right result. You will see the card with Amount Due, dates, total and what is paid. Click Change Booking to pick a different one.
  4. Step 2, Payment Method: click one of Cash, Bank Transfer, Credit Card, Debit Card, Mobile Money, Cheque or Other. If you skip this you will see "Please choose a payment method."
  5. Fill in Transaction Reference if there is one: a bank reference, cheque number or mobile money transaction ID.
  6. Step 3, Amount & Details: enter Payment Amount. This is the full amount received, including VAT. Set Payment Date (today by default).
  7. Choose Payment Status. It starts on Completed. Use Completed or Paid for money you have in hand. Use Pending or Partial for money not yet confirmed.
  8. Optional: Additional CC Emails (separate with commas) and Notes.
  9. Check the Payment Preview on the right: Subtotal, VAT (only when VAT is on), Total to record, and Outstanding after this payment.
  10. Click Record Payment, then confirm. You will see "Payment recorded successfully." and the payment details page opens.

Amount rules: it must be more than zero and not above 99,999,999. Example: a guest owes MWK 150,000 and hands over MWK 100,000 cash. Enter 100000, method Cash, status Completed. The balance becomes MWK 50,000.

If the booking already has no balance, a box "Booking fully paid" appears and the amount box stays locked until you tick Allow payment anyway (adjustment / credit).

What goes out automatically when you save as Paid or Completed:

  • Room booking: an invoice email and a receipt email go to the guest, with your CC addresses and the hotel's invoice CC list.
  • Conference booking: an invoice email goes out with the same CC addresses.
  • Gym and Event: nothing is sent by itself. Use Send Receipt on the payment page (see below).

Clicking Record Payment twice by accident does not record it twice. You will see "Payment already recorded (reference). Duplicate submission ignored."

What happens when a guest pays too much

Enter what the guest actually handed over. If it is more than they owe, the system keeps the booking at zero balance and moves the extra into a credit note.

  1. Record the payment as Completed or Paid.
  2. You will see "Overpayment detected — credit note CN... for ... issued and queued under Credit Notes."
  3. On the booking, the extra shows as a refund line with reason Overpayment, paid by credit note. The booking reads Paid with no balance.
  4. If the guest has an email address, the credit note is emailed to them.

Example: a guest owes MWK 40,000 and pays MWK 50,000. The booking is settled and a credit note for MWK 10,000 is issued. A cancelled booking is never converted this way; its paid money stays as credit that staff refund on purpose.

How to edit a payment

  1. Open the payment and click Edit, or click the pencil on the Payments list.
  2. Change the method, date, amount, status, CC emails or notes. The booking cannot be changed.
  3. Click Update Payment and confirm. You will see "Payment updated successfully".
  • The amount is the full amount received. The VAT split keeps the rate the payment was first recorded with.
  • Marking a Pending payment as Paid or Completed gives it a receipt number and recalculates the balance.
  • Refund lines cannot be edited. Use Settle this refund instead.
  • A payment with refunds against it is locked: its amount and status cannot be changed.
  • If an edit raises the amount above what is owed, the extra becomes a credit note, as described above.

How to send or reprint a receipt

  1. Open the payment (Paid or Completed, not a refund).
  2. Click Send Receipt. A window opens. After you record a new payment this window may open for you.
  3. To email: check the Email address and click Send email. To use WhatsApp: check the number and click Send WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens in a new tab with the message ready.
  4. Close the window with Done.

If the box is empty you will see "Enter an email address." or "Enter a phone number." For till payments the window also has a Print button.

How to refund a payment

  1. Open the payment and click Refund (or the undo arrow on the Payments list, then Open Refund to confirm).
  2. Check the strip: Original Payment, Already Refunded, Refundable Balance. Earlier refunds appear under Refund History.
  3. Refund to: Original payment method returns the money the way it was paid. Store credit (credit note) appears only when the hotel setting allows staff to choose. It is settled at once and a credit note is issued.
  4. Enter Refund Amount, or click Full, 50% or Clear. It cannot be more than the refundable balance.
  5. Choose Refund Reason: Early Checkout, Late Checkout Charge, Cancellation, Service Issue, Overpayment or Other. This reason is shown to the customer in the refund email.
  6. Choose Refund Status: Pending, Processing, Completed or Failed. Use Pending if the money has not gone out yet. Use Completed only when it has.
  7. Add Refund Notes if you want. They are internal and the customer does not see them.
  8. Check the Refund Summary and click Process Refund, then confirm.
  9. You will see "Refund processed" and "Refund created successfully! Reference: REF-...". A box shows whether the customer was emailed. If not, it says "Email not sent — notify the customer manually".

Mobile money refunds cannot be saved as Completed. They must start as Pending or Processing until the provider confirms.

Example: a guest paid MWK 200,000 and leaves a day early. Refund MWK 60,000, reason Early Checkout, status Pending. Later, when the money is sent, settle it (next section).

How to settle a pending refund

  1. Open the refund line (reference starting with REF). A panel Settle this refund shows when it is Pending or Processing.
  2. In Move to choose Processing (sent to provider), Completed (money paid out) or Failed (money not paid out).
  3. Add a Note (optional) such as a provider reference.
  4. Click Update refund and confirm. You will see "Refund REF-... is now completed." (or the status you chose).

A Pending refund can go to Processing, Completed or Failed. A Processing refund can go to Completed or Failed. Nothing else is allowed. Store-credit refunds cannot be settled here; void the credit note to reverse one.

How to see who still owes money

  1. On the Payments page click View Outstanding Balances.
  2. You will see every account with a balance, largest first: Reference, Client, Type, Total, Paid, Outstanding, Status. The top shows the number of accounts and the total owed.
  3. Click Collect to record a payment for that account, or the eye to open it.
  4. Click Back to payment ledger to return.

A balance of one tambala (0.01) or less counts as paid. Anything above that is shown as owing.

Invoices

Money → Invoices (page title Invoices & Credit Notes). It lists one line per payment, with the invoice number once an invoice exists. Filters: Booking Type, Payment Status, Search (invoice number, payment number, booking reference). Changing a dropdown applies at once. The fold-out Receivables aging and outstanding by source groups unpaid amounts into 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ day age bands.

Credit notes

Money → Credit Notes. A credit note is a stored amount owed to a guest that can be used against a later bill. Figures at the top: Total Issued, Total Redeemed, Outstanding Balance, Status Breakdown. Statuses: Active, Partial (partially applied), Used (fully applied), Voided, Expired.

Issue a credit note

  1. Click Issue Credit Note.
  2. Optional: tick Link to Existing Booking and pick the booking. Leave it unticked for a walk-in.
  3. Enter the guest name (required), email, Credit Note Value (more than zero), and a Reason: Goodwill Gesture, Booking Cancellation, Service Issue / Complaint, Early Checkout, Overpayment, Pricing / Billing Error or Other. Add notes if useful.
  4. Tick Send credit note by email to guest if wanted, then click Issue Credit Note.
  5. You will see "Credit note CN-... issued successfully."

Apply a credit note to a booking

  1. Click the double-tick button on an Active or Partial note.
  2. Choose the Booking Type, search for the booking, and click it.
  3. Enter Amount to Apply and optional notes, then click Apply Credit Note.
  4. You will see "Credit note applied: ... Remaining balance: ...". The amount is capped at the lower of what you typed, the note balance, and what the booking still owes.

Other actions

Click the three-dot button on a row for View PDF, Redemption history, Regenerate PDF, Resend email (only if the guest has an email) and Void credit note (only for Active or Partial notes). Voiding asks for Reason for Voiding and cannot be undone. It gives the money back to the booking. The note then shows as Voided.

Expiry: a new note expires after the number of months set in Hotel Settings (Credit notes expire after (months, 0 = never)). The Expires column shows the date. Expired notes cannot be applied.

Quotations

Money → Quotations. Quotations are created from the booking or enquiry. This page tracks them. Filters: Search, Type, Status (Sent, Accepted, Expired, Declined), From, To. Figures: Total Issued, Sent / Active, Accepted, Expired, Declined, Total Quoted Value.

  • PDF downloads the quotation.
  • Resend emails it again (not available once accepted). Only room quotations can be resent here; resend conference and event quotations from their own enquiry page.
  • Booking opens the booking.
  • The tick marks it accepted. For a room it confirms a pending or tentative booking: "Quotation marked as accepted and booking confirmed." An expired one gives "Cannot accept an expired quotation (expired date)."
  • The cross marks it declined: "Quotation marked as declined."

A quotation still marked Sent after its Valid Until date changes to Expired when you open the page.

Receipts register

Money → Receipts lists Paid and Completed payments with their receipt status. Figures: Total receipts, Missing numbers, Generated PDFs, Receipt value. Filter by type, status (Missing No., Generated, Emailed), dates and search. Ten rows show per page.

  • Generate Missing creates receipts for up to 500 payments that have none: "Generated receipts for N completed payment(s)."
  • The row buttons generate or regenerate the PDF, view it, send by WhatsApp or Email receipt (enter the address, click Send Receipt).
  • Export CSV downloads the filtered list. Recent receipt activity shows the last 20 actions.
  • The page also holds the receipt email and WhatsApp wording. Without Edit message templates it is view-only. With it, edit and click Save Templates. All three fields are required.

Accounting dashboard and VAT

Money → Accounting. Pick a period with This month, Today, 7 days, 30 days, This year, All time, or set From and To and click Apply. It opens on this month.

Needs attention lists things to chase: balances owed, pending payments, refunds waiting to be settled, quotations awaiting a reply, voided till orders, stock lost. The links on this page open the Payments list already filtered: Open today's payments and Today's payments show today's date range, and the completed and pending links show that status for the chosen period. The tabs below show Recent payments, Receivables, Daily detail, Refunds, POS by type and Compliance.

VAT settings

  1. On the Accounting page open VAT settings (or click VAT at the top).
  2. It shows the VAT mode, Rate and VAT Registration No. Without the Change VAT & refund settings permission you can only read it.
  3. With permission, click Unlock to Edit and answer Yes, unlock to edit.
  4. Choose the mode: No VAT, Prices include VAT (totals equal listed prices; documents show only the rate) or VAT added on top of prices (VAT is itemised).
  5. Enter VAT Rate (%) (0 to 100) and VAT Registration Number.
  6. Click Save VAT Settings. You will see "VAT settings updated successfully."

The new rate applies to future payments only. Existing invoices are not recalculated. When you record a payment, the VAT part is taken out of the amount received using that account's own VAT share; the rate in settings is used only when the account has no VAT figures. Refund rules (who is refunded automatically, and whether staff may choose store credit) are in Hotel Settings under Refunds & cancellations.

Rules the system enforces

  • Only Paid or Completed payments that are not refunds can be refunded. Total refunds can never exceed the amount paid.
  • Refunded or Partially refunded cannot be picked by hand. You will see "Refunded statuses are set by the refund process only. Use "Process Refund" on the payment details page."
  • A collected payment cannot be changed to Cancelled or Refunded by editing. Refund it instead.
  • You cannot lower a payment below what has already been refunded from it.
  • No payments on a cancelled, no-show or expired room booking. A checked-out booking accepts payments only while a balance remains.
  • Paid and Completed payments get a receipt number automatically.
  • A balance of 0.01 or less counts as settled.
