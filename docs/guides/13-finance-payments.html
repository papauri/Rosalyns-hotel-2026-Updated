Finance & Payments

Record payments, generate invoices and receipts, issue credit notes, send quotations, and manage the full payment lifecycle for every booking.

1. Adding a Payment

Use Add Payment (/admin/payment-add.php) to record any money received or owed against a booking. Reach it from:

  • A booking details page → Add Payment button
  • Admin sidebar → Finance → Add Payment
  • Invoices page header → New Payment

Payment form fields

Field What to enter
Payment date Date money was received (defaults to today).
Amount Gross amount before VAT if VAT-inclusive, or the net amount if you track VAT separately.
Method Cash · Card (manual) · Card (POS terminal) · Mobile money · Bank transfer · Credit note
Type Payment · Deposit · Part-payment · Balance payment · Advance · Refund · Adjustment
Status Completed / Paid = money in hand. Pending = awaiting clearance. Refunded = money returned.
Transaction ref Mpesa code, POS transaction ID, bank reference — anything that links to the transaction.
Notes Internal notes (not shown to guest).
CC emails Comma-separated extra email addresses to copy on any invoice/receipt email sent.

Document actions

Tick any combination before saving:

  • Generate invoice — creates a PDF invoice for this payment.
  • Email invoice — sends the invoice PDF to the guest (and CC addresses).
  • Generate receipt — creates a PDF receipt.
  • Email receipt — sends the receipt to the guest.
  • Generate quotation — creates a quote PDF (useful before payment is collected).
  • Send quotation — emails the quote; set validity days and optional notes in the fields that appear.
Double-submission protection: a hidden idempotency ID prevents the same payment being saved twice if the form is accidentally submitted more than once. The system detects the duplicate and redirects you to the existing record.

Paying with a credit note

  1. Select Method → Credit note.
  2. An extra field appears — enter the CN-YYYY-NNNNNN number.
  3. The system verifies the CN is active and has enough balance.
  4. The CN balance decrements automatically and the booking amount-due updates.

Editing a payment

Open the payment via /admin/payment-add.php?edit=N or from the Edit link in the payments ledger. All fields and document actions are available. VAT and booking totals recalculate automatically on save.

Refund type requires Refunded status. If you set Type = Refund, Status must be Refunded — the form will not save otherwise.

2. Payments Ledger

/admin/payments.php — every payment row across rooms and conferences, with filters, search, and inline actions.

Filters

  • Date range — filter by payment date.
  • Method — cash, card, mobile money, bank transfer, credit note.
  • Status — pending, partial, completed, refunded, cancelled.
  • Search — payment reference, booking reference, guest name.

Inline actions per row

  • View — full payment details with audit log.
  • Edit — opens payment-add in edit mode.
  • Refund — opens the refund flow (see Refunds).
  • PDF / Resend — regenerate or email invoice/receipt.

3. Invoices

/admin/invoices.php — lists every payment as an invoice with generation status, receivables aging, and document actions.

KPI tiles at a glance

Tile What it means
Paid & Closed Total value of completed/paid invoices. Sub-line shows count + MTD total.
Outstanding Value still owed on pending/partial invoices. Click to filter the list.
Generated How many have had a PDF created, plus coverage %.
Refunded / Credit Refund total + count of credit notes issued from this page.

Receivables aging

Outstanding invoices are bucketed by how old they are:

Bucket Action recommended
0 – 30 days Routine — send a polite reminder.
31 – 60 days Follow up by phone.
61 – 90 days Escalate to manager.
90+ days Highlighted red — immediate action required.

Per-row actions

  • Resend Invoice — emails the existing invoice PDF to the guest.
  • Send Reminder — same email, different subject ("Payment Reminder").
  • Regenerate Invoice — rebuilds the PDF and assigns a new invoice number if one was missing.
  • Generate Credit Note — only available for refund-type payments. Issues a CN against the refunded amount and links it to the invoice.
Invoice numbers are sequential and permanent — INV-YYYY-NNNNNN. You cannot edit a number; you can only regenerate the document.

4. Receipts

/admin/receipts.php — the receipt register for every completed payment. Covers PDF generation, email delivery, WhatsApp sending, bulk backfill, and template editing.

KPI tiles

Tile What it means
Receipt rows Total matched by the current filters.
Missing numbers Completed payments with no receipt number yet — run Backfill to fix.
Generated PDFs Receipts where a PDF file has been created.
Receipt value Sum of matched payment amounts.

Filters

  • Type — All / Rooms / Conference / Restaurant & POS
  • Status — All / Missing number / PDF generated / Emailed
  • Date range — filter by payment date
  • Search — receipt number, payment reference, booking reference, notes

Per-row actions

  • Generate — creates the PDF receipt file.
  • View — opens the PDF in a new tab (visible once generated).
  • Email — sends the receipt to the guest email (you can override the address in the modal).
  • WhatsApp — opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message in a new tab using the guest's registered phone number.

Bulk backfill

Click Generate Missing in the page header to auto-generate PDFs for up to 500 completed payments that have no receipt yet. Useful after a system migration or when receipts were skipped.

CSV export

Click Export CSV to download up to 5,000 rows matching the current filters. Columns: Receipt Number, Payment Reference, Type, Booking Reference, Payment Date, Method, Payment Type, Status, Amount, Generated, Emailed At, Recorded By.

Editing receipt templates

Scroll to the Receipt Templates panel at the bottom of the page. You can customise three fields saved to Site Settings:

  • Email subject — shown in the guest's inbox subject line.
  • Email body (HTML) — full HTML template for the email body. Preview renders a live sample below the field.
  • WhatsApp message — plain-text template for the WA deep link.

Available placeholders in receipt templates:

Placeholder Replaced with
{{site_name}} Hotel name
{{guest_name}} Guest full name
{{receipt_number}} Receipt number (e.g. REC-2026-000123)

{{total_amount}} Total including VAT

Click Preview next to the template fields to see a rendered sample before saving.

5. Credit Notes

/admin/credit-notes.php — issue, apply, void, regenerate, and resend credit notes. Credit notes are used when a refund is owed but cash cannot be returned immediately.

When to issue a credit note

  • Early checkout — guest departs before their scheduled check-out date.
  • Service issue — compensation for a problem during the stay.
  • Goodwill gesture — complimentary credit for a returning guest.
  • Overpayment — guest paid more than the booking total.
  • Pricing error — a booking was charged at an incorrect rate.
  • Cancellation — when a refund cannot be processed in cash immediately.

CN lifecycle

Active → Partially applied → Fully used
                   ↓
Voided (irreversible)      Expired (auto, past expiry date)

KPI tiles

Tile What it shows
Total issued Count and face value of all CNs.
Total redeemed Sum of all amounts applied against bookings.
Outstanding Balance remaining on active / partially-applied CNs.
Status counts Active · Partially applied · Fully used · Voided · Expired

Issuing a credit note

  1. Click Issue Credit Note.
  2. Enter the guest name (required), guest email, and amount (required, must be > 0).
  3. Select a reason: Goodwill · Cancellation · Service issue · Early checkout · Overpayment · Pricing error · Other.
  4. Add reason notes for a fuller explanation (logged for audit).
  5. Optionally link to an existing booking by selecting it with the live search.
  6. Tick Send email to guest to dispatch the CN PDF immediately.
  7. Click Issue. The CN number CN-YYYY-NNNNNN is assigned automatically.
Expiry: CNs expire after the period set in Booking Settings (credit_note_expiry_months). Set to 0 for no expiry. The system auto-expires stale CNs on every page load.

Applying a credit note to a booking

  1. Find the CN in the list and click Apply.
  2. The available balance is shown.
  3. Select the booking type and search for the booking by reference or guest name.
  4. Enter the amount to apply (cannot exceed the CN balance).
  5. Add optional notes and click Apply Credit.
  6. A credit_note payment row is inserted against the booking; the booking's amount-due decreases.

Voiding a credit note

  1. Click Void on the CN row.
  2. Enter a void reason (required).
  3. Confirm — this is irreversible. The CN cannot be re-activated.

Other actions

  • Regen PDF — rebuilds the credit note PDF (useful if the template was updated).
  • Resend Email — re-emails the CN PDF to the guest.
  • History — view every application and redemption event for the CN.

6. Quotations

/admin/quotations.php — manage quotations (price proposals) sent to guests before payment is collected. Quotations are typically used for tentative bookings and conference inquiries.

Quotation statuses

Status Meaning
Sent Quotation emailed; awaiting guest response.
Accepted Guest confirmed they will proceed.
Expired Validity date passed; auto-set by the system.
Declined Guest declined the quote.

Per-row actions

  • Download PDF — serves the stored PDF or regenerates it if the file is missing.
  • Resend — re-emails the quotation to the guest; resets status to Sent.
  • Mark Accepted — record that the guest agreed to the quote.
  • Mark Declined — record that the guest declined.
  • View booking — opens the linked booking or conference inquiry.
Quotations are never deleted — they are a permanent audit trail. Expired quotes are auto-set by the system each time the page loads.

Sending a quotation from Add Payment

You can generate and email a quotation at the same time you record a payment by ticking the Generate Quotation and Send Quotation checkboxes on the Add Payment form. Set the validity period (days) and add optional notes for the guest.

7. Refunds

Use /admin/payment-refund.php to process a refund against an existing payment.

Refund workflow

  1. Open the original payment from the Payments Ledger → click Refund.
  2. Enter the refund amount (cannot exceed the original payment).
  3. Select the refund method (usually the same as the original).
  4. Enter a reason — this is mandatory and logged.
  5. Choose: Cash refund (money returned) or Credit note (issue a CN instead — use this when you can't return cash immediately).
  6. Save. The booking's amount_paid and amount_due are recalculated automatically.
Refunds reduce the booking's paid total. If a refund would make the booking balance go negative, the system will alert you before saving.

Viewing pending refunds

The dashboard Refunds Pending tile (Operations Pulse section) shows refunds with status pending or processing. Click it to filter the Payments Ledger to those rows.

8. VAT & Tax Settings

Configure VAT in Admin → Finance → Accounting Dashboard → scroll to VAT Settings at the bottom.

Setting Notes
VAT enabled Tick to apply VAT to all new payments. Untick for zero-rated periods.
VAT rate (%) Numeric, 0–100. Current Malawi standard rate is 16.5%.
VAT number Printed on every invoice and receipt PDF.

VAT is calculated automatically on every Add Payment form:

VAT amount = payment amount × (VAT rate ÷ 100)
Total = payment amount + VAT amount
VAT settings are stored in site_settings. Changes take effect on the next Add Payment action — they do not retroactively modify existing payment records.
