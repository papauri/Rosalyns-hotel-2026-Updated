Staff & Access

How to add staff, invite them to set their own password, decide exactly what each person can open, and handle sign-in problems.

Administrator (a Manager only if given the staff permissions below)
Where to find it
Admin menu → Settings → Staff & Access (/admin/user-management.php)
Permissions needed
Create Users to add people, Edit Users to change them and resend invitations, Assign Permissions to change what they can open, Delete Users to remove them. An Administrator has all of these.

What is on the screen

  • Add New User button — opens the form for a new staff member.
  • Role filter buttons (All, then one per role) — show only the people with that role.
  • The staff table — name, username, email, role, status and last activity. Each row has buttons:
    • Edit — name, email, role, active/inactive, new password.
    • Permissions — tick exactly which pages and actions this person may use. Not shown for Administrators (they always have everything).
    • Envelope button — resend the invitation.
    • Bin button — delete the person.

You only see the buttons you have permission for, and only for people below your own level (see Rules).

How to add a new staff member and invite them

  1. Click Add New User.
  2. Full Name — e.g. Jane Banda. This appears in the invitation and on records they create.
  3. Username — what they type to sign in, e.g. jane.b. At least 3 characters: letters, numbers, dots, dashes and underscores only, no spaces. It must not already be used (capitals don't matter: Jane.B and jane.b are the same) and it cannot be changed later.
  4. Email — their own working email address. The invitation is sent here and password resets go here.
  5. Role — choose the closest role (see the table above). Nothing is chosen for you: the form will not save until you pick one.
  6. Password — leave this blank. The person will choose their own from the invitation. (Only type one if you must give it to them in person; it must be at least 8 characters.)
  7. Leave Send invitation email ticked.
  8. If the role's normal permissions are fine, leave Set their permissions before the invitation goes out unticked and click Create User. You will see "User '…' created successfully. Invitation sent to … (link valid 72 h)."
  9. If you want to choose their permissions first, tick it and follow the next section.

A username or email that is already in use shows a red message under the box as soon as you finish typing it, and Create User will not go through until you change it. While the account is being created and the invitation sent, the button reads "Creating and sending invitation..." — wait for it; do not click again. If anything stops the save, the form opens again with what you typed and the reason in red at the top.

No password is ever emailed. The invitation contains their username, role and a one-time Accept invitation & set your password button.

How to set permissions before the invitation goes out

Use this when the person needs more or less than their role normally gets, so their very first sign-in already has the right access.

  1. In Add New User, fill in the form as above and tick Set their permissions before the invitation goes out.
  2. Click Create User. You will see "… Choose their permissions, then press "Save & send invitation"." and the permissions window opens by itself for that person.
  3. Tick or untick permissions (see How to change what someone can open).
  4. Click Save & send invitation. The permissions are saved first, then the invitation is sent. You will see "Permissions updated successfully. Invitation sent to …"

If you close the window without saving, the account exists but no invitation has been sent and nobody can sign in to it. Open Permissions later, save, then use the Resend invitation button to send the invitation.

What the new person does with the invitation

  1. Open the email titled "You're invited to Rosalyns Beach Hotel — accept your staff account".
  2. Click Accept invitation & set your password. The page Accept your invitation opens on the hotel's own website.
  3. Type a password of at least 8 characters twice and save.
  4. Go to the sign-in page and sign in with the username from the email and the new password.
  • The link works once and expires after 72 hours (the exact time is printed in the email).
  • The link opens on the same website address the administrator was using when they sent it.
  • Sending a new invitation cancels any earlier link that was not used.

How to change what someone can open

  1. Click Permissions on the person's row. The window Edit Permissions opens with their current ticks.
  2. Permissions are grouped by area (Core, Reservations, Guest Services, Property, Content, Restaurant & Stock, Stations, Finance, Settings, System, Administration). Tick what they may do; untick what they may not.
  3. Shortcuts: Select All, Deselect All, Role Defaults. Role Defaults ticks exactly the permissions their role gets by default and unticks everything else. Nothing is saved until you click Save Permissions.
  4. Click Save Permissions. You will see "Permissions updated successfully."

On a phone, the Change password and Logout buttons are at the bottom of the menu (open it with the Menu button). On a narrow phone they are not in the top bar.

Their menu changes on their next page load: items they cannot open disappear, and opening such a page directly sends them back to a page they are allowed to see.

Two Reservations permissions are deliberately not part of any role's defaults: Delete & Restore Bookings (remove duplicate or test bookings; every delete records who, when and why and can be undone) and Edit Booking Financials (complimentary upgrades, keeping the original rate on a downgrade). Administrators always have both, and Managers can also authorise complimentary upgrades; tick them only for people you trust with those decisions.

What each person sees

The dashboard, the menu, favourites, search and the links inside pages all follow two things: the person's Permissions and the parts of the system switched on in Modules. A link to a page they cannot open is simply not shown. For example, a receptionist without finance permissions sees no takings, balances or money figures, and a POS-only user sees only the POS.

  • If someone does not have the Dashboard permission, they land on the first page they are allowed to open (for example Bookings, POS Till or Payments) when they sign in.
  • If they are allowed no page at all, they see No accessible pages with a Sign out button. Ask an administrator to tick their permissions.
  • Administrators always see everything.

What each booking action needs

Buttons the person may not use are hidden or greyed out. The system also refuses the action if they try it another way, with "You do not have permission to do that (Edit Bookings)." (the name in brackets is the permission they are missing).

Check a guest in (and undo a check-in)Check-in Guests
Check a guest outCheck-out Guests (and Check-out With Balance if they still owe money)
Cancel a booking, or mark a no-showCancel Bookings
Mark a booking paid, or change a paymentAdd Payments
Change the dates, Confirm Booking, Convert to Confirmed (tentative bookings), add or void a charge on the guest's billEdit Bookings
Change a room's status on the Room DashboardHousekeeping or Room Maintenance (either one)

Charges that come in from POS or room-service orders are added by those orders and do not need Edit Bookings.

The normal Receptionist role has Check-in Guests, Check-out Guests, Add Payments and Receipts, so reception can take a payment and print the receipt at checkout. It does not have Cancel Bookings or Edit Bookings. Tick those only for receptionists you trust with them.

"Nobody" means no role ticks it for you, but an Administrator always has it. Tick it for a person only when they need it. A permission only has an effect if the part of the system it belongs to is switched on in Modules.

Example: set up a receptionist

Goal: a front-desk person who checks guests in and out, confirms bookings and takes payments, but cannot see the company's finance reports.

  1. Open Settings → Staff & Access and click Add New User.
  2. Fill in the name, username and email. Choose the role Receptionist.
  3. Tick Set their permissions before the invitation goes out and click Create User.
  4. In the permissions window the Receptionist ticks are already set (Dashboard, View Bookings, Calendar View, Check-in Guests, Check-out Guests, Add Payments, Receipts and more). Leave them.
  5. Also tick Edit Bookings (to confirm bookings and change dates), Add Payments (to mark a booking paid) and, if they may cancel, Cancel Bookings.
  6. Leave the Finance permissions unticked.
  7. Click Save & send invitation.

When they sign in, their menu shows only the front-desk pages, and no money figures appear on their dashboard.

How to edit, deactivate or change someone's role

  1. Click Edit on their row.
  2. Change Full Name, Email or Role as needed. Username is read-only.
  3. To stop someone signing in without deleting their history, untick Active Account.
  4. To set a password for them directly, type it in New Password (leave blank to keep current) (at least 8 characters). Normally use the invitation or "Forgot password" instead.
  5. Click Save Changes.

Changing someone's role resets their permissions to the new role's defaults. You will see "Permissions reset to role defaults." Re-apply any custom ticks afterwards.

How to resend an invitation

Use this when the first email was lost, the 72 hours passed, or you created the account with "set permissions first" and closed the window.

  1. Click the Resend invitation button on their row and confirm.
  2. You will see "Invitation sent to … (link valid 72 h)." Any earlier unused link stops working.

Resending does not change their current password. It also works as a "set a new password" link for someone who has forgotten theirs.

How to delete a staff member

  1. Click the bin button on their row.
  2. In the Delete User window, click Delete User. You will see "User deleted successfully." Their permissions are removed with them.

Prefer deactivating (untick Active Account) for people who have left: their name stays on the bookings and payments they handled.

Passwords and signing in

Forgot password (staff do this themselves)

  1. On the sign-in page click Forgot Password?
  2. Enter the email address on their account and send.
  3. Open the email and click the link within 1 hour, then choose a new password (at least 8 characters).

Change my password (while signed in)

  1. Click the key button in the top bar.
  2. Enter the current password and the new one twice (at least 8 characters, different from the current one).
  3. A 6-digit code is emailed to you. Enter it within 10 minutes to confirm. You will see "Your password has been changed. Use it from your next sign-in."

Automatic sign-out

You stay signed in for your whole shift. After 8 hours with no activity you are signed out and must sign in again. Using any admin page counts as activity, so a busy shift never signs you out.

After a system update: everyone is signed out once and must sign in again. This is normal and happens one time only. If you are signed out earlier than 8 hours, tell the administrator.

Who's online

This panel shows who is signed in and using the system right now. It is at the top of Staff & Access and on System Logs (Settings → System Logs). Only an Administrator or a Manager sees it. Other roles do not see the panel at all. It only shows information. It does not sign anyone out and it does not keep anyone signed in.

  • The heading Who's online has a count beside it, for example 3 online.
  • Each row shows the person's name and role, with (you) beside your own name.
  • Active now means they used the system in the last 2 minutes. Idle 7 min means they have been quiet for about that long. Idle shows up to 30 minutes.
  • The row also shows the screen they have open, and Signed in with the time they signed in (the date too, if it was not today).
  • Administrators also see each person's IP address (the device's address on the network). Managers do not.
  • The panel updates by itself every minute. You do not need to reload the page.

Someone drops off the list after 30 minutes with no activity, for example a tab left open and untouched. When someone clicks Logout, they leave the list straight away. If nobody is signed in you will see Nobody is signed in right now.

Use it to see who is at the desk before you ask someone to check a booking, or to spot a sign-in you do not recognise. If you see someone you do not expect, change their password or untick Active Account (see How to edit, deactivate or change someone's role).

Rules the system enforces

  • You can only manage people whose role is below yours (a Manager cannot edit another Manager or an Administrator). Administrators can manage everyone.
  • Someone who is not an Administrator cannot give permissions they do not have themselves.
  • Administrator permissions cannot be changed — an Administrator always has everything.
  • The last active Administrator cannot be deactivated, demoted or deleted.
  • You cannot delete your own account.
  • Usernames and email addresses must be unique. A duplicate is flagged under the box while you type, and again when you save.
  • After 5 wrong passwords an account is locked for 15 minutes. Too many failures from one location also blocks sign-in from there for 15 minutes.
  • Deactivated accounts cannot sign in or reset their password.

If invitations don't arrive

  1. Open Settings → Hotel Settings and find the Email Settings card. Check SMTP Host, SMTP Port, SMTP Security (SSL, TLS or None), SMTP Username and SMTP Password. The password box is always empty. Type a password only to change it. Click Save Email Settings.
  2. Ask your web host or email provider whether outgoing port 465 is open. If it is blocked, ask which port and security setting they give you, and enter those in Email Settings.
  3. If you see "Invalid security token. Please try again.", sign out, sign in again, and repeat the step that failed.
  4. Click the Resend invitation button on the person's row to send a fresh invitation. Any earlier link stops working.
