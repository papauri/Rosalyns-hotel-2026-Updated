The Assistant

The Assistant is a chat helper for staff in the Rosalyns Beach Hotel admin. The first part shows staff how to use it; the rest covers setting it up on Assistant Settings: switching it on or off, adding a provider key, choosing models, limits, who has access and usage. It ships switched off: nothing changes for anyone until an administrator sets it up and switches it on.

The Assistant answers questions, looks things up with its team of department leads, gives each person a daily briefing, points you to the right screen and prepares changes as action cards that you confirm with one tap. The nightly clean-up of old chats arrives in a later update.

Who uses it
An administrator sets it up. Staff who are given access then use it.
Where to find it
The chat: the Assistant button at the bottom right of every admin screen. Setting it up: Admin menu → Settings → Assistant Settings (/admin/ai-settings.php)
Permission needed
Assistant Settings to open the page. Assistant Usage to see the Usage table. Using the chat needs Use the Assistant; confirming the changes it suggests needs Confirm Assistant actions.
Only shown when
The settings page is always reachable for people with the permission, so it can always be switched on or off.

Using the Assistant

For everyone who has Use the Assistant. The button only appears once an administrator has switched the Assistant on.

How to use the Assistant

  1. Click the Assistant button (speech bubbles) at the bottom right of any admin screen. The chat opens on the right; on a phone or tablet it fills the screen.
  2. Type your question in plain words in the box at the bottom, for example "Arrivals today" or "Is the Deluxe Room free next Friday for 2 adults?", and press Enter or Send. Shift + Enter starts a new line.
  3. Or tap one of the suggestion buttons above the box. They change with the screen you are on (Bookings, Housekeeping and so on).
  4. The answer appears in the chat. When it mentions a screen or a guide, a button under the answer opens it (guides open in a new tab).
  5. Click Stop while it is answering to cancel. Click the cross, or press Esc, to close the chat. It stays open while you move between admin screens and keeps your conversation.

What it can answer

  • Today's arrivals and departures, who is still to arrive, who has no room yet, who is due out and what they still owe.
  • Who is in house, room status (ready, occupied, cleaning, out of order) and which rooms have an arrival today.
  • Finding a booking by name, email, phone or reference, and the details of one booking (dates, room, party, total, paid, balance).
  • Which room types are free for some dates, and a price for a stay worked out exactly as Create Booking would (rate plans, child charge, VAT and levy included).
  • A guest's past stays, the active rate plans, and (for administrators and managers) who is online.
  • For people with money permissions: who still owes money, and where money is being lost (see the daily briefing).
  • For the departments your account can see: occupancy, ADR and RevPAR, pace and pickup, till sales and slow tickets, stock alerts, conference and gym, reviews and enquiries, housekeeping and maintenance jobs (see the team of leads).
  • How to do anything in the system, from the staff guides (see below).

Figures come from the same places as the screens, so the chat and the screen always agree. Short lookups such as "Arrivals today", "Departures tomorrow", "Who is in house?" and "Room status" are answered straight from the system.

What it cannot do

  • It never changes anything on its own. It can prepare some changes as action cards, and nothing happens until you tap Confirm.
  • It never takes payments or refunds, and never changes stock, recipes, till orders, invoices, credit notes, shift or end-of-day closes, prices, rates, deals, settings or users. Ask it how, and it gives the steps and a button to the screen.
  • It only works on the hotel and this system. Anything else gets one friendly line: "That one's outside the hotel, so I'll leave it there - ask me anything about guests, rooms, your shift or the portal."
  • It never says the system reports to or submits anything to MRA or any tax authority: there is no such connection.

It knows who you are

The Assistant sees only what your own account can see on screen, and checks your permissions on every lookup. If you ask for something outside them, for example takings or revenue without a money permission, it answers in one line: "That's outside what your account can see — a manager or admin can help with that." It never gives the figure, a hint, a total or an average that would reveal it. Ask "What can I do here?" to see what your account can do.

Guides and how-to questions

Ask "How do I check a guest in?" and it answers from the staff guides, with a button to the guide. Ask "Summarise the housekeeping guide" (or one part of it, such as "Summarise the part of the housekeeping guide about assigning cleaning") and it summarises only what the guide says. It only uses guides for screens your account can open.

Privacy: what leaves the hotel

The model that writes the answers runs at the provider chosen in Assistant Settings. Before anything is sent, guest and staff names, emails, phone numbers and booking references are swapped for codes such as [GUEST_3]; card, passport and ID numbers and addresses are removed. The answer comes back with the codes and the real names are put back on the hotel's own server, so the provider never learns who anyone is. Your chats are kept on the hotel's server.

Costs

Quick lookups and their follow-ups, small talk, repeated questions within two minutes, off-topic and out-of-reach requests are answered on the hotel's server and cost nothing. The daily briefing is worked out on the server and costs one short reply. Everyday questions use the cheapest chosen model (Fast); only questions that need real thinking, such as "How can we lift occupancy next month?", use the Primary model. Each person has a daily allowance and the hotel has a monthly cost limit (see budgets and limits).

Action cards: changes with one Confirm

For people who also have Confirm Assistant actions. Ask for a change in plain words, for example "Check out room 12", "Book the Deluxe Room 3 to 5 November for 2 adults" or "Add a deep clean for room 7 tomorrow". The Assistant prepares a card; nothing is changed until you tap Confirm on it.

How to confirm a card

  1. Read the card. Each line shows what changes: the old value (crossed out) and the new value, and for money the total. Lines in red (and a red border) mean something serious, such as a cancellation or a room going off sale.
  2. Fill in anything the card asks for, for example the guest's name, email and phone on a new booking, or the reason for a cancellation. Confirm stays grey until they are filled in.
  3. Tap Confirm. The change is made by the same screen action a click would use, with the same checks, permissions, emails and booking timeline. The card then says "Done." with the screen's own message, or "Not done." with the reason.
  4. Tap Cancel if you do not want it. Nothing is changed.

When the card opens a screen instead

Some things are always finished on the screen, so the card has a button that opens it instead of Confirm: check-out when money is owed or the guest leaves on a different day (the settlement on Bookings), a booking with more than one room type or with a payment (Open in Create Booking, with the guest, dates and party filled in for you), joined family rooms (assigned as a pair on Bookings) and WhatsApp messages (sent from the booking screen).

Safety rules

  • Only you can confirm your cards, once. A card expires after 15 minutes; ask again for a fresh one.
  • When you tap Confirm the card is checked again. If the price or availability changed since it was made, it is not used: a new card with the new figures appears for you to check.
  • Text written by guests (special requests, reviews, enquiries) is treated only as information. It can never make the Assistant change anything.
  • Every confirmed card is written to the activity log as "... via Assistant", and the screen adds its usual booking timeline entries.
  • When you sign out, the names behind the privacy codes in your chats are removed from the server and unconfirmed cards expire; old chats then show (guest) in place of names.

An administrator can turn all cards off (answers only) on Assistant Settings → Budgets and limits → Action cards, or for one person by removing Confirm Assistant actions.

The team of leads behind it

Behind the chat sits a small team of department leads, like the heads of department on a shift. For a simple question the Assistant answers itself. For a question that spans departments, or when you ask for the full picture, it asks up to three leads at the same time, each looks up its own figures, and one merged answer comes back. Each lead sees only what your account can see: a lead you have no permission for simply does not exist for you.

Limits on every question: at most three leads, at most eight lookups in all, and the Most tokens for one question limit on Assistant Settings. None of the leads can change anything.

The daily briefing ("the tea")

The first time you open the Assistant each day it gives you a short briefing: what needs you today, most important first. Ask for it any time with "Give me the tea", "What did I miss?" or "Brief me". It is worked out from the hotel's data on the hotel's own server; only the short list of findings goes to the model, to be explained in two to four sentences.

Each point is set out like a line in a report: the headline figure, what it is, why it matters, which screen it comes from, and one button that opens that screen already filtered (for example today's check-outs, or reviews needing a reply).

It never guesses

Every amount, booking, reference, date, count or name in an answer comes from the hotel's data, looked up for that question. If nothing is found it says so plainly, for example "I can't find a booking with that reference - check it and try again?", and offers the next step. It never gives an estimate as if it were a fact.

The system checks every answer before you see it. A figure that is not in the data is sent back to be looked up; if it still cannot be confirmed, that sentence is removed and the answer ends with "I couldn't confirm part of that from the hotel's data, so I left it out."

Talking to it

Talk to it like a colleague. Follow-ups work: after "Arrivals today" ask "And tomorrow?" and it repeats the same lookup for tomorrow straight from the system, without asking the model again. A greeting, a thank-you or a word about a hard shift gets a short friendly reply, and it gives practical hospitality advice (handling a complaint, welcoming a guest). It stays on the hotel, its guests, your shift and this portal; anything else gets one friendly line steering back.

While it works, one plain line under your question names the step it is on, for example "Checking today's arrivals and departures..." or "Asking the Money, Front Desk lead...".

How to add a provider key

A provider is the company whose models answer the questions. You need an account and a key from at least one. The provider bills that account directly.

  1. Get a key from the provider's website (the hint under each box says where).
  2. Open Settings → Assistant Settings.
  3. On that provider's card paste the key into the box and click Save and test.
  4. The page saves the key, lists the provider's models and sends one short test message. A green message such as "Key saved for Anthropic. Connected. 7 chat models found; Claude Opus 5.5 answered the test in 640 ms." means it works.

The key is saved scrambled and is never shown again: the card shows only four dots and its last four characters. To replace it, paste a new one. Test connection checks the saved key again; it costs a fraction of a cent. Remove key deletes it after you confirm, and un-chooses that provider's models.

DeepSeek is hosted in China. Questions sent to it are processed there.

How to choose the models

  1. After a key passes the test, its models appear under Models, most capable first.
  2. The five most capable are ticked as a suggestion, with roles filled in. Change the ticks if you like: up to 5.
  3. Give each ticked model a role: Primary answers questions and plans (exactly one); Fast does short replies and routing; Fallback takes over if Primary fails; Extra is kept ready.
  4. Check the prices. A model marked New - set price is not in the built-in list: type its prices from the provider's price page (US dollars per million tokens in and out).
  5. Click Save models. That provider becomes the one In use. Only one provider is used at a time.

Check for newer models reads the provider's list again. The system also does this once a week while the Assistant is on. Your chosen models and typed prices are never changed by it. Newest marks the most recently released model in the list.

How to switch it on or off

  1. Make sure a key passed Test connection and you saved models with one Primary.
  2. In On or off click Switch on. The line then reads, for example, "On - using Anthropic (Claude Opus 5.5), 3 people have access."
  3. To stop it for everyone click Switch off.

The Assistant is also listed on Settings → Modules under Staff Tools. It needs both switches on; switching either off hides the button for everyone at once.

Switching off never deletes chats, keys, models or limits; switching on again carries on where it was. The Assistant can also pause itself, for example when the monthly cost limit is reached; the line then starts "Paused automatically:" with the reason. Clicking Switch on again lifts the pause.

How to set budgets and limits

  • Daily limit per person (tokens): words read plus written per person per day, counted in tokens (a token is about three quarters of a word). 1,000 to 50,000,000. Default 200,000.
  • Monthly cost limit (US$): when the estimated cost this month reaches it, the Assistant stops sending questions to the provider until next month or until you raise it; quick lookups still work. 1 to 100,000. Default 20.
  • Messages per person in 10 minutes: 1 to 500. Default 30.
  • Keep chats for (days): 0 keeps chats; otherwise 7 to 3,650 days, and older chats are removed each night.
  • Most tokens for one question: all the work for one question together (the Assistant, up to three leads and the merged answer). 5,000 to 1,000,000. Default 60,000. When it is reached the Assistant stops and says so.
  • Action cards: On (it can prepare changes for people to confirm) or Off (answers only, no cards). Default On.

Click Save limits. You will see "Limits saved." Costs are estimates from the prices under Models; the provider's bill is the real figure.

Who has access

By default administrators and managers may use the Assistant and confirm its actions. Nobody else has it until you give it. Only administrators open Assistant Settings by default; the Usage table is shown there to people who also hold Assistant Usage.

  1. Click Change on Staff & Access (or open Settings → Staff & Access).
  2. Open the person's permissions and tick or untick, under Assistant: Use the Assistant, Confirm Assistant actions, Assistant Usage. Assistant Settings is under Settings.

Use the Assistant and Confirm Assistant actions are offered on Staff & Access while the Assistant is switched on; people who already hold them keep them. The Assistant only ever sees and does what that person could already see and do on screen.

Reading the usage table

Estimated cost this month adds up every reply since the 1st. The table lists the last 30 days: Day, Person, Model, Questions (requests sent to the provider), Tokens in, Tokens out and Est. cost.

Rules the system enforces

  • It is off until both the switch on this page and the Assistant module are on; if anything cannot be checked, it stays off.
  • Keys are never shown, logged or sent to the browser. They cannot be saved until the server has its encryption setting.
  • At most 5 models, exactly one Primary, at most one Fast and one Fallback.
  • Every change the Assistant suggests waits for the person to confirm, and runs the same screen action with the same checks. It never handles stock, recipes, till orders, payments, refunds, invoices, credit notes, end of day, prices or deals.
  • Every change on this page is written to the activity log.
