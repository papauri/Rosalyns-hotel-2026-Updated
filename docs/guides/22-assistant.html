Rosalyns Beach Hotel All guides Admin reference

The Assistant

The Assistant is a chat helper for staff in the Rosalyns Beach Hotel admin. The first part shows staff how to use it; the rest covers setting it up on Assistant Settings: switching it on or off, adding a provider key, choosing models, limits, who has access and usage. It ships switched off: nothing changes for anyone until an administrator sets it up and switches it on.

The Assistant answers questions, looks things up with its team of department leads, gives each person a daily briefing, points you to the right screen and prepares changes as action cards that you confirm with one tap. The nightly clean-up of old chats arrives in a later update.

Who uses it
An administrator sets it up. Staff who are given access then use it.
Where to find it
The chat: the Assistant button at the bottom right of every admin screen. Setting it up: Admin menu → Settings → Assistant Settings (/admin/ai-settings.php)
Permission needed
Assistant Settings to open the page. Assistant Usage to see the Usage table. Using the chat needs Use the Assistant; confirming the changes it suggests needs Confirm Assistant actions.
Only shown when
The settings page is always reachable for people with the permission, so it can always be switched on or off.

Using the Assistant

For everyone who has Use the Assistant. The button only appears once an administrator has switched the Assistant on.

How to use the Assistant

  1. Click the Assistant button (speech bubbles) at the bottom right of any admin screen. The chat opens on the right; on a phone or tablet it fills the screen.
  2. Type your question in plain words in the box at the bottom, for example "Arrivals today" or "Is the Deluxe Room free next Friday for 2 adults?", and press Enter or Send. Shift + Enter starts a new line.
  3. Or tap one of the suggestion buttons above the box. They change with the screen you are on (Bookings, Housekeeping and so on).
  4. The answer appears in the chat. When it mentions a screen or a guide, a button under the answer opens it (guides open in a new tab).
  5. Click Stop while it is answering to cancel. Click the cross, or press Esc, to close the chat. It stays open while you move between admin screens and keeps your conversation.

Clear chats

  1. Open the chat and click the Clear chats eraser at the top.
  2. Choose Clear this chat to remove the open chat, or Clear all my chats to remove every chat on your list. Choose Cancel to keep everything.
  3. The chat is taken off your list and a fresh chat starts.

Nothing is deleted. Confirmed cards stay on the record, and cleared chats still count towards your message limit.

What it can answer

  • Today's arrivals and departures, who is still to arrive, who has no room yet, who is due out and what they still owe.
  • Who is in house, room status (ready, occupied, cleaning, out of order) and which rooms have an arrival today.
  • Finding a booking by name, email, phone or reference, and the details of one booking (dates, room, party, total, paid, balance).
  • Which room types are free for some dates, and a price for a stay worked out exactly as Create Booking would (rate plans, child charge, VAT and levy included).
  • A guest's past stays, the active rate plans, and (for administrators and managers) who is online.
  • For people with money permissions: who still owes money, and where money is being lost (see the daily briefing).
  • For the departments your account can see: occupancy, ADR and RevPAR, pace and pickup, till sales and slow tickets, stock alerts, conference and gym, reviews and enquiries, housekeeping and maintenance jobs (see the team of leads).
  • Follow up one order. Ask "Status of table 5", by tab name, by order or receipt number, by room number (room service) or by guest name. It shows each item's progress at the kitchen, bar or coffee bar (waiting, being made, ready, served), whether it is paid or voided, minutes waiting and the balance left. If several orders match, it lists them so you can pick one. If none match, it shows what is open now. It looks back 24 hours. It never changes an order: voids, payments and changes stay on the POS screen, and it links you there. Till-only staff see only orders they opened; managers and station staff see all.
  • Follow up one gym member (only where Gym is switched on). Ask by name, phone or member number. It shows the package, whether it is active, its end date, days left, last visits and booked classes. The balance owed shows only for staff with gym finance, reports, accounting or payments access.
  • Follow up one gym class. Ask "Who's on tomorrow's 6am class?" It shows who is booked and the spaces left, using the capacity in the gym slot calendar.
  • Follow up one record: one conference or event booking, one room's cleaning or repair jobs, one stock item, or one purchase order or delivery.
  • Common questions such as "Status of table 5" are answered instantly.
  • Wording follows the business type set in Settings → Modules. A gym, restaurant or shop never hears about rooms or check-in. Anything switched off is "not offered here".
  • How to do anything in the system, from the staff guides (see below).
  • The hotel's own facts: check-in and check-out times, opening hours of the restaurant kitchen, bar, coffee bar and gym, the menu with prices, facilities, policies, the hotel's phone and address, conference rooms and upcoming events. Times come from Hotel Settings and the Station Hours screen, and it says whether the kitchen, bar or coffee bar is open right now. A time that is not saved, it tells you so. Parts the hotel does not offer are left out.
  • When something is not available, what is: other room types free on the same dates, the nearest dates (3 days either side by default) with the room asked for, or a joined family room for a large party, each with its price; other dishes from the same part of the menu; when a closed bar or kitchen opens next and what is open now; the facilities the hotel does have. It never makes an option up: if nothing is free, it says so. When it cannot do something, it gives the screen, the best guide section and what it can help you with instead.

Figures come from the same places as the screens, so the chat and the screen always agree. Short lookups such as "Arrivals today", "Departures tomorrow", "Who is in house?", "Room status", "What time does the bar open?" and "What's check-out time?" are answered straight from the system.

Spelling mistakes and follow-up questions

  • Spelling mistakes are fine. Type a guest name, room type, menu item, stock item, supplier, gym member or class, event, staff member or tab as best you can. If one clear match exists, it carries on and shows the correct name, for example "Mr Nthani (Charles Nthani, RBH...)". If several could match, it asks one short question. If nothing matches, it says so and shows what exists. Typos in questions work too, for example "arivals tomorow".
  • A typo never makes it change anything. Changes still need a card and Confirm.
  • Follow-ups stay on the same booking. Once a booking is found, "they", "him/her", "Mr X" and "the room" mean that booking. Ask "How much was the room?" and it gives that booking's own total, paid and balance, the same as the booking screen.

Is it included?

For one booking, ask "Is breakfast included?" or "What's included?". It reads the booking's packages, rate plan and the room's listed extras. If none of them mention breakfast, it says so and gives the kitchen hours. It never guesses. To make it show, add the package or rate plan to the booking (see Problems and fixes).

Room prices and child charges

  • Ask "room rates", "price list", "child policy", or name one room type.
  • Each room shows only the prices it offers, its guest limit and the child rule.
  • Room prices include VAT and the tourism levy. Nothing is added on top.
  • For an exact total, give the dates and who is staying. If the party does not fit the room, it says why and offers rooms that do.

What it cannot do

  • It never changes anything on its own. It can prepare some changes as action cards, and nothing happens until you tap Confirm.
  • It never takes payments or refunds, and never changes stock, recipes, till orders, invoices, credit notes, shift or end-of-day closes, prices, rates, deals, settings or users. Ask it how, and it gives the steps and a button to the screen.
  • It only works on the hotel and this system. Anything else gets one friendly line: "That one's outside the hotel, so I'll leave it there - ask me anything about guests, rooms, your shift or the portal."
  • It never says the system reports to or submits anything to MRA or any tax authority: there is no such connection.

It knows who you are

The Assistant sees only what your own account can see on screen, and checks your permissions on every lookup. If you ask for something outside them, for example takings or revenue without a money permission, it answers in one line: "That's outside what your account can see — a manager or admin can help with that." It never gives the figure, a hint, a total or an average that would reveal it. Ask "What can I do here?" to see what your account can do.

Guides and how-to questions

Ask "How do I check a guest in?" and it answers from the staff guides, with a button to the guide. Ask "Summarise the housekeeping guide" (or one part of it, such as "Summarise the part of the housekeeping guide about assigning cleaning") and it summarises only what the guide says. It only uses guides for screens your account can open.

Privacy: what leaves the hotel

The model that writes the answers runs at the provider chosen in Assistant Settings. Before anything is sent, guest and staff names, emails, phone numbers and booking references are swapped for codes such as [GUEST_3]; card, passport and ID numbers and addresses are removed. The answer comes back with the codes and the real names are put back on the hotel's own server, so the provider never learns who anyone is. Your chats are kept on the hotel's server.

Costs

Quick lookups and their follow-ups, small talk, repeated questions within two minutes, off-topic and out-of-reach requests are answered on the hotel's server and cost nothing. The daily briefing is worked out on the server and costs one short reply. Everyday questions use the cheapest chosen model (Fast); only questions that need real thinking, such as "How can we lift occupancy next month?", use the Primary model. Each person has a high daily allowance that only guards against a runaway loop, and the hotel has a monthly aim that is shown, never enforced (see budgets and limits).

Action cards: changes with one Confirm

For people who also have Confirm Assistant actions. Ask for a change in plain words, for example "Check out room 12", "Book the Deluxe Room 3 to 5 November for 2 adults" or "Add a deep clean for room 7 tomorrow". The Assistant prepares a card; nothing is changed until you tap Confirm on it.

How to confirm a card

  1. Read the card. Each line shows what changes: the old value (crossed out) and the new value, and for money the total. Lines in red (and a red border) mean something serious, such as a cancellation or a room going off sale.
  2. Fill in anything the card asks for, for example the guest's name, email and phone on a new booking, or the reason for a cancellation. Confirm stays grey until they are filled in.
  3. Tap Confirm. The change is made by the same screen action a click would use, with the same checks, permissions, emails and booking timeline. The card then says "Done." with the screen's own message, or "Not done." with the reason.
  4. Tap Cancel if you do not want it. Nothing is changed.

When the card opens a screen instead

Some things are always finished on the screen, so the card has a button that opens it instead of Confirm: check-out when money is owed or the guest leaves on a different day (the settlement on Bookings), a booking with more than one room type or with a payment (Open in Create Booking, with the guest, dates and party filled in for you), joined family rooms (assigned as a pair on Bookings) and WhatsApp messages (sent from the booking screen).

Safety rules

  • Only you can confirm your cards, once. A card expires after 15 minutes; ask again for a fresh one.
  • When you tap Confirm the card is checked again. If the price or availability changed since it was made, it is not used: a new card with the new figures appears for you to check.
  • Text written by guests (special requests, reviews, enquiries) is treated only as information. It can never make the Assistant change anything.
  • Every confirmed card is written to the activity log as "... via Assistant", and the screen adds its usual booking timeline entries.
  • When you sign out, the names behind the privacy codes in your chats are removed from the server and unconfirmed cards expire; old chats then show (guest) in place of names.

An administrator can turn all cards off (answers only) on Assistant Settings → Budgets and limits → Action cards, or for one person by removing Confirm Assistant actions.

Password, lock and the card record

Cards that ask for your password

Some cards cannot be undone from the chat, so they ask for your own account password (the one you sign in with) before Confirm works: cancelling a booking, checking a guest out, correcting a check-out date, taking a room off sale (room status Maintenance or Out of order, or a maintenance job that blocks the room), and anything sent to a guest: Send guest email, Send invoice, Reply to enquiry, and Reply to review when Email it to the guest too is set to Yes. If you switch that choice to Yes on the card, the password box appears when you tap the button. WhatsApp the guest only opens the booking screen; the message is sent there as usual.

  1. Check the card.
  2. Type your password in Your password on the card.
  3. Tap the card's button (for example Cancel booking).

A wrong password says "Wrong password. 4 tries left...". After 5 wrong passwords confirming is locked for that account for 15 minutes and the people who manage Assistant Settings get a notice. Wait 15 minutes, or ask a manager if you have forgotten your password.

The Assistant locks after a quiet spell

When the portal has not been used for the time set in Lock the Assistant after (default 15 minutes), the chat is cleared from the screen and the panel shows Locked. Type your own password and tap Unlock; your chat comes back. Wrong passwords here count towards the same 5-try lock. Signing out always removes the names behind the privacy codes from the server.

The record of confirmed cards

Every card that is confirmed, finished or cancelled is added to a sealed record (each entry is locked to the one before it). Each night, and whenever someone opens Assistant faults, the record is checked against the cards. If a card was edited or deleted by hand afterwards, an Assistant fault titled "The Assistant card record does not match" appears with the card numbers. The check changes nothing; compare the card with the booking timeline and forward the fault to the developer.

Alerts and reports on request

The Assistant button shows a small number when something needs you now: a guest past check-out, a balance to collect before departure, an arrival with no room or a room not ready, a tentative hold about to lapse, an earlier arrival who never checked in. Tap the bell in the panel to see them; each has a button to the right screen and Dismiss (hidden for today). An alert clears itself once it is dealt with. Alerts come from your own briefing, so you only get what your account can see (for example, money alerts only with Accounting or Reports).

Reports on request: ask "Give me the occupancy report for last month" or "Revenue report 1 to 15 October". You get the headline figures (the same as Reports for the same dates) and a button that opens that Reports section; Export CSV is on that screen. Needs Reports or Accounting.

Draft replies, upgrades and what guests say

  • Draft replies: ask "Which reviews need a reply?" or "Draft a reply to the enquiry from [name]". The Assistant writes a draft on a card: Reply to review (posted under the review, and emailed to the guest if you choose) or Reply to enquiry (sent from the hotel email). Read and edit the text in the card, then tap Post reply or Send reply. Nothing is posted or sent until you do. Needs Reviews or Contact messages. Booking emails (confirmation, reminder, invoice) use the Send guest email card.
  • Upgrade chances: ask "Who could we upgrade this week?". You get arrivals in the next 14 days whose better room type is free for their dates, with the rack-rate difference. Ask for a room type change card to price it exactly; joined family rooms and multi-room bookings are left alone.
  • What guests say: ask "What are guests saying about us?". You get average ratings (overall, service, cleanliness, location, value), the spread of stars, how many reviews were replied to, and the themes most praised and most complained about. Counts only; no guest text leaves the hotel.

Searches that found no room and rate suggestions

When a guest searches the website and no room of a type is free for their dates, the search is noted (room type, dates and party size only - no name, email or address). Ask "Where are we turning guests away?" to see these lost searches by room type and week.

Ask "Should we change our rates next month?" for rate suggestions: for each room type, occupancy on the books, searches that found no room and the current rate, with a plain suggestion - hold, raise (busy and turning guests away) or add a deal (quiet). These are suggestions only. The Assistant never changes a price, rate plan or deal; a person makes any change on Rate Plans or Rooms. Needs Hotel Settings, Reports or Accounting.

The weekly digest email

Once a week (the first daily check of the week, usually Monday) only the people who manage Assistant Settings (the Assistant Settings permission) get a short email: what needs attention and last week's room figures, worked out from what their own account can see, with no guest names. To stop it for yourself: open the Assistant, tap Reply settings, set Weekly digest email to Off and tap Save. An administrator can turn it off for everyone on Assistant Settings → Budgets and limits → Weekly digest email.

The team of leads behind it

Behind the chat sits a small team of department leads, like the heads of department on a shift. For a simple question the Assistant answers itself. For a question that spans departments, or when you ask for the full picture, it asks up to three leads at the same time, each looks up its own figures, and one merged answer comes back. Each lead sees only what your account can see: a lead you have no permission for simply does not exist for you.

The daily briefing ("the tea")

The first time you open the Assistant each day it gives you a short briefing: what needs you today, most important first. Ask for it any time with "Give me the tea", "What did I miss?" or "Brief me". It is worked out from the hotel's data on the hotel's own server; only the short list of findings goes to the model, to be explained in two to four sentences.

Each point is set out like a line in a report: the headline figure, what it is, why it matters, which screen it comes from, and one button that opens that screen already filtered (for example today's check-outs, or reviews needing a reply).

It never guesses

Every amount, booking, reference, date, count or name in an answer comes from the hotel's data, looked up for that question. If nothing is found it says so plainly, for example "I can't find a booking with that reference - check it and try again?", and offers the next step. It never gives an estimate as if it were a fact.

The system checks every answer before you see it. A figure that is not in the data is sent back to be looked up; if it still cannot be confirmed, that sentence is removed and the answer ends with "I couldn't confirm part of that from the hotel's data, so I left it out."

Talking to it

Talk to it like a colleague. Follow-ups work: after "Arrivals today" ask "And tomorrow?" and it repeats the same lookup for tomorrow straight from the system, without asking the model again. A greeting, a thank-you or a word about a hard shift gets a short friendly reply, and it gives practical hospitality advice (handling a complaint, welcoming a guest). It stays on the hotel, its guests, your shift and this portal; anything else gets one friendly line steering back.

While it works, one plain line under your question names the step it is on, for example "Checking today's arrivals and departures..." or "Asking the Money, Front Desk lead...".

How it learns and checks itself

The Assistant gets better every day from what staff tell it and from its own mistakes. It keeps short notes on the hotel's own server; it never changes its own code, and a note can only change what it knows and how it words things - never what it may see or do, its limits, or the Confirm step on a card.

Helpful / Not right and corrections

  1. Under each answer, tap Helpful or Not right (Was this right?).
  2. After Not right, type what it should have said in the box (optional) and tap Send, for example "Breakfast starts at 6:30 in the garden restaurant."
  3. You can also correct it in the chat: start with "No, ..." or "Actually, ...".

A correction becomes a note on trial; it is used fully once later answers that used it were marked Helpful, and retired if they keep being marked Not right. Wishes about style ("shorter please", "reply in Chichewa") change only your own reply settings. Notes cannot hold contact details, prices or amounts (figures always come from the system), or anything about one particular guest.

To undo the style it learned from you, open Reply settings (the sliders button) and tap Reset my reply settings.

The Assistant memory tab

On Assistant Settings → Assistant memory an administrator sees every note: its kind (fact, reply style, lesson, playbook, word meaning), who it applies to, where it came from, how often it was used and how often it helped. Teach it something adds a note straight away. Each note can be edited, pinned (preferred and never retired automatically) or retired. Reset learning archives everything it learned (nothing is deleted); notes you typed yourself are kept. A playbook says which lookups answered a kind of question, so the cheaper model can answer it next time.

The Assistant faults tab

When something needs a code fix - a lookup that keeps failing, a card type that keeps failing, a booking total that differs from its card - the Assistant writes it up in plain words with the evidence on Assistant Settings → Assistant faults. Copy it into a PATCH request (Copy for a PATCH request) and mark it Forwarded. When the fix is in, mark it Fixed: a question is added to the nightly check so the problem cannot quietly come back. Recent problems below lists the last 50 things that went wrong or were corrected.

When it pauses itself

It pauses itself, and tells the people who manage Assistant Settings with a small notice on any admin page, when:

  • 5 or more cards failed or were cancelled within one hour;
  • its five key questions score below the Pause below setting in a daily check.

While paused nobody can use it and nothing is sent to a provider. To resume, switch it on again on Assistant Settings. A lookup that keeps failing is switched off for one hour on its own (the rest keeps working) and is tried again after the hour.

Daily checks - no scheduled task needed

After the first admin page load of each day by anyone signed in, the Assistant runs its checks in the background - nobody waits for them: it removes the real names kept for chats of people whose session has ended, checks for newer models once a day (a newer one is used only if it answers the question set at least as well and costs no more; Undo this model change puts the old one back), reviews the day's mistakes into short lessons, tests the provider and every lookup, and asks itself a fixed set of about 40 questions whose answers come from your own data. It also checks the sealed card record, sends the weekly digest when due, and removes old Assistant records when Keep chats for is above 0. The results are on Assistant Settings → Learning and daily checks; Run the checks now runs them at once. The scripts in scripts/ai-*.php can also run from a scheduled task, but do not have to.

Switches

  • Learning: Frozen keeps what it knows but keeps nothing new.
  • Cloud providers: Off sends nothing outside the hotel; quick local answers (arrivals, departures, in house, room status) still work.
  • If the portal cannot be opened, whoever runs the server can type php scripts/ai-kill.php off (and on to resume).

When it says the feature is complete

The status under Learning and daily checks counts clean days in a row. Each day it asks its 5 key questions, and once a week the whole question set (both on the Fast model, so the checks cost very little). A clean day: the day's questions pass at the Pass rate needed (and the latest weekly run passed too), nothing that passed before now fails, no open faults, no new kind of problem, every lookup passes its self-test and it did not pause itself. After the set number of clean days (14 by default) it shows "This feature is complete - there is nothing else to patch" with the date and the evidence, stops writing new lessons, and keeps checking every day. If a check later finds a problem it goes back to learning by itself, writes up the fault and tells you. An administrator can also Mark complete by hand or Reopen.

How to add a provider key

A provider is the company whose models answer the questions. You need an account and a key from at least one. The provider bills that account directly.

  1. Get a key from the provider's website (the hint under each box says where).
  2. Open Settings → Assistant Settings.
  3. On that provider's card paste the key into the box and click Save and test.
  4. The page saves the key, lists the provider's models and sends one short test message. A green message such as "Key saved for Anthropic. Connected. 7 chat models found; Claude Opus 5.5 answered the test in 640 ms." means it works.

The key is saved scrambled and is never shown again: the card shows only four dots and its last four characters. To replace it, paste a new one. Test connection checks the saved key again; it costs a fraction of a cent. Remove key deletes it after you confirm, and un-chooses that provider's models.

DeepSeek is hosted in China. Questions sent to it are processed there.

How to choose the models

  1. After a key passes the test, its models appear under Models, most capable first.
  2. A suggestion is ticked with roles filled in: the three cheapest models that still perform (never a big model such as Opus) as Primary, Fallback and Fast, plus the next best as Extra. Change the ticks if you like: up to 5.
  3. Give each ticked model a role: Primary answers questions and plans (exactly one); Fast does short replies and routing; Fallback takes over if Primary fails; Extra is kept ready.
  4. Check the prices. A model marked New - set price is not in the built-in list: type its prices from the provider's price page (US dollars per million tokens in and out).
  5. Click Save models. That provider becomes the one In use. Only one provider is used at a time.

Check for newer models reads the provider's list again. The system also does this once a day while the Assistant is on. Your chosen models and typed prices are never changed by it. Newest marks the most recently released model in the list.

Cheapest models that still perform: once a day the Assistant checks each provider for newer models. Of the models that perform close to the best one (within 15 points of its rank), it suggests the three cheapest. Big models (such as Opus, Fable, Gemini Pro or Argon) are never suggested for chat. Gemini 4 models are never listed, suggested, chosen or switched to (owner rule); use a Gemini 3 model such as Gemini 3.8 Flash. Prices come from each provider's own price page. A new model with no price is never used. It waits until someone types its price, and the people who manage Assistant Settings get a notice.

How to switch it on or off

  1. Make sure a key passed Test connection and you saved models with one Primary.
  2. In On or off click Switch on. The line then reads, for example, "On - using Anthropic (Claude Opus 5.5), 3 people have access."
  3. To stop it for everyone click Switch off.

The Assistant is also listed on Settings → Modules under Staff Tools. It needs both switches on; switching either off hides the button for everyone at once.

Switching off never deletes chats, keys, models or limits; switching on again carries on where it was. The Assistant can also pause itself, for example after 5 failed or cancelled cards in an hour (spending never pauses it); the line then starts "Paused automatically:" with the reason. Clicking Switch on again lifts the pause.

How to set budgets and limits

  • Daily limit per person (tokens): words read plus written per person per day, counted in tokens (a token is about three quarters of a word). Only a guard against a runaway loop: normal heavy use never reaches it. If it is reached the person is told plainly; quick lookups still work. 1,000 to 50,000,000. Default 2,000,000.
  • Monthly aim (US$): what the Assistant aims to cost a month, whatever the size of the hotel's data. Shown next to this month's cost as information only: chat never pauses and nobody is warned. 1 to 100,000. Default 5.
  • Messages per person in 10 minutes: 1 to 500. Default 30.
  • Keep chats for (days): 0 keeps chats; otherwise 7 to 3,650 days, and older chats are removed each night.
  • Most tokens for one question: all the work for one question together (the Assistant, up to three leads and the merged answer). 5,000 to 1,000,000. Default 60,000. When it is reached the Assistant stops and says so.
  • Action cards: On (it can prepare changes for people to confirm) or Off (answers only, no cards). Default On.
  • Lock the Assistant after (minutes without use): 0 never locks; otherwise 5 to 240. Default 15. See Password, lock and the card record.
  • Weekly digest email: On or Off for everyone. Default On. See The weekly digest email.

Keep chats for above 0 also removes old Assistant cards, alerts, feedback, problem notes and usage rows older than that (this month's usage always stays, so this month's cost stays right). It only ever touches the Assistant's own records - never bookings, money, stock or settings - and nothing at all while it is 0. What the Assistant has learned and the fault list are kept.

Click Save limits. You will see "Limits saved." Costs are estimates from the prices under Models; the provider's bill is the real figure.

Who has access

By default administrators and managers may use the Assistant and confirm its actions. Nobody else has it until you give it. Only administrators open Assistant Settings by default; Assistant Usage opens the usage dashboard (administrators and managers by default).

  1. Click Change on Staff & Access (or open Settings → Staff & Access).
  2. Open the person's permissions and tick or untick, under Assistant: Use the Assistant, Confirm Assistant actions, Assistant Usage. Assistant Settings is under Settings.

Use the Assistant and Confirm Assistant actions are offered on Staff & Access while the Assistant is switched on; people who already hold them keep them. The Assistant only ever sees and does what that person could already see and do on screen.

The usage dashboard and the monthly aim

Open Settings → Assistant Usage (needs the Assistant Usage permission), pick From and To and tap Show.

  • This month's estimated cost: every reply since the 1st, next to the monthly aim, with the percentage of it (information only).
  • Answers: replies in the chosen days, and how many needed a model.
  • Cost per answer: estimated cost divided by all answers.
  • Answered without a model: the share answered straight from the hotel's data (quick lookups, the briefing, repeated questions) at no cost. Higher is cheaper.
  • Automatic checks a month: what the Assistant's own checks are expected to cost in a month (the 5 key questions each day, the whole set once a week, lessons and model checks), worked out from the last 4 weeks.
  • Tables By person, By day and By model. Nightly checks is the Assistant testing itself.

Keeping it cheap and quick: the monthly aim is US$5 or less, whatever the size of the hotel's data: every lookup sends a fixed-size summary (counts, totals and the top 10 with "and N more"), never a list that grows with the bookings, guests or stock. Arrivals, departures, in house, room status, your access, the briefing and questions that are not about the hotel are answered straight from the hotel's data at no cost. Other questions go to the Fast model with only the lookups for that department; advice questions (such as lifting next month's occupancy) have their figures looked up first and get one Primary answer. Answers are never cut short to save tokens: an answer that uses more than 8,000 input tokens is only counted as heavy on the Answers card and noted in the system log. The Assistant looks things up at most 4 times for one question, and stops sooner when it would only repeat a lookup it has already made.

The monthly aim is not a limit: passing it never pauses the chat and sends no notice. Assistant Settings also keeps a short usage table for the last 30 days.

Rules the system enforces

  • It is off until both the switch on this page and the Assistant module are on; if anything cannot be checked, it stays off.
  • Keys are never shown, logged or sent to the browser. They cannot be saved until the server has its encryption setting.
  • At most 5 models, exactly one Primary, at most one Fast and one Fallback.
  • Every change the Assistant suggests waits for the person to confirm, and runs the same screen action with the same checks. It never handles stock, recipes, till orders, payments, refunds, invoices, credit notes, end of day, prices or deals.
  • Every change on this page is written to the activity log.
  • What it learns changes only its knowledge and wording - never its tools, permissions, limits or the Confirm step. It never changes its own code; problems are written up on Assistant faults for a person to forward.
